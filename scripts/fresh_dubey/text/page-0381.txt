LEDUM PALUSTRE

Marsh Tea HAHNEMANN Ericaceae

INTRODUCTION

Ledum is one of the frequently used
medicines for injury, skin affections, rheumatic
and gouty complaints. It is a remedy gifted by the
vegetable kingdom; particularly so from a wild
plant which normally has no utility. But
Hahnemann found its usefulness in a number of
ailments after potentisation.

CLINICAL
Ascites, Asthma, Bruises, Gout, Injury,
Rheumatism, Skin affection, Wounds, etc.

SPHERES OF ACTION

Its seat of action is mainly on skin, serous and mucous membranes, fibrous tissues,
circulatory system and joints.

The action of Ledum on skin retards capillary circulation, especially on the external
surface of the body and the extremities, resulting in coldness of the surface afed due to
its affections on the fibrous tissues and joints, joints are enlatged and become the seats
of nodosities and “gout-stones”.

CONSTITUTION

Since it is more or less an acute drug, consideration of constitution is not of much
importance. However, Ledum patient is very chilly, with psoric and syphilitic
background having rheumatic diathesis.

GUIDING SYMPTOMS

1. There is general lack of animal heat; yet heat of bed is intolerable. The patient is
unnaturally cold.

2. Complaints of people who are cold all the time; cold to touch but not cold subjectively
to patient.

3. In spite of coldness of the body, the symptoms are relieved by cold application (like
Secale cor., Rev. of Arsenic).

4. Relief from cold, is so prominent that sometimes the only amelioration is obtained
by putting the feet into ice-cold water,
