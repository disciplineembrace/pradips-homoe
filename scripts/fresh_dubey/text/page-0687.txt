3. Modalities

¢ General modalities - better from rest, nourishment and heat. Symptoms are
generally aggravated by noise, exertion — both mental and physical, excitement,
worry, arising from a sitting position, etc.

* Pains worse by cold air, continued exercise and after rest

¢ Symptoms are generally ameliorated by gentle motion, eating, excitement or
pleasant company; worse when alone.

CLINICAL USE

One may apply this remedy for the following conditions and indications:
* Mental exhaustion, neurological overwhelm, weak memory
¢ Mental deterioration, senility and Alzheimer’s disease
¢ Chronic fatigue from overuse of the intellect
¢ Insomnia — difficulty sleeping after midnight, nightmares
* Hayfever
¢ Shyness and depression

¢ Nerve tonic — “homeopathic valium”
