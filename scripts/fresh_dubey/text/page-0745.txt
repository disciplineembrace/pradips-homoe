742 Text Book of MATERIA MEDICA

Clergyman’s sore throat : Hoarseness in a.clergyman usually due to excessive lectures.
Clinker : Hard ball of mucus.

Clonic : Irregular paroxysmal contraction.

Clumsy: unregulated, irregular.

Coalescing : Joining together.

Coryza: Running down of thin mucus from nose.

Cold abscess: Non-inflammatory abscess.

Coma vigil: Stupor with wakefulness.

Concomitants: Symptoms appearing without any logical relation to the complaint.
Commotion: Violent agitation.

Contracture: Permanent muscular contraction.

Contradiction: Opposing conditions.

Concussive: Leading to pressure on brain due to violent shakings.
Confinement : Restraint indoors due to disease or due to childbirth.
Constipation: Difficult defecation.

Corpulent: Bulky state.

Corrosive : Excoriating.

Crib: High sided bed for baby.

Cri encephalique: Brain cry, Sudden shrill cry as in meningitis and encephalitis.
Croup: Hard dry cough usually occurring in paroxysms.

Crumbling: Breaking into small pieces.

Cutaneous: Relating to skin.

Cyanosis: Blueness of skin usually due to deoxygenated discoloration.

Cyst:
1. Abnormal sac containing gas, fluid of a semi-solid matter.
2. The bladder.

Dandruff: Flakes of dead.skin formed on the scalp or scaly material shed from the scalp.

Death rattle : Respiratory rattling of mucus in throat of a dying person due to loss of cough
reflex.

Debauch : To go astray morally, corrupt.
