Erigeron is chiefly known as a remedy
for haemorrhages and congestions in all
parts of the body. Haemorrhages are caused
and cured by this remedy. The haemorrhage
of this remedy, it is immaterial from what
orifice it may take place : is characterised by
the bright redness of the blood (Mill.,
Trill.). It can be used with great advantages
in cases of haematemesis due to ulceration or rupture of blood vessels in the stomach, in
epistaxis, haemorrhage from the bowels may be due to fissures, metrorrhagia and
menorrhagia from urinary bladder, chronic cystitis, polyp & malignancy etc.
Haemorrhage from the uterus, bloody lochia even leucor-rhoea are usually attended with
painful micturition or urinary irritation and in some cases with irritation of the rectum.

Dr. Moore used Erigenon in several cases of placenta praevia where the indications
were profuse flow of bright red blood, pallor, weakness, and an aggravation of the flow
from the slightest movement on the part of the patient.

Author has found this remedy very useful in checking the haemorrhage from
urinary bladder than any other remedy. It may be due to chronic cystitis, polyp or
malignant condition.

Female : This remedy is indicated in metrorrhagia menorrhagia, leucorrhoea and
prolapsus uteri etc. Usually when associated with violent irritation of bladder & rectum.
Bright red flow. Bloody lochia returns after least motion & comes in gushes.
Pregnant women with “Weak uterus”. Nosebleed instead of menses.
