ARSENICUM ALBUM

White Oxide of Arsenic HAHNEMANN AS,O,

INTRODUCTION

ARSENIC patient can be compared to a
RACE HORSE as PULSATILLA to a SHEEP
and ANTIM CRUD to a SWINE. Arsenic is a
deadly poison. The fatal dose is 2-4 grains,
Arsenic was used as a medicine from ancient
times. Hughes says that Arsenic was used in
China long ago and in India for cutaneous
affection, neuralgia, chorea and_ cholera.
Physiological dose will produce vomiting,
purging and great prostration due to excessive
secretion from gastro-intestinal tract. It
produces feeble pulse. In short, it gives the full picture of a cholera patient or you may
imagine a dying man with burning sensation, continuously sipping warm drinks and
wants to be carried from one place to another, with a cadaverous odour from his body.
Kent even says that from the very odour itself you may recognise an Arsenic patient as
soon as you enter his room.

SPHERES OF ACTION

Main seats of action of Arsenic are mind, nerves, mucous membrane, joints,
periosteum, liver, spleen, lymphatics, blood, lungs, skin, stomach and alimentray canal.
Arsenic acts more promptly on vegetarians than on non-vegetarians (rev.—Nux vy; All
sat).

Arsenic is a poison and causes constant and violent irritation to all tissues such as:—
1. Acting on gastro-intestinal tract, it produces a picture like Cholera Asiatica.

2. It attacks blood, causing mente changes i.e. exanthemata, ecchymosis, petechial
haemorrhage, etc.

3. It attacks veins, varices which burn like fire, especially at night.
