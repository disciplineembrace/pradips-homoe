INTRODUCTION
Apis is a very useful anti-psoric medicine
prepared from the poison of Honey Bee. Bee sting

gives burning, stinging pain, soreness, red rose
hue and puffiness of the part which is sensitive to
touch. Person becomes irritable, restless and
cries or even weeps till the pain subsides.

So, also when Apis is proved on healthy

human being, the same pathogenetic symptoms
are found.

SPHERES OF ACTION

Apis acts on cellular tissues, glands, skin, brain, urinary system, heart, pleurae and
serous membrane.
PATHOGENESIS

Apis is predominantly a right sided medicine. Acting on-cellular tissue, it causes
oedema of skin and mucous membrane, inflammation of kideny causing oedma and even
anasareca; on membranes of brain, producing hydrocephalus; on pleura, setting in
pleurisy with effusion and on serous membrane of the joint, manifesting synovitis; on
glands it causes enlargement and induration.
