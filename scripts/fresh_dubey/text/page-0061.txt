INTRODUCTION |
The Nitrite of Amyle was introduced into medicine by the old

school as a remedy for Angina pectoris, and was supposed to act by
lessening arterial tension.

CLINICAL:
Angina pectoris, Blushing. Convulsions. Epilepsy.
Climacteric-ailments of. Flushings. Headache.
Heart-affection of. Sea-sickness. Sunstroke.

SPHERES OF ACTION & PATHOGENESIS :

It affects the vaso-motor. nerves causing dilatation of all
arterioles and capillaries” thereby flushing of face, heat and
throbbing in the head are produced.

CONSTITUTION :

Make up—It is suited to patients subject to flushings and blushings, nervous.
sensitive, plethoric women during and after menopause.

Temperament—Nervous, anxious patient.
Relation with heat & cold—Hot patient.
Miasm—Psoric.

GUIDING SYMPTOMS :

1) Flushings : Start from face, stomach, various parts of the body followed by often
profuse hot sweatings and prostration.

2) Intense surging of blood to face and head (Bell., Glon.) I 3) Face flushes at the
slightest emotion (Coca., Per.)

4) Craves fresh air, patient opens clothing, removes bed covering and opens windows
in the coldest weather (Arg Nit., Lach., Sulph.)

5) Constant stretching for hours, impossible to satisfy the desire and call for help to
stretch.

6) Profound and repeated yawning (Kali C.)
7) Collar seems too tight, must loosen it (Lach., Sepia)
8) Hemicrania, especially when affected side is pallid.

9) Puerperal convulsions immediately after delivery.
