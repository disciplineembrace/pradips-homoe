During difficult dentition, a child is very restless, irritable, peevish and his face looks pale, body smells

sour.
There is constant & profuse sweat on scalp, forehead, face, mouth and nose. The hair is always wet
whether asleep or awake, quiet or in motion. Sweat may or may not be sour.

Rheum baby desires various kinds of food but cannot eat them ; becomes repugnant child, desires
many things & cries but dislikes even favourite playthings.

The colic of Rheum baby is worse at once by uncovering an arm or leg and when standing and not
better by stool.

The child may suffer from difficult micturition due to great weakness of the bladder. He requires great
deal of pressure to expel the accumulated urine from the inert bladder.

During dentition if stupor of the child is associated with distended abdomen, due to accumulation of
urine in the bladder ; Rheum is invaluable remedy.

Mentally Rheum baby is impatient & vehement, ugly & impossible to satisfy. He has sour temper,
screams, kicks & makes himself unpleasant. He is cross & tosses about all the night.
Relation :

Complimentary—Mag Garb.

Antidotes—Camphor, Chamomilla.
