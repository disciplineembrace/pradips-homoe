Glossary : 743

Decubitus :
1. The position of the patient in bed;
2. Bed sore.

Delirium: A confused and excited state of organic orgin marked by incoherence, illusions and
hallucination.

Delirium tremens: A form of acute insanity due to alcoholic poisoning marked by sweating,
restlessness, anxiety, precordial distress, mental congestion and hallucination.

Delusion: False belif or wrong judgment.

Desire: Liking for a thing.

Despondency: Loss of courage or hope; dejection.

Diathesis ; An unusual constitutional susceptibility or predisposition to a particular disease.
d. rheumatic: Predisposition to rheumatism.
d, haemorrhagic : Predisposition to haemorrhage.

d, tubercular : Predisposition to tuberculosis.
d. scrofulous : Predisposition to glandular swellings especially in the neck.

Doctrine of signature : Belief that there were specific therapeutic essences, a cure for every
disease, to be found in plants, minerals or chemicals.

Diplopia: Double vision

Diurnal: Occurring during day.

Distortion: The State of being pushed out of normal shape or position.
Dysmenorrhoea: Menstruation so painful as to disable the lady from normal work.
Dyspepsia: Indigestion or upset stomach.

Dyspnoea: Difficult respiration.

Dyscrasia: Morbid state resulting from presence of toxic matter in blood.

Ecchymosis: Purplish patch due to extravasation of blood into the skin; black and blue spot.
Emaciation: Extreme loss of flesh.

Emphysema: Permanent dilation of pulmonary air vesicle,

Entreating: Appealing earnestly.

Enuresis: Incontinence of urine.

Epilepsy: A chronic nervous disorder with attacks of unconsciousness or convulsion or both.
Epistaxis: Nose bleed.

Erythism: Abnormal state of excitement or irritation either general or local.
