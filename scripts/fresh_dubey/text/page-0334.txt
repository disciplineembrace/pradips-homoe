IGNATIA AMARA

“St. Ignatius’ Bean HAHNEMANN Loganiaceae

INTRODUCTION

Ignatia is famous as “a REMEDY OF GREAT
CONTRADICTIONS”. It is A WOMEN’S
REMEDY, and is the “feminine” of “masculine”,
Nux-vomica. The deep insight of Hahnemann
penetrated into the curative value of this very
useful drug. All the leading physicians, from time
to time, were undoubtedly unanimous about its
very contradictory nature as verified in the daily
practices but for every true practitioner this is an
indispensable remedy. The full implication of the
numerous contradictory symptoms of the drug on
one hand and those of the actual disease on the
other, for which this remedy is aptly used with
sure success, can hardly be understood by any person without exercising his prudence.
Still the utility of the remedy has been well recognised by one and all, in their own way
through daily practice.

CLINICAL

Abdominal disorders, Anxiety, Appetite, Chorea, Convulsion, Constipation, Croup,
Debility, Genital organs, Epilepsy, Flatulence, Haemorrhoids, Headache, Heart affection,
Hysteria, Intermittent fever, Locomotor ataxia, Menstrual disorders, Melancholia,
Numbness, Paralysis, Sleep, Throat and respiratory troubles, etc.

SPHERES OF ACTION

Ignatia’s domain of action is predominantly on entire nervous system, especially
medulla oblongata, spinal cord and consequently the whole nervous system.
PATHOGENESIS

It acts especially on medulla oblongata and spinal nervous system, producing
convulsion, spasm, chorea, hysteria and dyspnoea; intense acuteness of nerves resulting
in HYPERAESTHESIA, EMOTIONAL DISTURBANCES, INCOORDINATION AND

VARIOUS CONTRADICTORY SYMPTOMS.

CONSTITUTION
Make-up of the body: Dark looking lady, with dark hair and dark complexion.

Temperament: Nervous temperament, VERY SENSITIVE, easily excited, quick in
Perception and rapid in execution.
