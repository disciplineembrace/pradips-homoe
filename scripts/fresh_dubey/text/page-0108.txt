ASTERIAS RUBENS

Starfish PETROZ Radiata

INTRODUCTION

This medicine is prepared from star fish
belonging to the natural order of Radiata so it
shows its pathogenetic relationship to Sepia &
Murex which are prepared from cuttle fish and

purple fish respectively.
CLINICAL :

Acne. Apoplexy. Cancer, Constipation,
Convulsions. Epilepsy. Headache. Diarrhoea.
Uterus-affection of.

SPHERES OF ACTION & PATHOGENESIS:

It mainly acts on circulation, nerves, female sexual organs & rectum. This remedy
produces disturbances of the circulation with pulsations and congestion in head, womb &
chest., Acting on female sexual organs It produces great excitement.

CONSTITUTION :

Make up of the body : It is suited to flabby lymphatic constitution with red face.

Temperament : Iritable temperament.

Diathesis : Sycotic Diathesis.

Miasm : Sycotic & psoric miasms are in the background.

GUIDING SYMPTOMS :
1) Easily excited by any emotion, especially by contradictions (Ana. Con.)
2) Sanguinous congestion of the brain.
3) Heat of the head, as if surrounded by hot air.
4) Apoplexy, face red, pulse hard, full, frequent.
5) Gait unsteady, muscles refuse to obey the will (Alum., Gels.)
6) Epilepsy, twitching over the whole body four or five days before the attack.
7) Constipation : Obstinate, ineffectual desire with hard round ball like stool.

8) Diarrhoea : Watery, brown, gushing out in a violent jet (Grot. T.,) Orat, Gam. Jatr.,
Thuja)
