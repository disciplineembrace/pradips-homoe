Backache :

Location: Backache of supra-renal region extending downwards.

Pain: Intense, unbearable pain, shifts rapidly from side to side.

Spine aches from base to brain to coccyx. Very much“ensitive to touch and pressure.
Modalities: Aggravation from rest and on first motion.

General Modalities :

Aggravation: In the.moring on one day and in the evening of the next day.
Amelioration: Cold and cold drinks, rest and lying down.

Relations :
Similar to: Apis, Lachesis, Murex.
