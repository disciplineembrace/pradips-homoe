Aconitum Napellus 17

Thirst during fever ; Especially during the heat state, the patient is very much thirsty. There is
burning thirst for large quantity of cold water.

Mental symptoms during fever : The patient is very much anxious. There is intense
nervousness and restlessness, He tosses about in agony. The face is expressive of fear, fear of
death and even predicts the time of death.

Becomes intolerable towards the evening and on going to bed, The patient thinks that he will
definitely die soon and the fever is so severe that it will be fatal to him unless treated immediately.

Modalities:
* Aggravation : Towards the evening and at night and in a warm room.

* Amelioration ; In open air and by prespiration.

General Modalities :

Aggravation : Evening and at night, pains are unsuportable; in a warm room; when rising from
bed; lying on painful or affected side.

Amelioration : In open air.

Relations :

Aconite is complementary to Coffea in fever, sleeplessness and intolerance of pain; to Amica in
traumatism; to Sulphur in all cases, Rarely indicated in fevers which bring out eruptions.

Aconite is the acute of Sulphur and both precede and follow in acute inflammatory conditions.
In Aphonia, when Acon., Phos., Spongia fail, Cina should be thought of.

Antidotes : Bell., Cham., Coffea, Nux v., Sepia, Spongia, Sulph.

Antidoted by: Acet. acid., Alcohol.
