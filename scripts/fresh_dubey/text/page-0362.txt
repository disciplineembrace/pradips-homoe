KALI SULPHURICUM

"Potassium Sulphate KSO,

INTRODUCTION

One of the twelve tissue remedies introduced
by Dr. Schussler. This drug is yet to be proved
homeopathically. Symptoms available are from
Schussler’s work as well as clinical sources. This
drug is known as “Schussler’s Pulsatilla”.

CLINICAL

Asthma. Catarrh. Dandruff. Eczema.
Epithelioma. Itch. Polypus. Psoriasis.
Rheumatism, whooping cough. Ch. Eustachian
Catarrh.

SPHERES OF ACTION

Its chief action is on epidermis, epithelium,
muscles, nerves & blood corpuscles,
PATHOGENESIS

The Oxygen taken up by the iron content in the blood corpuscles is carried to every
cell of the organism by Kali Sulph, so the deficiency of Kali Sulph produces vertigo,
chilliness, palpitation, fear, melancholy, desquamation of epidermis & yellow mucus

discharge.

PERTICULARS

Head :

Rheumatic headache beginning in evening. Bald spots. Dandruff and scaldhead. Worse in warm room
and better in cool or open air.

Ears :

Eustachian deafness. Discharge of yellow matter. Pain under the ear, sharp cutting pain, tension.
Stitches and piercing below the mastoid process.

Nose:

Colds with yellow, slimy expectoration. Nose obstructed. Smell lost. Engorgement of the nasal
pharyngeal mucous membrane, mouth-breathing, snoring ete. remaining after adenoids.
