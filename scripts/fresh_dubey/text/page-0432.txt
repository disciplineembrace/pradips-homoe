Moschus 423

Respiratory symptoms :

Hysterical spasm of chest, difficult respiration, chest oppressed. Tightness of chest, is obliged
to take a deeper breath. Sudden constriction of larynx and trachea, Asthma with intense anxiety, fear and
smothering sensation. Globus hystericus. Cough ceases, mucus cannot be expectorated.

General Modalities :

Aggravation : Cold.
Amelioration ; Open air, rubbing.

Relations :
Campare : Nux Mos., Asaf., Valer., Sumbul, Ign, Castor.
Campatible: Ambra.
Antidotes : Camph ; Coff.
