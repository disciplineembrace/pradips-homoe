Labour :

During labour: There is irregular pain, which is too weak, feeble and ceasing occasionally.

Everything seems to be loose and open, but no expulsive action. There may be fainting
attacks during labour.

After pains: Too long, too painful, hour-glass contraction.

Breasts: Suppression of milk in thin, lean and exhausted woman. Breast is small and does not fill in
properly.
Abortion :

Secale cor, is a wonderful drug for treatment of cases especially of threatened abortion at third
month.

Causation: Lifting, straining and hard labour.

Threatened abortion, especially at third month. There may be prolonged, bearing down, forcing
pains with other generalities.

Gangrene: Dry, senile, following injury, with burning as if sparks of fire were falling on the
parts affected.

There is aggravation from external heat and relief by cold application in spite of coldness of the parts.

There may appear blisters at the commencement of gangrene.. Large ecchymosis and blood blisters. Skin
feels cold to touch, yet the patient cannot tolerate any covering. There is icy coldness of the extremities.

Skin of the face looks pale, pinched, ashy, with sunken eyes, blue rings around the eyes. Skin hangs in
folds as if very loose.
General Modalities :

Aggravation: FROM COVERING WORSE FROM HEAT, WARM APPLICATION, WARM DRINKS.
after eating and from touching.

Amelioration: In the open air, getting cold, uncovering, exposing the affected parts, rubbing, wants
to be fanned, cold application and open air.

Relations :
Antidoted by: Camphor, Opium.
Compatible: Arsenic, Aconite, Belladonna, Mercurius, Pulsatilla.
Compare: Medorrhinurn, Camphor.
