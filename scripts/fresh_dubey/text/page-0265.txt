CYCLAMEN EUROPAEUM

Sowbread PROVER : HAHNEMANN Primulaceae

INTRODUCTION

Cyclamen has a traditional reputation as a
remedy for affections of the uterus and
appendages. The later oprovings have
demonstrated the correctness of this. It is in many
ways analogous to Pulsatilla, from which it differs
mainly in having no better from open air ; and in
not having thirstlessness as so frequent an
accompaniment of other conditions.

CLINICAL

Acne. Anaemia. Chlorosis. Dyspepsia. Eyes
affections. Headache. Heel pain. Menstrual disorder. Mental. Pruritus. Rheumatism.

SPHERE OF ACTION & PATHOGENESIS

It acts upon cerebro-spinal system affecting sensorium, eyes, gastrointestinal canal
and more especially female sexual system.

CONSTITUTION

Physical make up—Best adapted to blonde leuco-phlegmatic who are pale and
anaemic. Temperament: Phlegmatic and irritable temperament. Heat cold relation :
Chilly patient. Miasm : Psoric and sycotic miasms are in the back ground.

GUIDING SYMPTOMS

1) Aversion to open air : The patient dreads open air (coryza and cough relief in open
air).

2) Deranged Menses in pale, chlorotic women : attended with headache, vertigo and
dim vision.

3) Weeping disposition : The patient is mentally sad, irrit-able, peevish, morose and
desires to be alone.

4) Thirstlessness : There is no thirst all day but it occurs in the evening during fever.

5) (a) Desire: For lemonade, (Bell., Nit. Acid., Pills,, Sabina., Sec-Cor.) Inedible things
(Calc. Os., Cina., Cicuta., Psorinum etc.)
(b) Aversion : To fat, meat, bread & butter. Ordinary food and beer. Pork disagrees.
(c) Appetite : Satiety after a few mouthfuls (Lye.) and then he loathes food which
causes nausea,
(d) Taste : Saliva and all food taste salty.
