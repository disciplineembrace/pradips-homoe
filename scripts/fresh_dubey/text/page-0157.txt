PARTICULAR

Head : Headache with nausea & with feeling as if eyes were sunken. Headache comes on from
becoming cold, but made better by cool air.

Vertigo, with deadly nausea, on moving head quickly.

Gastrointestinal Symptoms :
Mouth : Inside of lips sore. Dry hot mouth, Gum boils.

Tongue : Tongue flabby, resembling a layer of dried clay. Sour Soapy acrid taste. Yellow coating at
base.

Throat : Suppurating sorethroat. Last stage of ulcerated sore throat with discharge of yellow matter.
Suppurating stage of tonsillitis when abscess is discharging.

Stomach : There is burning pain in stomach. Nausea with vertigo. Desire for fruit, cold drinks, acids.
salt and sweet. Great thirst and appetite.

Abdomen : There is pain in the region of liver and in right side of Pelvis followed by weakness.
nausea and pain in stomach.

Stool : Purulent diarrhoea mixed with blood, Pus like slimy discharge from the bowels. Diarrhoea
after maple sugar and change of weather.

Respiratory Symptoms:

Cough : Cough associated with hectic fever and purulent and sanious expectoration, there is pain
across chest. Burning & weakness in chest.

Catarrh with thick lumpy, white yellow or pus like secretions is guiding symptom in respiratory
complaints.

Skin : There are numerous skin symptoms in Calc Sulph. Unhealthy skin, cuts, wounds, bruises ete:
do not heal quickly. Skin affections with yellowish scabs & purulent exudations. Burning and itching of

skin. Dryness of skin. Itching in bed and better by scratching. Ulcers bleed, burn & are scaly, crusty and
deep. Ulcers discharge bloody pus, offensive, thick yellow.

Many little matterless pimples under the hair bleeding when scratched.
