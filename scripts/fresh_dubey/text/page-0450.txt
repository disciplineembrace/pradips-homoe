INTRODUCTION

Natrum sulphuricum—Sodium sulphate was
discovered by Glauber; hence it is called
Glauber’s salt, it is used by the old school as a
Jaxative or purgative. But Homoeopathy has
raised its status and established its great utility
in various diseased conditions like pneumonia,
asthma, etc. Schuessler has laboriously
explained the role this salt plays in the economy
of life and he included it as a member of the
tissue remedies. It was studied by Grauvogl, who
found in it the hydrogenoid constitutions.

CLINICAL

Asthma, Abdominal disorders, Biliousness, Cough, Diarrhoea, Debility, Diabetes,
Epilepsy, Flatulence, Gonorrhoea. Headache, Injury, Liver affection, Meningitis,
Pneumonia, etc.

SPHERES OF ACTION °

It acts mainly on gastro-intestinal tract, respiratory system and skin. Meninges and.
mind are also affected with equal severity. Occasionally, head, conjunctiva and genital
organs are also affected.

PATHOGENESIS

On the alimentary system, it produces a state of inflammation causing jsentery and
diarrhoea. Vomiting may also result occasionally. The drug has got its main seat of
action on the respiratory system causing sycotic pneumonia and humid asthma. Its
action is undoubtedly directed upon the blood through which it produces irritating
disturbances in nearly every tissue of the body. Meningitis and brain symptoms, due to
effects of long-age injury are its characteristic effects,

CONSTITUTION

Physical make-up: HYDROGENOID CONSTITUTION, where the complaints are due
to living in damp houses, basement, cellers, etc.

Temperament: Bilious temperament.
Relation with heat and cold: Very chilly patient.
Miasm: Sycosis is in the background.
