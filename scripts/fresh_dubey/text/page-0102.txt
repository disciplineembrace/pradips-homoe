Cuprum Met : Epilepsy from a fall or blow upon the head ; from getting wet, from
bad effects of re-percussed eruptions. Aura begins in knees and ascends. Aggravation at

Lachesis : Epilepsy comes on during sleep ; from loss of vital fluids ; onanism,
jealousy.
Silicea : Nocturnal epilepsy especially about the time of the new moon.

Arg. Nit : For days or hours before the attack the pupils are dilated and after the
attack the patient is very restless and has trembling of hands.

Kali Brom : Congenital, Syphilitic, tubercular ; usually a day or two before menses ;
at new moon, headache follows attack.

PARTICULARS :

Epilepsy

Causations : Fright, grief, badnews, blow on the head, after masturbation, teething or with menstrual
discharges.

Before : Epilepsy without aura.

During : Attacks frequently repeated, then followed by a long interval of rest.

After - Attack accompanied or followed by profuse offensive sweat : by seminal ejaculations.

Eyes :
Pain and blurring of vision, better rubbing worse using eyes.
Coloured light produces dizziness.
