CALCARCA SULPHURIUM

Calcium Sulphate C. HERING CaSO,

INTRODUCTION

This is one of the twelve tissue remedies by
Schussler. In the last edition of his bio-chemic
therapy, Schussler discarded Cale Sulph.
because it is not an actual constituent of the
tissues & he distributed its functions between
Silicea & Natrum. Phos. Homoeopaths, having
no bio-chemic theory to support, continue its use
specially as it was proved by Dr. Bering &
Others.

CLINICAL

Abscess Carbuncles. Eczema. Fistula,
Haemorrhages. Tonsillitis, Ulcers.

SPHERES OF ACTION

Liver, mucous membranes, skin, connective tissue and glands.

This drug has got a special affinity for suppuration. Calc. Sulph. resembles Hepar
Sulph, but acts deeper and more intensely and is often useful after Hepar has ceased to
act, It is also useful when Kali Mur ceased to act. Cale Sulph. shares importance with
Sulph. Tuber & Psorinum. The tendency to formation of abscesses.in the body in any
place is a strong feature of this remedy, and is quiet similar to Pyrogen.

GUIDING SYMPTOMS
1) Tendency to formation of abscesses in the body in any place.
2) Mucous discharges are yellow, thick and lumpy. 3] Burning & itching of
soles of feet.
4) Desire fruit, green sour vegetables, tea.
5) Inflammations of the eyes with discharge of thick yellow matter.
6) Yellowish discharge from posterior nose.
7) Tongue is flabby with yellow coating at the base of the’ tongue.
8) Pimples and pustules on the face,
9) The presence of pus with a vent.
10) Unhealthy skin : Ulcers do not heal rapidly.
11) Painful abscesses about the anus in cases of fistula.
