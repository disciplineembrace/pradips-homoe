Stomach :
Causation: Sedentary habits.

Sensation: “All gone" sensation in the stomach.

Character; Gastric headache of persons who lead a sedentary life and who eat and drink in hurry
with choking sensation in the throat while swallowing. There is “all gone” sensation in the stomach when

it is empty.
Modalities: Aggravation in empty stomach and relieved by eating.

Rectum :

Paretic weakness or actual paralysis of the rectum, with great desire for stool but with the effort,
the desire passes away without evacuation. There is plug-like sensation in rectum.

Skin :
Look of the skin: Erysipelatous eruptions of malignant type. Yellow vesicles with intense burning
and itching. Warts on the palm of the hand (Natrum mur,). ‘
