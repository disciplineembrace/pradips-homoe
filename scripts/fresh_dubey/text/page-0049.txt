INTRODUCTION

As a result of a fast civilization, people are
much habituated to using metals and their
compounds for various purposes. One of them is
the use of metals in cooking utensils—
Aluminium being the major component.
Prolonged use of these utensils brings a state of
chronic aluminium poisoning. This state is now-
a-days often met with for which potentised
Aluminium is the remedy. Hering has remarked
“apparently as usual, crude poison is antidoted
by its potencies—200, etc.”

CLINICAL

Anus affection, Constipation of infants, Cough, Diarrhoea, Dysentery, Eczema,
Headache, Leucorrhoea, Locomotor Ataxia, Menstrual disorders, Nervous affection, Skin
affection etc.

SPHERES OF ACTION

Alumina directly affects the motor nervous system; most specific action is on mucous
membrane, specially of intestine and vagina. Skin remains the main seat of action.

PATHOGENESIS

When acting on the motor nervous system, it produces a state of paralysis—paralytic
condition. By its action on mucous membrane, like Bryonia, it produces a condition of]
extreme dryness, lack of secretion, with more or less irritation. Skin under the action 0

this drug also becomes dry.
CONSTITUTION

Physical makeup: Alumina patient is spare, dry, thin, having dark complexion,
persons of sedentary habits who suffer from chronic ailments; to constitutions which
suffer from diminished animal heat.

Temperament: Hypochondriac.

Relation with heat and cold: Very chilly patient.
Miasm: Psora is in the background.

Diathesis: Scrofulous diathesis.
