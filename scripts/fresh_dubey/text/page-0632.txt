Thyroidinum

Extremities

Rheumatic arthritis with tendency to obesity, coldness and cramps of extremities. Trembling of limbs
and entire body. Oedema of legs. Peeling of skin of lower extremities.

Skin

Psoriasis associated with adiposity. Browny swelling. Skin dry, impoverished. Eczeme. Swelling of
glands of stony hardness. Jaundice with itching. Ichthyosis, lupus Itch-ing without eruption, worse at
night.

N.B. : Thyroidinum is contraindicated in tubercular patients for it may most likely bring about further
reduction of weight.
