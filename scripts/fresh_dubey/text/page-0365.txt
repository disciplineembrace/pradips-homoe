Neuralgia :

It is a great remedy for prosopalgia esp. when right side is affected. Pain starts after exposure to
cold and extends over the head, ear, face and then goes down to the arm. Pain attended with or
succeeded by numbness in affected parts.

Modalities :
Aggravation : Right side, becoming cold, Sunrise to sunset. Amelioration : By eating.

Rheumatism :
(a) Causation : Chill, Exposure.
(b) Location : Specially right side.
(c) Sensation .-There is sensation of numbness, coldness, weakness in limbs.

Character of pain : Pains are sticking, darting, pressing, shooting in a downward direction. Pains
are attended or succeeded by numbness of affected part. There is severe pain in joints which changes
place suddenly and goes from one joint to another. Joints are hot, red, painful & swollen.

Modalities :
Aggravation ; From least movement, becoming cold. Amelioration : Continued motion.
Concomitant: Heart complaint alternates with rheumatism.

Heart: This remedy is suited to heart troubles that have developed from rheumatism or when heart
is involved as a sequel of rheumatism or gout. The heart's action is tumultous, rapid and feeble. There is

a very sharp pain in the heart which takes away the breath with extreme dyspnoea and anguish.
Pain shoots down into the abdomen and stomach. Palpitation worse leaning forward. Pulse slow.
scarcely perceptible (35 to 40 per minute) ; pale face and cold extremities.

Modalities :

Aggravation : Bending forward, lying on left side.
General Modalities :

Aggravation : Leaning forward, looking downward, motion, open air, with the sun, becoming cold.
Amelioration : Eating, continued motion.
Relation :

Similar to Led. Rhod., Spig., in rheumatic affection & gout.

Complementary: Benzoic Acid.
It follows : Spig. well in heart diseases.
