Glossary 747

L

Laceration : A wound produced by tearing of skins.

Lachrymation : Shedding of tears.
Lactation: (1) Secretion of milk.

(2) Period of secretion of milk.
Laparotomy : Surgical incision

(1) through abdominal wall.

(2) into the loin,

Lame: Stiff and very painful.

Lardaceous : Resembling lard.

Lassitude : A state or feeling of being tired.
Linen: The underpart of dress.

Lesion: Any hurt, wound or local degeneration.
Let’s up with a snap : Stops suddenly.

Leucoderma: An acquired condition with localised loss of pigmentation of the skin resulting
in abnormal whiteness.

Leucorrhoea: A whitish discharge from the vagina and uterine cavity.
Lienteria : Diarrhoea with passage of undigested food.

Linear spots : Discolouration of skin following dyspepsia.

Lock jaw: Inability to open the jaws as seen in tetanus.

Longing for : A strong desire for.

Locomotor ataxia : Total or partial inability, to co-ordinate voluntary bodily movements.
Loquacity : Excessive talkativeness.

Marrow: The soft, fatty substance filling up the medullary cavities and cancellous
extremities of the long bones.

Magnetized : To be rubbed.

Mania: (1) wild or violent mental disorder.
(2) An excessive craze for something.
a. Nymphomania : Extreme sexual desire in women.
b. Religios mania: Mania with abnormal religious impulses.
c. Peurperal maina: Mania which follows childbirth.
d. Satyriasis : Extreme sexual desire in men.
e. Lascivious mania : Mania characterised by expression of lust.
