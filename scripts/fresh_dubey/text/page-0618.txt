Tarentula Hispania — 609

Tarentula patient feigns all sorts of sickness. They not only imagine themselves sick
but they pretend to be sick, when they are not. When there are no observers there is no
hysteria but when attention is directed to her, she beging to twitch.

Violence is a strong feature of this remedy. Strikes her attendant, nurse, tears cloths.
In short Tarentula patient has foxy cunning and destructive mentality. Consolation
causes weeping. Relief in the evening after eating.

NERVOUS SYSTEM :

Extra irritability of Nervous system especially termini of nerves is the red strand of
Tarentula. We notice choreic affec-tion of the whole body or right arm and left leg.
Constant movement of legs, arms, trunk with inability to do anything.

Twitching and jerking of muscles. Hyperaesthesia aggravated by touch of affected
parts but relieved by rubbing the affected parts. Restlessness—must keep in motion
though it aggra-vates. Relieved by music but sometimes it excites violently.

GENERAL MODALITY :

Aggravation—motion, contact, touch of affected parts, noise, change of weather.
Amelioration : In open air, music, rubbing affected parts.
RELATION :

Antidote : Lachesis.

When Arsenic is well selected but fails, then think of Tarentula. In dry itching
eczema when Arsenic & Sulphur fail.

Similar to—Apis, Crotalus, Lach, Platina, Mygale, Naja, Theridion.
