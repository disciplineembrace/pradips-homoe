INTRODUCTION

It exists as bright yellow glistening crystal. It
has sour odour, but it has a very bitter taste. It is
one of the important medicines for diseases of spinal

origin.
CLINICAL

Anaemia, Boils. Brainfag. Burns. Debility.
Headache, Lumbago. Myelitis. Neurasthania. Paralysis.
Priapism. Self-abuse. Spinal diseases. Writers’ Cramp.

SPHERE OF ACTION AND PATHOGENESIS

It causes degeneration of spinal cord with
paralysis. It produces a fair picture of “Nervous
frustration”. Has also action on blood, genito-urinary system and skin.

CONSTITUTION

Make up suited to dark complexioned persons with dirty appearance about knuckles;
anaemic, cachetic persons, worn out persons, who are overtaxed mentally and physically.

Relation with heat & cold : The patient is better by cold air and cold water.
Miasms : Psoric, Sycotic & Syphilitic.

over work brings on headache and causes burning along the spine (Kali Phos.)

depressing emotions, occipito-cervical region.Might bandaging (Bell., Arg. Nit.)

6) Heaviness, numbness, formication, and weakness are the words which are the
keynotes of this drug.
