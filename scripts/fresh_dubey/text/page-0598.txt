STRAMONIUM

“Thorn Apple HAHNEMANN "Solanaceae

INTRODUCTION

Stramonium, the last of the great TRIO OF
DELIRIUM, is a wonderful remedy obtained
from the vegetable kingdom. Thorn apple is very
poisonous, affecting primarily the brain and
secondarily other organs. Hahnemann’s high
intelligence penetrated into this aspect of the
problem and transformed this very destructive
poisonous property into a useful unique remedy
applied successfully for the characteristic mental
derangement. It has been found to be very useful
for the human race for all time to come.

CLINICAL

Apoplexy, Chorea, Delirium tremens, Ecstasy, Epilepsy, Eye affection, Headache,
Hysteria, Mania, Meningitis, Stammering, Trismus, Vomiting, etc.

SPHERES OF ACTION

The main seats of action of Stramomium, are nervous system, brain, mind, skin and
mucous membrane.

The action of this agent is very similar to that of Belladonna, except that the
sympathetic nervous system is more affected by this drug than by Belladonna. It is a
powerful narcotic and antispasmodic. The heart’s action is rendered more irregular than
by Belladonna and the involuntary muscular fibres or the bronchial tubes are relaxed to
a great extent. It produces suppressed secretions and excretions of mucous membrane.

CONSTITUTION

Just like Belladonna, Stramonium too is more or less a drug required mostly in acute
cases and as such the consideration of constitution of the patient is of little importance.
However, it is more adapted to young plethoric persons and especially children in chorea,
mania and fever with delirium.

Temperament: Bilious temperament,
Relations with heat and cold: Easily affected by cold in general.
Miasm: Psora is in the background.
