General Modalities :
Aggravation: At night and from motion.
Amelioration: By rubbing, hard pressure and physical exertion.

Relations :
Bad effects of Plumbum are antidoted by Alumina, Platina, Zincum met. and Sulphuric acid.

Compare: Alumina, Platina and Opium in colic.
