Fever :

Although Stramonium is not a fever remedy, it can be thought of in remittent, intermittent and
continuous fever, provided the fever is acompanied and associated with furious delirium.

During fever, there is coldness of the whole body with redness of face and suppressed urine. In chill
stage, there is great sensitiveness to being uncovered. Wants to cover up completely. Chill is without
thirsts and is followed by heat. The patient sleeps during heat stage and there is violent thirst after waking
up. Heat with anxiety or with thirst and vomiting. There is also profuse sweat which does not afford any
relief.

Pain is very irregular, generally full, hard and quick, or small and rapid.

During all the stages of fever the patient has VIOLENT AND FURIOUS DELIRIUM which is very
characteristic and peculiar in this drug.

General Modalities :

Aggravation: In darkness, when alone, looking at bright or shining objects, after sleep, when
attempting to swallow and in forenoon.

Amelioration: From bright light, from company and from warmth.
