Relation with heat and cold: Hot baby, yet the child has great liability to catch cold with frequent
sneezing and watering of the nose.

Modality. The baby is very much cross, irritable even when kindly spoken to, cries from the slightest

cause. The child gets into a passion about trifles, especially when consoled, consolation aggravates all
troubles.

Desire and Aversion: The baby has intense craving for salt and child has great aversion to
bread.

Tongue :
The baby is having MAPPED TONGUE. Siow in learning to talk, with heavy and difficult speech.
Stool; Usually constipated.
Urine: There is involuntary urination while coughing, sneezing and laughing.
Modalities:
* Aggravation : From taking excessive salt and heat of sun.
¢ Amelioration: Going without reqular meals arid open air.
General Modalities :

Aggravation: From sunrise to sunset, especially from 10 to 11 a.m. At seashore, from sea air heat of
sun, mental exertion, lying down and by consolation.

Amelioration: In the open air, cold bathing, going without regular meals, lying on right side
(Sulphur).
Relations :

Complementary to: Apis.

Natrum mur. is the chronic of Ignatia, but in mental plane only.

Is followed well by: Sepia and Thuja.
