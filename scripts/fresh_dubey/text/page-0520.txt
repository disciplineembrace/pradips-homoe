Pyrogen is also a great remedy for Constipation, constipation with complete inertia. Obstinate
constipation from impaction or obstruction of faeces. Stool is large, BLACK AND VERY

Uterine symptoms :

Pyrogen is an excellent remedy in uterine troubles, especially in those cases who have never been
well since a septic fever and also to arouse the vital capacity and reaction of uterus and to enable it to
expel its contents. The remedy is often required when the foetus or secundines are retained, following
abortion or confinement; foetus decomposed or dead for day. The discharges from the uterus are BLACK
DECOMPOSED AND HORRIBLY OFFENSIVE.

Pyrogen is also of great help in case of trouble arising from irregular lochia, which is very thin, acrid
and brown and very much foetid or suppressed. There may be septic fever arising from and consequent
upon or associated with suppressed lochia, followed by chill and profuse foetid perspiration,

Pyrogen patient has distinct consciousness of the heart. The heart feels tired; as if enlarged,
throbbing and pulsating constantly in ears, preventing sleep. Cardiac weakness is caused by septic
condition.

Modality : Heart's troubles are aggravated by motion.
