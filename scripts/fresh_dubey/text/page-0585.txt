SPONGIA TOSTA

Roasted Sponge HAHNEMANN Coelentirata

INTRODUCTION

This remedy was introduced into our Materia
Medica by Samual Hahnemann. According
to Hahnemann toasted sponge was first
mentioned as a specific, remedy for goitre by
Arnald Von Villanova. It contains Iodine and
certain other ingredients of minor importance.

CLINICAL

Angina Pectoris. Asthma. Cough. Croup. Goitre-
Exophthalmic. Heart-affections. Laryngitis.
Rheumatic fever. Testicles-inflammation of.
Tuberculosis. Whooping cough.
SPHERES OF ACTION & PATHOGENESIS

Its chief action is on the respiratory tract, larynx & trachea. It also acts on heart &
glands, especially, thyroid & testes.
CONSTITUTION

Physical make up : It is especially adapted to diseases of children and women &
persons having light hair, lax fibre, fair complexion.

Heat & Cold relation : Chilly patient.
Miasm : Psoric, Syphilitie & Sycotic. All the three miasms are in the background.
Diathesis : Tubercular diathesis.

GUIDING SYMPTOMS
1) Swelling & induration of glands.
2) Awakens in a fright and feels as if suffocating; as if he had to breathe through a
sponge.

3) Great dryness of mucous membranes of air passages—throat, larynx,
trachea, bronchi — “dry as a horn.”

4) Goitre : Thyroid glands swollen even with chin; with suffqcative paroxysms at night.

5) Cough : Dry, barking, croupy; sibilant like a saw driven through a pine board
< from sweets, cold drinks, lying with head low > from eating & drinking warm

things’.
