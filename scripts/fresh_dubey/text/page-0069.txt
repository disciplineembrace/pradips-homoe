ANTIMONIUM CRUDUM

Sulphide of Antimony CASPARI Sb,S;.

INTRODUCTION

Here is yet another effective remedy from
the Mineral kingdom—Antimonium crudum.
The crude drug substance is a compound formed
by Antimony and Sulphur. When it is
potentised, it becomes a very good polychrest
remedy. Antim. crud. corresponds in a sense to
the race of SWINE, as ARESENIC does to
HORSE and PULSATILLA to SHEEP.

CLINICAL

Catarrh, Constipation, Corns, Diarrhoea, ’
Feet sore and horny, Fever, Lung affection, Piles, Stomach disorders, Skin affection,
Thirst, Tongue coated, Voice low, Warts, Whooping cough, etc.

SPHERES OF ACTION

The main action of Antim Crud. is on mucous membrane, especially, of stomach and
intestine; then corners skin and mind.

PATHOGENESIS

It causes disorganisation and vital depression of mucbus membrane of stomach and
intestine, interferes with digestion creating a catarrhal condition in the whole of
intestinal canal, resulting in alternate diarrhoea and constipation and above all peculiar
tongue; it produces on skin a disturbed and depressed state of vitality and its action on
brain results in various mental symptoms.

CONSTITUTION

Make-up of the body: Suited to children and young people, who are inclined to grow
fat—extremes of life, having tendency to thick unhealthy skin.

Old people with morning diarrhoea (Phosphorus).

Relation with heat and cold: Antim crud., patient is easily affected by extremes of
heat and cold and as such it is difficult to conclude whether he is hot or chilly.

Miasm: Psora and sycosis are in the background.
Diathesis: Rheumatic and Gouty diathesis.

GUIDING SYMPTOMS
1. A THICK MILKY WHITE COATING ON TONGUE is the red strand of the remedy:
2. Patients who are in extremes of life and who are inclined to grow fat.
