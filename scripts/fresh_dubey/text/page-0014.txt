INTRODUCTION

Abrotanum is a very useful medicine derived
from the vegetable kingdom. The mother tincture
is prepared from fresh leaves of the plant, which
abundantly grows in Southern Europe. This is a
remedy often required in cases of Marasmus in
children and for Rheumatism caused by checked
diarrhoea or other secretions. Abrotanum is one of
the drugs having prominent alternating actions.

CLINICAL

Chilblains, Gout, Haemorrhoids, Hectic Fever,
Hydrocele, Indigestion, Lientery, Marasmus, Pleurisy, Rheumatism, Umbilicus, Oozing

from, etc.
SPHERES OF ACTION

The chief centres of action of Abrotanum are gastro-intestinal tract, nerves, mind,
fibrous tissue and serous membrane.

PATHOGENESIS

Its action on gastro-intestinal system causes disturbed digestion, defective
assimilation, impaired nutrition, lientery distension of abdomen resulting in progressive
emaciation and marasmus. While its action on fibrous tissue produces rheumatic and
gouty manifestation, its affection on serous membrane sets in pleurisy, hydrocele etc.
