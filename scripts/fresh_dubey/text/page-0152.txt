CACTUS GRANDIFLORUS

Night-Blooming Cereus RUBINI Cactaceae

INTRODUCTION

This important medicine was introduced to
Homoeopathy by Dr. Rubini from Italy. The
mother tincture is prepared from the young,
tender branches and flowers. Dr. Hughes says
that it closely resembles Aconite in producing
general rigor, followed by much heat and sweat,
pain, haemorrhage and acute congestion in head
and chest. But the differentiating point is that
while Aconite weakens the nervous system,
Cactus never does so.

CLINICAL

Angina pectoris, Apoplexy, Asthma, Brain
affection, Fever, Haematuria, Heart affection,

Lung affection, Respiratory troubles,
Rheumatism, etc.
SPHERES OF ACTION

It acts on heart, blood vessels, uterus and
ovary in particular and the head and body in general.

PATHOGENESIS

It produces all types of overaction of the heart; from nervous palpitation to acute
—— hypertrophy due to valvular disease, angina pectoris and chronic palpitation,
CONSTITUTION

Physical make-up: Plethoric, full blooded persons.

Temperament: Sanguine temperament,

Heat and cold relation: Neither hot nor chilly.

Miasm: Psora is in the background.

GUIDING SYMPTOMS

1. Constriction, contraction and congestion of any organ in the body, especially heart,
uterus, throat, chest, bladder, rectum etc CONSTRICTING PAIN runs through the
whole PATHOGENESISis of Cactus.
