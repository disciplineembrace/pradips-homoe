COLOCYNTHIS

Bitter or HAHNEMANN Cucurbitaceae
Squirting Cucumber

INTRODUCTION

In traditional medicine, Colocynthis has
been used as a purgative. But in Hemoeopathy,
during proving, the range of action of this
medicine made Hahnemann place it high among
the remedies for COLIC. Nash says, “No remedy
produces more severe coHc than this one and no
remedy works more promptly.”

CLINICAL

Colic, Biliary colic, Diarrhoea, Dysentery,
Dysmenorrhoea, Headache, Menstrual,
Neuralgia, Rheumatism, Sciatica, Vertigo, etc.

SPHERES OF ACTION

It acts on visceral organs, colon rectum and nerves such as abdominal plexus,
trigeminal and sciatic nerves.

PATHOGENESIS

It acts upon the colon and rectum to cause colic and evacuation of blood and mucus.
It may, even, produce peritonitis. It, acts on the female genital organs’causing ,ovarian
neuralgia. Its action on nerves causes neuralgia, sciatica and lumbago.

GUIDING SYMPTOMS

1. The strongest characteristic calling for Colocynth is TERRIBLE COLIC WITH
AGONISING PAIN in abdomen compelling the patient to bend double. Relief is obtained
by MOTION and HARD PRESSURE and the motion is kept up steadily by the patient
while the pain lasts. Colic may occur by itself or with dysentery, cholera, etc.

2. Neuralgic affection and rheumatic troubles. Dr. Hughes quotes Dr. Bahr as saying
that Colocynth is useful in rheumatic colic and Plumbum in neuralgic colic.

3. Rheumatic pains affecting the joints are much aggravated by motion and better by
rest and pressure.

4. Worse from 4.9 p.m., is another characteristic of Colocynth.

5. Shooting pain like lightning shocks down the whole limb, left hip, left thigh, left
knee and the popliteal fossa. Crampy pain in the thigh as though screwed in a vice.
The patient lies upon the affected side to get relief.
