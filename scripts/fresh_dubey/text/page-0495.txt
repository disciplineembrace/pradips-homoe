PARTICULARS

Mind
Brainfag of literary or business people. Slightest excitment, mental exertion on overwork brings on
headache and burning along spine. Lack of will-power. Disinclined to work.

Dementia with prostration, Sits still & listless.

Extremities

Great weakness. Tired heavy feeling all over body, especially of limbs. Worse exertion. Weariness,
progressing from a slight feeling of fatigue on motion to complete paralysis. Acute ascending paralysis.
Burning along spine and great weakness of spine & back, softening of cord.

General Modalities
Amelioration <- From cold air and cold water.
Aggravation : Least mental exertion; motion; study; wet weather.
