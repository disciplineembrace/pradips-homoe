Constipation :
* Causation: After lead poisoning from interia of bowels.

* Character of stool: The stool is very much hard as if burnt. There is frequent urging to stool
and inability to strain a stool. Stool may be of soft, clay coloured, which adheres to the rectum and
anus (Alumina). Bad type of constipation; after Nux fails.

General Modalities :
Aggravation: Sitting, standing and in the evening.
Amelioration: By walking in the open air.
Relations :

Antidotes: Puls., also bad effects of lead.
