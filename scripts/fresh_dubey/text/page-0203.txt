Location: Small joints e.g. in fingers, toes, etc.

Character of pain: There is reheumatic affection of fhe neck and swelling of the finger joints.
There is severe drawing, tearing pain. Pain is of flying nature, migrates from one place to another. Severe
drawing pain in writst and fingers. Fingers are very stiff.

General Remedies :

Aggravation: In open air and by coffee.
Amelioration: By emission of flatus.

Similar to: Actea race., Belladonna, Lilium tig, Pulsatilla, Secale cor.
Labour pain similar to Pulsatilla, but mental conditions are opposite.

Similar to Sepia for “Mouth Patches” and reflex symptoms from uterine irregularities.
