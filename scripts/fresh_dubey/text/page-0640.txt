INTRODUCTION

This remedy was proved by Dr. Hurt on
himself and others. This is a fungus that grows
on the Indian corn. The provings of this remedy
brought out specific affin-ity for the generative
sphere of both sexes. The next important sphere
of this remedy is on the skin, hair and nails.
