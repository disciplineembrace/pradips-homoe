Remex Crispus 523

———

Aphonia after exposure to cold, from slightest inhalation of cold air from changing room or air
and relieved by warmth.

Aggravation in the evening (Aconite. Phos., Carbo veg.)

Stomach :

Cannot tolerate meat. It disagrees. It Causes eructation. Chronic gastritis; aching pain in the pit of
stomach shooting in the chest.

Aggravation from motion or talking.

Diarrhoea :
Time: Early in the morning from 5 TO 10 A.M..
Drives out of bed early in the morning.
Character of stool: Stool is proufse and gushes out. Painless, offensive, brown or black, thin and
watery.
Before stool: There is sudden urging, preceded by pain in abdomen.
Modality: Aggravation from motion and in the morning.
Concomitants: Catarrhal state alternates with diarrhoea.
Skin :
Intense itching of skin; itching of various parts, especially of lower extremities; urticaria.
* Aggravation from cold, WHEN UNDERSSING, UNCOVERING OR EXPOSURE TO COLD AR.
* Amelioration : By covering the parts.

General Modalities :
Aggravation: Cold air; lying down and in the evening.
Amelioration : Warmth, keeping mouth covered to exclude cold air.

Relations :
Antidoted by: Bell., Camph.
