Colocynthis 235

Dysentery :
Causation: Anger with indignation, vexation and taking cheese.

Character of stool: There is frequent excessive urging to stool, with sensation as if rectum and
anus were weakened. Urge for stool is renewed after taking least tood and drink. Stool is foamy, sour,
containing mucus and blood, with tenesmus.

Character of pain: There is gripping, crampy, agonising pain in the abdomen, compelling the
patient to bend forward for relief. Cramp in abdomen, especially at night, relieved by eructation. Feeling
of emptiness in the stomach, tearing pains which come from different parts of the chest and abdomen
and concentrate in pit of stomach. Pain in groin and in both sides of abdomen.

Modality: Pain is relieved by pressure, bending forward and eructation..

Concomitants: Painful swelling of haemorrhoids; paralysis of sphincter. Headache, nausea and.
vomiting with bitter disgusting taste in the mouth.
Sciatica :

For Left sided sciatica, Colocynth is a royal remedy. There is crampy pain in the hip, as though
screwed in a vice. The pain is of. shooting type, like lightning shoeks extends down the whole limb,
left hip, left thigh, left knee and into popliteal fossa. Patient wants to lie on affected side apparently to get
relief.

Modality: Relief by pressure and lying on affected side.

General Modalities : ;

Aggravation: Anger, indignation, mortification, touch, any attempt to eat and drink, by motion,
evening and night.

Amelioration: Bending double, hard pressure, violent exercise, warmth and discharge of flatus.
Relations :

Antidotes to: Causticum.

Antidoted by: Camphor, Coffee, Caust., Staph., Chamomilla.

Complementary to: Mere. Sol.

Compatible: Staphysagria, Chamomilla.

In Colic: Compare with Chamomilla and Mag. Phos.
