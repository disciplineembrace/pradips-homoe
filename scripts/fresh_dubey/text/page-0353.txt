SPHERES OF ACTION

Kali carb. acts specifically on the respiratory mucus membrane, digestive tract, and
upon the ovaro-uterine system, heart, liver, kidney, etc.

PATHOGENESIS

It produces a condition of excessive irritation giving rise to a state bordering on
inflammation, yet hardly reaching that point, The serous and mucous membrane become
extremely dry, as a result of which there is SHARP STITCHING PAIN, enfeebles the
heart, lowers the temperature, causes anaemia, promotes tissue waste, increases the
amount of urine and its solid constituents especially uric acid and urea.

CONSTITUTION

Physical make-up: The patient is dark skinned and haired, but of lax fibres with
inclination to obesity. Especially helpful in diseases of old people who are fleshy with
dropsical condition. Puffiness of upper eyelids and paretic tendencies.

Relation with heat and cold: The patient is always chilly.
