SPHERES OF ACTION

The chief action of Stannum met. is centred upon the nervous system, respiratory
organs and female genital organs.

It produces catarrhal inflammation of mucous membrane, profuse muco-purulent
expectoration with cough, bronchitis, etc. On the cerebro-spinal system it leads to
profound prostration, paralysis, convulsion and neuralgic pains.
