THLASPI BURSA PASTORIS

Shepherd’s Purse

INTRODUCTION

It is one of the important anti
haemorrhagic remedies of our Materia
Medica and is frequently used by us to
control uterine haemorrhage. In this
respect it stands near the Sabina.

CLINICAL

Abortion : haemorrhage after. Dysuria,
Haematuria, Haemorrhages, Leucorrhoea,
Metror-rhagia, Uterus-cancer of. Renal

Calculi. Fibroma.
GUIDING SYMPTOMS

1) Profuse, passive haemorrhage from every outlet of the body where the character of

blood is dark & clotted.

2) Metrorrhagia—wth violent cramps and uterine colic ; after abortion ; miscarriage or

labour, with cancer uteri.

3) Menses : Too early & too profuse. Protracted, tardy instarting; first day merely a
show ; second day colic, vomiting, haemorrhage with large clots ; each alternate
period more profuse. Exhausting haemorrhage from uterine inertia, scarcely

recovers from one period before another begins.
4) Haemorrhage accompanying uterine fibroid.

5) Leucorrhoea before and after menses (Graph.) ; bloody, dark offensive ; stains

difficult to Wash off.

6) Urinary symptoms: It is one of the anti Uric acid remedies and useful in renal
colic, chornic cystitis, dysuria, haematuria and albuminuria during gestation, etc-
There is frequent desire for urine with heavy urine containing brickdust

sediment and phosphates,
RELATIONS

Compare : Sinapis, Trillium, Viburnum, Ustilago.
