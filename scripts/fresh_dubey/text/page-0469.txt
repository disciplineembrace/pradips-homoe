2) Onosmdodium is very rich in eye symptoms. The eye symptoms may be due to
hyperaemic condition of optic disc, enlargement of retinal vessels, muscular
asthenopia and paretic condition of internal eye muscles. There is pain in eyeballs
between orbit and ball, extending to left temple. Eyes feel strained, heavy and

dull. Vision is impaired or blurred.
Modality: Aggravation—From using the eyes.

3) Sexual weakness in both sexes males and females. The sexual desire is
completely destroyed. In Onosmodium impotency is of more psychical
character.

4) Breast : There is aching in breast and itching of nipples. Yingling prescribed
Onosmodium in a woman whose breast was only partially developed with dryness
of the mouth, throat and nose and the usual size of breast was restored.

5) Male : In Onosmodium impotency is more of psychical character. There is loss of
desire, deficient erections & early emissions.

6) Female;In female sexual desire is completely destroyed. With this there
may be history of too early, too prolonged menses & severe bearing down pains in
uterine region.
