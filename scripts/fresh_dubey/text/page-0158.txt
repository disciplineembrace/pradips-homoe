CALCAREA ARSENICA

Arsenite of lime HERING Ca,(ASO),

INTRODUCTION

Calcarea ars. is prepared by mixing Calcarea and
Arsenic, So in Calcarea ars. we get few symptoms of
Calcarea carb. and few symptoms of Arsenicum album,
e.g. the person is chilly, fatty, ailments occur during
climacteric period, mental depression, weak heart
Palpitation on ascending, etc., are the symptoms of
Calcarea; whereas mental anxiety, chill, great
weakness, etc. are the symptoms of Arsenic. Dr. Hering
proved this drug on his own body.

CLINICAL

Asthma, Cholera, Constipation, Fever, Heart
affection, Headache, Kidney and Liver affections,
Nephritis, etc.

SPHERES OF ACTION
It acts on mind, heart, liver, spleen and kidney.

PATHOGENESIS
Calearea arsenica, acting on heart, causes palpitation; on kidney it produces
inflammation, resulting in acute and chronic nephritis.

CONSTITUTION

Make-up of the body: The patient is fair, fatty and flabby and looks like a calcarea
patient.

Relation with heat and cold: Very chilly patient.

Miasm: Psora is in the background.

GUIDING SYMPTOMS
1. Slightest emotion causes palpitation of heart (Lith.c.)
2. Rush of blood to the head and chest (Glonoine).
3. Kidney region is very sensitive to pressure.
4. Enlarged spleen and liver of the children.
5. Epilepsy from valvular diseases of the heart.

6. Mind : Anger, anxiety, great mental depression, desires company, confusion,
delusion and illusion.
