VIBURNUM OPULUS

~ Cramp Bark H.C. ALLEN Caprifoliaceae

——

INTRODUCTION

This plant is a native of Great Britain. It
bears red berries which are sour and are utilised
in making jelly. One learns most about this
medicine from Male’s Materia Medica of the New
Remedies. It is a great remedy for women’s
complaints.

CLINICAL

After-pains, Cramps. Dysmenorrhoea-
spasmodic, membranous, neuralgic, Labour
pains,— false, Miscarriage.

SPHERES OF ACTION

Viburnum acts especially upon the female
genital organs uterus, ovaries and other genital
organs. Left side is more affected than the right.

CONSTITUTION
Suited to tall, slender, dark or fair haired hys-terical subjects.

1) This remedy is valuable in spasmodic and membranous dysmemorrhoea with

following indications

Menses Time too late

Duration Lasting a few hours.

Quantity Scanty

Character of blood Thin light coloured and offensive in odour.

Before menses Severe bearing down pains, pain in anterior muscles

of thigh, Heavy aching feeling in sacral and pubic regions.

Character of blood : Blood clotted, bright red. There is also oozing of dark blood,
highly coagulated, forming occasion-ally long, black, stringy clots. Flow profuse and did
not cease entirely until next period.

Vicarious menstruation from lungs and bowels. Menor-rhagia at climaxis; active &
Constant flowing with frequent clots.
