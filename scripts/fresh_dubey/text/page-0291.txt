FERRUM METALLICUM

Tron HAHNEMANN Fe, An Element

INTRODUCTION

The metallic iron suggests anaemia to many
common people as well as to medical men. Iron
plays a vital role in the manifestation of
anaemia; hence the word “FERRUM” is
invariably associated with “ANAEMIA” and vice
versa by orthodox school of medicine. WHAT
ZINCUM IS TO NERVES, THE SAME, IRON IS
TO BLOOD. Ferrum was proved by Hahnemann
who said, “most of the provings of iron were
observed from the employment of the solution of
the actuals of iron, yet the symptoms correspond
to those of the metallic iron.”

SPHERES OF ACTION

Iron has chief action on the blood element, HAEMOGLOBIN OF R.B.C., skin and
muscular tissues of the body are acted upon later after the blood has been primarily
affected. Gastro intestinal tract and uterus are the other seats of action.

Iron has an important place as a constituent of haemoglobin. Initially iron increases
the R.B.C. count causing FALSE PLETHORA but later on it produces anaemia. A
disturbance of the equilibrium causes relaxation. The vessels walls dilate resulting in
congestion, increased blood pressure and haemorrhage. In alimentary canal, peristalsis
is weakened, producing vomiting, constipation, diarrhoea, etc.
