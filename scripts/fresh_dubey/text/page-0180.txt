CANNABIS SATIVA

INTRODUCTION

This European and American Hemp is said to be
botanically identical with Cannabis Indica, Both of
them belong to the same natural order. The difference
in soil, and climate is responsible for the difference in
their properties. Cannabis saliva was first introduced
accidentally in the field of Homoeopathic therapeutics
by Dr. O. Shaughsessy who also described the effects
of the Indian hemp, Cannabis Indica.

CLINICAL

Asthma, Constipation, Gonorrhoea, Respiratory

SPHERES OF ACTION

It acts chiefly upon the urinary organs, especially the mucous lining of the urethra,
sexual and respiratory organs.

It causes excessive irritation followed by inflammation of the mucous membrane of
urethral passage producing mucus-like discharge, the whole condition closely simulating

Gonorrhoea.

CONSTITUTION

There is no particular constitution of the patient as such. However, there is sycotic
and syphilitic background for the troubles.

GUIDING SYMPTOMS
1. Acute inflammatory first stage of gonorrhoea.

2. Urethra is very sensitive to touch and pressure. Cannot walk with legs close
together. They hurt the urethra.

3. There is tearing pain along the urethra in zigzag direction.

4. Pain extends from external orifice of the urethra backwards, burning and bitting.
Pain while urinating.

5. Obstinate constipation causing retention of urine, with constriction of anus.

6. Dyspnoea or asthma where the patient can breathe better only by standing up.

7. Choking in swallowing; things “go down the wrong way.”
