Antimonium Crudum 63

There is great AVERSION TO COLD BATHING. Child cries when washed or bathed with cold
water; cold water causes violent headache.
General Modalities :

Aggravation : After eating; cold bath; acids and sour wine; after heat of sun or fire, extremes of
cold or heat.

Amelioration: In the open air, during rest; after a warm bath.

Relations :
Complementary to Squilla.
Similar to Bry., Ipec., Lyco., Puls., in gastric complaints.
Antidoted by: Calc. carb., Hepar sulph.
Followed by: Pulsatilla, Sulphur.
Bryonia compares very closely in degestive conditions, coated tongue and aggravation from warmth.
