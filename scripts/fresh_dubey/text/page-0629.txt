Intermittent fever :

Attacks of chill come at different times of the day, mostly towards the evening, AT 3 P.M. AND 3
A.M. Chill usually on left side of the body. Chill begins in thighs. Feels cold to touch. Wants to cover up
the body. Chill without thirst, after midnight and in early morning. Internal chill with external heat and
great thirst. Heat in the evening, especially on face. Dry heat on covered parts. Muscles feel as if beaten.

Sweat stage follows heat. Sweat on first going to sleep. Sweat on uncovered parts only with dry heat
on covered parts. Sweat immediately after the chill without thirst. Sweat is often greasy, sometimes
offensive or smelling, sweetish like honey, oh genitals. Sweat appears especially during sleep.

Any complaint, such as rheumatism, prostatitis, impotency or growths and constitutional
troubles linked with suppressed or maltreated gonorrhoea make one think of Thuja.
General Modalities :

Aggravation: Tiere is general aggravation at night, at 3 p.m. and 3 a.m. from cold, damp air, cold
water, after taking breakfast, fat, coffee, tea, vaccination, by heat of bed, spirituous liquors, tobacco,
touch, bright light and blowing the nose.

Amelioration: In open air, warmth, by movement, pressure, rubbing and scratching.

Relations :
Complementary to: Medorrhinum, Sabina, Silicea.
Compare: Cannabis sativa, Staph.
Follows well: After Medorrhinum, Mercurius sol., Nit. ac.
Thuja should be given at bed time for better results like Nux vom.
