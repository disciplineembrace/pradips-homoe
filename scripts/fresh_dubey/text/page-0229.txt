GLEMATIS ERECTA

Virgin's Bower Ranunculaceae

INTRODUCTION

Clematis, like other Ranunculi, is a direct
irritant of the skin producing inflammation and
vesication. It was used by Stoerck before
Hahnemann’s time in cancerous and other foul
ulcers, inveterate skin eruptions, syphilis and
rheumatism.

CLINICAL

Eye affection, Gonorrhoea, Headache.
Glandular affection. Rheumatism. Stricture of
urethra. Skin affection. Toothache.

SPHERE OF ACTION

It acts on skin, glands, genito-urinary

organs, especially testicles, and mucous
membrane.

PATHOGENESIS

It is a direct irritant of the skin producing inflammation and vesication. It inflames
and infiltrates tis-sues and so causes swelling and induration of glands.
CONSTITUTION

It acts best on light-haired people; torpid, cachectic conditions.

All the three miasms—Psoric, sycotic and syphylitic are in the back ground. Chiefly
Sycotic.
GUIDING SYMPTOMS

1) Painful swelling and induration of the glands.

2) Gonorrhoeal orchitis, the testicle is painful indurated and hard as a stone with pain
along the spermatic cord, especially right side.

3) Toothache relieved by holding cold water in the mouth.

4) Interrupted flow of urine with burning sensation during micturition.
5) Aggravation of all skin symptoms by the heat of bed and-, from washing.
6) Nearly all symptoms aggravate at night.

7) Aggravation during increasing moon, amelioration dur-ing waning moon.
8) Ailments from : Suppressed gonorrhoea.
