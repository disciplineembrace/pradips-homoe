CEDRON

Rattlesnake Bean Simanubaceae

CLINICAL

Ciliary neuralgia. Headache. Hysteria.
Intermittent fever. Neuralgia.  Neuritis.
Toothache.

GUIDING SYMPTOMS

1) The great characteristic of Cedron is a clock
like periodicity in the recurrence of the
symptoms.

2) Neuralgia—Especially right side of face,
head is affected. There is severe pain in
eyeball with radiating pains around
eye shooting into nose. Periodic supra orbital neuralgia with scalding
lachrymation.

3) Intermittent fever : Cedron is valuable in intermittent fever especially when
there is a clockwise recurrency in successive attacks of fever and chill.

Chill : Before chill, there is a great mental excitement and exaltation of vital
energy.

During Chill : General coldness renewed by every movement as in Nux and
Cinchona.

Heat stage : Red face in heat stage. Thirst for warm drink is marked.

Sweat stage : The dry heat state is soon followed by a state of profuse perspiration.

4) Complaints after coition such as epilepsy. Chorea.

Relations :

In heart disease complicated with haemorrhoids consult collinsonia when Cac. Dig.
and other remedies fail. Has cured colic after col. & Nux had failed.

Compare :
Aesc. Aloe, Nux. Sulph.
