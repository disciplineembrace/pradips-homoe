36 Text Book of MATERIA MEDiqy
—

General Modalities :
Aggravation: Predominently in the evening and in warm room (puls—in open air, Euphrasia).
Amelioration: In cold room and open air (puls).

Relations :

Complementary to Phosphorus, Puls., Thuja. Compatible before Calcarea and Silicea in polypus,
Similar to Euphrasia, but coryza and lachrymation are opposite.

Bad effects from getting wet (Rhus tox.).
Antidoted by: Am., Cham., Nx v., Thuja.
