INTRODUCTION

Red coral, the product of coralligene Zoophyte,
contains carbonate of lime, Oxide of iron, gelatin
and other elements. In old physic it was
considered to have “strengthening properties” and
to be astringent, sudorific, diuretic and absorbent.

Catarrh. Eruptions. Post nasal catarrh.
Cough-whooping, hysterical & other types of eough.

1) “Minute gun cough” during the day and whooping cough at night.

2) Inspired air feels cold (Ars., Aesc., Brom.)

3) “Whooping cough or any other kind of cough when the attack comes on with a very
rapid cough and the attack follows so closely as to almost run into each other”—
Guernsey.

4) Violent paroxysm of cough with bleeding from mouth & nose.

5) Profuse secretion of mucus through the posterior nares obliging one to hawk
frequently. (Nat. Garb.)

6) “Continuous hysterical cough”—Author has found it useful after Ignatia has failed.

7) Feels suffocated and greatly exhausted after whooping cough.

8) Skin : Red Flat ulcers. Coral-colored, then dark red spots, changing to copper
colored spots. Psoriasis of palms & soles.

PARTICULARS

Respiratory Symptoms :
Nose : Post nasal catarrh. Profuse secretion of mucus dropping through posterior nares. Dry coryza;
nose stopped up and ulcerated. Painful ulcers in nostrils. Odors of smoke, onions etc. °

Throat: Throat very sensitive, especially to air. Hawking of profuse mucus.

Chest: Dry spasmodic suffocative cough ; very rapid cough, short, barking. Cough with great
sensitiveness of air passages. Feel cold on deep inspiration. Continuous hysterical cough. Patient feels
suffocated and greatly exhausted after whooping cough. “Minute gun cough” during the day and
whooping cough at night. Violent paroxysm of cough with bleeding from mouth & nose.
