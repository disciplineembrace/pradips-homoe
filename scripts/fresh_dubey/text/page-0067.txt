Arsenic Alb. : In Arsenic Alb. we get the same atrocious burning pain ichorous and offensive
discharge, marked prostration and ailments from dissecting wounds and anthrax,—like Anthracinum. But

unlike it Arsenic pains are relieved by Hot application, associated with great restlessness, midday,
midnight aggravation and thirst for little quantity of water at short intervals.

Tarentula Cubensis : Tarentula Cub. is also useful in felons, carbuncles, boils, abscesses, malignant
ulcers, gangrene and anthrax.

:
Tarentula has also got atrocious burning pain, bluish discolouration of affected part; but pain

associated with hyperaesthesia is its peculiarity.

There is pain of death; it soothes the last struggles. There may be aggravation by contact or touch
Of affected parts but relieved by rubbing affected parts.

Secale Cor : It is also a medicine for gangrene, ulcers, boils etc. Secale has also got burning as of

sparks of fire. Haemorrhages are offensive, putrid, watery, thin and black. Its Characteristic is relief of
burning pain by cold application (rev. of Ars.)

Relation:
When Arsenic or best selected remedy fails to relieve the burning pains of Carbuncle or malignant

ulceration, then think of Anthracinum. When Arsenic, Anthracinum fail to relieve terrible pains of cancer,
carbuncle or erysipelas then think of Euphorbinum.
