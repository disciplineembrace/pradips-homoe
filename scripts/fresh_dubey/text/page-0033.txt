INTRODUCTION

Hale writes of Aesculus, “A veritable
polychrest having a wide range of action, but
like some other polychrests it has a central
point of action from which radiates a series of
reflex symptoms.

This central point of action is the liver and
portal system and nine out of every ten of its
symptoms are due to this action.” It is one of
our great haemorhoidal remedies.

CLINICAL :

Backache. Constipation. Coryza. Haemorrhoids. Liver-affection of Uterine—affection
of.
SPHERES OF ACTION :

It appears to have a marked influence on the venous especially portal system, liver
and lumbo-sacral articulation.

Mucous membrances of mouth, throat, rectum also come under the domain of this
remedy.

Pathogenesis : It produces many symptoms of disordered liver resulting engorged
haemorhoidal veins. Due to congestion of veins sense of fullness, dryness, heat & pain
are produced.

Constitution : It is suitable to persons having haemorrhoidal tendencies and who
suffer with gastric, bilioiis or catarrhal troubles. It is an antipsoric medicine.
GUIDING SYMPTOMS :

1) Severe dull backache in lumbo-sacral articulation; more or less constant,
affecting sacrum & hips is the greatest concomitant symptom.

2) Sensation of fullness in various parts such as heart, lungs, stomach, anus, brain,
pelvis etc.

3) Piles-purple, painful, burning, blind, rarely bleeding.

4) Stool followed by fullness of rectum and intense pain in anus for hours (Aloe., Ign.,
Mur. Ac., Nit.ac. Sulph., Ratan., Paeonia.)

5) Frequent inclination to swallow, with burning, pricking stinging and dry constricted
fauces (Apis., Bell.)
