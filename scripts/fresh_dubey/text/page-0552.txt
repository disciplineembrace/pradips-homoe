{INTRODUCTION

Sambucus nigra is yet another excellent
remedy from the vegetable kingdom suitable for
respiratory troubles. The mother tincture is
prepared from the leaves and flowers. The
freshly expressed juice contains viburnic acid
and vibriani which produce an unpleasant odour.
The trees are found in Central Europe. The inner
bark as well as the leaves are employed for
making ointment for external application. The
flowers are used for fermentation and also for
preparing medicated tea. It was proved and
added to Materia Medica Pura by our Master
Hahnemann.

CLINICAL

Asthma, Chest oppression of, Goryza, Cough, Croup, Headache, Hoarseness,
Perspiration, Whooping cough, etc.
SPHERES OF ACTION

The main seat of action of Sambucus nigra is the mucous membrane of respiratory
organs, nasal passage, larynx, chest and skin.
PATHOGENESIS

Its action on the mucous membrane results in difficult spasm of the respiratory
organs, dyspnoea, coryza and suffocative cough. It also produces profuse debilitating
sweat, especially during waking hours. In addition, it causes oedematous swellingon
various parts of the body particularly legs.

CONSTITUTION

Make-up of the body: It is adapted to persons who were formerly robust and fleshy,
but suddenly became emaciated; and to the diseases of scrofulous children with affection
of the air passage especially.

Relation with heat and cold: Very chilly patient.
Miasm: Psora is in the background,
Diathesis: Scrofulous diathesis.
