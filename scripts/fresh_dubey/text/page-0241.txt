INTRODUCTION

Collinsonia Can. is an important haemor-
rhoidal remedy of our materia medica. Most of
the symptoms of collin. are due to portal
congestion ajid pelvic congestion. Due to its
congestive action it produces severe
constipation, piles, dysmenorrhoea, headache
and even heart symptoms etc.

1) Congestion especially of portal & pelvic
region resulting in dysmenorrhoea and
haemorrhoids.

2) Chronic, painful, bleeding piles with sensation as if sticks, sand or gravel had
lodged in rectum. Vascular engorgement of rectum. Most obstinate constipation
with protruding piles. There is congestion of pelvic viscera with piles in later

months of pregnancy, haemorrhoidal dysentery with tenesmus.

3) Constipation : It is very admirable remedy for constipation and usually presents in
association with the haemorrhoids, headache and flatulence. In Collinsonia
constipation is generally due to congestive inertia of lower bowel. (Sulph. Sepia).
Stool is sluggish and hard with pain and great flatulence. Dr. Nash has permanently
cured a case of obstinate constipation with Collinsonia in a patient who averaged
only two stools a week for years and had taken any amount of cathartics without

any lasting relief.

4) Heart complaints associated with piles. Palpitation in patients subject to piles and
indigestion. Heart’s action persistently rapid but weak. In Collinsonia after relief of

heart complaints old piles reappear or suppressed menses return.

5) Pruritus in pregnancy with haemorrhoids, unable to lie down. The genitals are

swollen and inflammed.

General Modalities :

from cold.
