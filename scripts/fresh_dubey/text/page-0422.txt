INTRODUCTION

Mercurius is the KING OF ANTI-
SYPHILITIC remedies, prepared and proved by
Hahnemann. It is prepared by precipitating
Mercury from its solution in Nitric acid by means
of Caustic ammonia. The Pathogenesis of
Mercury is found in the provings of Merc, viv.,
and Merc. sol. “I'wo different preparations, but
not different enough to make any distinctions in
practice” remarks DR KENT. Even CLARKE
supports this fact in his Dictionary by saying,
“No pains have been taken to keep distinct Merc, sol and Merc, viv., and I do not find it
practicable to attempt to separate them”. One more thing to clarify is that the pure
element, Mercurius, manifested the same symptoms as soluble mercury—Merc. sol.
Hence there is no point in separately discussing them.

SPHERES OF ACTION

Every organ and tissue of the body is more or less affected by this powerful drug—
such as bones, glands, lymphatic system, internal organs, intestine, mucous membrane,
etc.

PATHOGENESIS

Lesions produced by Mercury are very similar to those of syphilis. It decomposes
blood, producing profound anaemia. It has great power over suppuration with sero-
Sanguinous discharge, slimy in character. It also produces ulceration of mouth and
throat, much salivation, with falling of hair. It causes inflammation which leads to
induration and from induration to ulceration.
