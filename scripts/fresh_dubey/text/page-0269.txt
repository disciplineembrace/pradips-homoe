DIGITALIS PURPUREA

Foxglove HAHNEMANN Scrophulariaceae

INTRODUCTION

This is another excellent remedy from
the vegetable kingdom, usually indicated in
heart diseases. This is a wild plant grown
best in siliceous soil in bushy grounds and
waste places of Europe. The fresh leaves
from this uncultivated plant are gathered,
and from this the mother tincture is
prepared and subsequent potencies are
made.

Angina pectoris, Asthma,  Bright’s
disease, Cyanosis, Delirium, Diarrhoea, Dropsy, Headache, Heart affection, Lung
troubles, Urinary troubles, etc.

SPHERES OF ACTION

Its main seat of action is heart and circulation, gastro-intestinal tract and uro-genital
organs.

Its action through cardiac peripheral end of vagus inhibits the activity of heart,
resulting in SLOW HEART BEAT; prolonged diastole and rise of blood pressure. It also

causes suppression of urine producing oedema. It is also capable of irritating gastro-
intestinal tract, producing vomiting and diarrhoea.

CONSTITUTION

There is no particular constitution or make-up of the body as such. However, the
patient is light-haried scrofulous; children of fair complexion and people of climacfric age
having weak heart, but without valvular complicaions. Blueness of the skin, with cyanosis
of eye-lids, lips, tongue and nails.

Relation with heat and cold: Chilly patient with great sensitiveness to cold air and
cold weather; cold food and drink which aggravate.

Miasm: Psora and Sycosis are in the background,

Diathesis: Scrofulous diathesis.
