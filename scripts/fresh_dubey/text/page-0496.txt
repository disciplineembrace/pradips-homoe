INTRODUCTION

Platinum, the costliest and the heaviest
metal in the mineral kingdom, is nick-named by
some authors as the “BLACK” LETTER
MEDICINE” meaning thereby that the drug has
occupied its place in pur pharmacopoeia by
causing (in proving) and by curing (in
homoeopathic preparation) maladies especially
mental or those connected with perverted mental
conditions. The curious thing in Platina, is that
this mental subconscious state extends outwards
into the physical sphere.

general tendency to anaesthesia, paralysis, etc, manifested especially in women.

melancholia and menstrual disorders.
