SYZYGIUM JAMBOLANUM

Jambul Myrtaceae

CLINICAL
Diabetes melitus, Prickly heat. Ulcers;

It is a native of India, The tincture is
prepared from pow-dered seeds. The fruit is of
the size of grapes, black in colour and tastes
delicious.

It is a most useful remedy in diabetes
melitus as it causes a marked diminution of
sugar in urine.

Great thirst, weakness, emaciation inspite of
proper nutriti-ous diet, profuse urination of high
specific gravity, are the characteristic indications.

Prickly heat in upper part of the body. Small red pimples with much itching.
This is also one of the important symptoms which the author has verified (can be given
exter-nally and internally).

It is also to be thought of in old ulcers of skin associated with diabetes melitus.
