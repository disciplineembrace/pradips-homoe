SPHERES OF ACTION & PATHOGENESIS

Its principal action is in its tendency to cause
induration & enlargement of glands of the body. Next to glands its chief action is on the
respiratory tract & heart. It also acts on female & male genitals. Tendency to infiltrate
glands, become hard but seldom suppurate.
