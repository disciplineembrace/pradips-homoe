GUIDING SYMPTOMS
1) Congestions, relieved by haemorrhage.

2) Headache : Violent congestive or nervous headaches.
Location : Frontal Headache intense in left supra orbital region.

Sensation : Sensation of tension and wave like movement in brain with vertigo and
nausea.

Character : Violent, congestive, periodical, nervous headaches, every week or every
four weeks. Modality—Aggravation—By motion, by thinking. Amelioration—By
lying down, by profuse urination, by epistaxis, by menstruation. Concomitant—
Retching, vomiting, cold hands and feet.

3) Epistaxis preceded by intense redness, flushing of face and throbbing of carotids
with general relief.

4) Very red face precedes haemorrhage from every organ.

5) Constipation : Stool is difficult, painful with feeling con-striction, fullness and
throbbing in anus. No desire until there is large accumulation.

6) Convulsions of nervous children during dentition. It is also a medicine for
infantile spasms, eclampsia & epilepsy.

7) Mind : Inability to fix mind, to study; memory will not retain any thing. Wants to
run away and hide. Delusion; thinks every one is looking at her. Religious
melancholy with intensely red face.

8) General Modalities :
Aggravation—rainy changeable weather, approach storm, motion, 4 p.m.
