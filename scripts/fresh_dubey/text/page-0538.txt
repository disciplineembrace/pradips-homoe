INTRODUCTION

Rhus is a native medicine of the North America and {s
one of those most valuable medicines which we owe to the
domestic practice of the North American Indians.
Hahnemann in his Materia Medica Pura praised this
medicine as a remarkable and valuable medicinal substance
having a great number of characteristic peculiarities in its
action. There are other members of the Rhus family which
are also used in the Homoeopathic Therapeutic.

CLINICAL

Abortion, Beriberi, Cough, Chilblains, Cyanosis,
Dengue fever, Diarrhoea, Dysentery, Eczema, Enteric
fever, Erysipelas, Eye and Ear affection, Gout, Headache, Herpes zoster, Lumbago, Measles.
Menstrual disorders, Neuralgia, Paralysis, Ptosis, Pyaemia, Rheumatism, Restlessness, Sciatica,
Sleep, Sprain, Tongue affection, etc.

SPHERES OF ACTION

Rhus tox. acts predominantly upon the organs of animal life, upon the mucous membrane, lymphatic
glands, skin, muscular tissues and component parts of joints e.g. LIGAMENTS, FIBROUS TISSUES.
TENDONS, etc.

By its actions, it produces a condition of primary irritation of serous membrane which may proceed
to inflammation or if it stops, it produces serous discharge, effusion and oedema. On the skin it produces
theumatoid inflammation, eruptions, burning and itching. The lymph glands throughout the body enlarge
and inflame under the influence of Rhus tox. When circulatory system becomes the seat of its action, it
causes a state of blood poisoning typhoid like symptoms and febrile disturbances. Intermittent fevers, etc.
are also noticed.

CONSTITUTION

Since it is more or less an acute remedy, the physical, make-up or temperament, is not of much
importance. However, the patient is very chilly, easily susceptible to cold and aggravated by cold in general.

Relation with heat and cold: Very CHILLY patient. Even open air is intolerable.
Miasm: Psora is in the background of all the troubles.
Diathesis: Rheumatic and gouty diathesis.
