INTRODUCTION

One of the richest and very useful remedy of
the Vegetable kingdom belonging to the family
Iridaceae, is Crocus sativus, commonly named as
saffron. Saffron is used frequently as a
Condiment. It bears an individual characteristic,
being brick-red in colour which, on proving
produces haemorrhages of DARKISH RED hue.
Of course, brightness may also be an occasional
feature. VISCIDITY AND STRINGINESS are the
other two characteristics of its blood picture.

CLINICAL

SPHERES OF ACTION

It acts especially upon the female genital organs, but eyes, chest and abdomen may
also be affected in rare instances. Brain and mind too are important centres for its action.

It causes chorea and hysteria. Acting on female genital organs; it causes abortion and
produces HAEMORRHAGE, THE BLOOD BEING DARK, STRINGY AND CLOTTED.

hysterical temperament.
