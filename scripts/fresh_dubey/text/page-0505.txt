PARTICULARS

Headache :
It is indicated in chronic sick headache.
Time: Periodic headache.
Sensation: As if the head would burst.

Character: Congestive type of headache, as if all the blood has rushed towards the head. Burstins
type of headache especially on the back of the head.

Modality: Worse in the morning and in hot weather.
Concomitants: Headache alternates with diarrhoea; headache in winter and diarrhoea in summer.

Abdomen:
Distended abdomen.

Liver region is very painful. The patient constantly rubs and shakes the region of liver with
hands. Sensation of weakness and sinking in abdomen. Rumbling and shifting of flatus in abdomen with
nausea and vomiting.

There is thirst for large quantity of cold water (Bry).
Constipation :

Clay-coloured stool, very much dry, hard vioded with great difficulty. Constipation of bottle fed
babies with dry crumbling stool (Alumina).
