GENERAL MODALITIES
Aggravation : Noon, before midnight, while standing, when sitting.
Amelioration : From walking. heat in any form after sleep. Better by uncovering, cold
bathing & in open air.
Dulcamara :
Urticaria over whole body, no fever; itching burns after scratching.
Aggravated by warmth & better by cold.

Nat. Mur : Urticaria, acute or chronic; over wholebody; espe-cially after violent
exercise.
Antipyrine : Urticaria appearing and disappearing suddenly with internal coldness.
