ODUM

“Todine HAHNEMANN The Element

_——

INTRODUCTION

It is a drug proved and recorded in chronic
diseases by Hahnemann, who speaks of it as a
heroic medicine, even when employed in the highest
potency. Iodine, belongs to a family of Halogens,
others being Chlorine, Bromine and Fluorine. If at
all a student wants to remember lodum in rhymes,
the following few lines may be helpful.

“Ravenous Iodine howls for her dinner,

Eats much sand eats often, but only grows thinner,
Her phenomenal meals she’s ever repeating,
Because all her Symptoms are better by eating,
She is fidgety, fearful, unable to rest;

While, she eats, while she walks, in the cold, she feels best
Air her glands (save the mammae) congested appear,

With corrosive, destructive to clothes leucorrhoea.”

CLINICAL

Appetite disordered, Atrophy, Brain affection, Constipation, Consumption, Cough,
Debility, Diarrhoea, Diptheria, Goitre, Glandular affection, Headache, Heart affection,
Jaundice, Laryngitis, Leucorrhoea, Liver affection, Menstrual disorders, Pneumonia,
Rheumatism, Rickets, Throat and lung affection, etc.

SPHERES OF ACTION

lodum especially acts on all the glandular system, lymph glands, thyroid, liver.
spleen, breasts, ovaries, testes, salivary glands, etc. Mucus membrane of eyes, nose.
respiratory tract and even blood vessels are not spared from its action.

PATHOGENESIS

When acting on the glandular system, it leads to HYPERTROPHY, ENLARGEMENT
AND INDURATION, EXCEPTING THE BREASTS WHICH DWINDLE AND
ATROPHY. The hypertrophy of glands is at the expense of muscles, generalised
emaciation. Mucus membrane produces a state of catarrhal inflammation. Blood vessels
get Constricted, vaso-constriction, capillary congestion followed by oedema, ecchymosis
haemorrhage and nutritive disturbances.
