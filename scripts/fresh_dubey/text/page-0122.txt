INTRODUCTION

Belladonna was known to the medical
profession as early as 1500 A.D., but its full
therapeutic value was not realised until it was
potentised and proved by Hahnemann. The word
“Belladonna” is a combination of two Latin words
“Bella” and “Donna” which means “Beautiful
Lady”. The Venetian ladies, of olden days used
this plant as a cosmetic to brighten their eyes
and flush the cheeks due to the property of its
alkaloids, Atropin and Belladonnin. It was
Hahnemann’s deep insight which could find out the immense curative value of these
alkaloids and transformed them into a marvellous medicine through the process of
potentisation.

Belladonna is one of Hahnernann’s Polychrests which has its Paramount utility in
ACUTE AND VIOLENT CONDITIONS. It is one of those medicines without which we

might indeed to close our shops.
Belladonna is one of CLARKE’S A.B.C. NURSERIES, others being Aconite and

Chamomilla. WHILE BELLADONNA CAUSES TURMOIL IN BRAIN, ACONITE and
CHAMOMILLA CAUSE TURMOIL IN CIRCULATION AND TEMPERAMENT

RESPECTIVELY.
