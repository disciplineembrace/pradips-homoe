DULCAMARA

Bitter Sweet HAHNEMANN Solanaceae

INTRODUCTION

For this important drug and its exact and
scientific use, we owe gratitude to our Master
Hahnemann, It was regarded by him as “a very
powerful plant” and he says that its duration of
action is a long one. “Before the scientific proving
of the drug was made”, remarked Kent “our
mothers used to make ointments of Dulecamara
for its astonishing, soothing effects on smarting
wounds over the skin surface”. This effect has
been proved and established rationally by a dozen
of provers whom Hahnemann employed.

CLINICAL

Anasarco, Catarrh, Bladder affection, Cough, Cholera, Diarrhoea, Dropsy, Dysentery,
Fevers, Headache, Herpes, Influenza, Lumbago, Measles, Meningitis, Nettle rash,
Neuralgia, Ophthalmia, Nose affection, Skin troubles, Tonsillitis, Urinary affection,
Warts, Whooping cough, etc.

SPHERES OF ACTION
Dulcamara affects subversively as well as curatively all mucous membranes, besides
glands, skin and muscles.

PATHOGENESIS

It causes glandular enlargement, especially of the lymphatic system, cellular effusion
and produces skin eruptions. It also produces catarrhal inflammation of mucous
membrane and skin, resulting in profuse secretion-of mucous membrane and skin.

CONSTITUTION
Physical make-up : Especially suited to persons of phlegmatic scrofulous constitution.
Temperament: Irritable temperament.

Relation with heat and cold: Neither chilly not hot. Easily affected by sudden change
from heat to cold or from cold to wet rainy weather.

Miasm: Sycotic and Psoric patient.
