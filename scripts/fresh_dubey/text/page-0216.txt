CHININUM ARSENICOSUM

“Arsenite of Quinine DR. WILLIAM BIGLER

INTRODUCTION

The symptoms of general weariness and
prostration produced by the drug have been
utilised in prescribing it homoeopathically as a
general tonic, often with very marked beneficial
and prompt effect. In diphtheria specially
prolonged cases, malaria, neuralgia etc. it has
been found curative. It is also useful in asthmatic
attacks which recur periodically, with great
prostration.

CLINICAL
Asthma, Diarrhoea, Gastralgia. Hectic fever.
Neuralgia. Rheumatism.

1) Neuralgia : The periodicity is itensified (Cedron, Kali Bich.) An irritable mood
precedes the headache, which is aggravated by mental and bodily exercise.

2) Gastralgia : Pressure in solar, plexus with tender spine just at back of it.
Alteration of hyperacidity and decrease of acid, nausea and vomiting a.re followed

by sleep.
3) Diarrhoea : Egg produces diarrhoea.

4) Gastralgia from coarse food of ill-baked bread. Indiges-tion from unripe fruits. Colic
from incarcerated flatus (Raphanus).

5) Chronic rheumatism of joints without swelling.

6) Intermittent fever : With great prostration & periodicity. Chill stage
predominates with little thirst and continuous almost convulsive yawning.
