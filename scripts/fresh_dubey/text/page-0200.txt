CARDUUS MARIANUS

Silybum DR. REIL Compositae

INTRODUCTION

Carduus M. is pre-eminently a liver remedy.
It stands next to Chelidonium in our materia
medica so far liver complaints are concerned, It
is, useful in various kinds of complaints which
are secondary to liver affections.

CLINICAL

Bronchitis. Dropsy. Epistaxis. Fever.Gail
stones. Haemorrhoids. Influenza, Jaundice.
Liver—affection of. Metrorrhagia, Neuralgia.
Rheumatism. Varicose Veins. Spleen—affection of. Hemoptysis. Cholecystitis.

Cholelithiasis. Hepatitis.
PHYSIOLOGICAL ACTION
It has at least three special centres of action through the nervous system.
1) Liver—It acts upon the liver and portal system giving rise to jaundice and bilious
disturbances.
2) Bowels—Here it acts as a laxative producing a catarrhal discharge.

3) Kidneys—It produces diuresis.

GUIDING SYMPTOMS

1) Dragging pain in right hypochondrium when lying on left side (Arn., Mag. M., Nat.
S.)

2) The liver enlargement in the transverse direction (that of Cheli, more vertical.)

3) Sadness, depressed and hypochondriacal with vertigo and dull heavy pain in
forehead.

4) Constipation : Stools hard, difficult, knotty, alternates with diarrhoea. Clay
coloured bileless or bright yellow.

5) Want of appetite with nausea, retching, vomiting of green acid fluid.

6) Complaints from abuse of alcoholic beverages.

7) Complaints such as dropsy, haemoptysis, spleen affec-tions, influenza etc. when
associated with liver derange-ment.

8) Modalities : Agg.—Lying on “left side, beer, eating, motion, pressure, from stooping.
