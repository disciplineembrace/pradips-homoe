INTRODUCTION

It is one of the important remedies for
rheumatism and especially adapted to people of
rheumatic and gouty diathesis with tendency to
metastasis. Dr. Hering and his friends proved
this remedy for the first time and introduced it
into homoeopathic practice.

CLINICAL
Angina Pectoris, Gout. Heart—disease of,
Reratitis, Neuralgia. Rheumatism. Vertigo.
SPHERES OF ACTION AND PATHOGENESIS
Kalmia has prominent action on heart, blood circulation, nerves, joints and skin.

GUIDING SYMPTOMS

1) Kalmia is adapted to rheumatic and gouty complaints esp. when heat is involved as
a sequel of rheumatism or gout.

2) Pain : (a) sticking, darting, pressing, shooting in a downward direction.
(b) Pain attended or succeeded by numbness of affected part.
3) Heart complaints developed from or alternate with rheumatism.

4) Rhuematism : Joints hot, red, swollen and painful ; shifting pain going from one
joint to another suddenly.

5) Pulse slow, scarcely perceptible (35 to 40 per minute) pale face and cold extremitis.
6) Severe stitching pain in right eye and orbit ; pain worse when turning the eyes.

7) Vertigo when stooping or looking down (Spig.)

8) Ailments from : Chill, Exposure, wind, sun.

PARTICULARS
Eyes :

It is particularly useful in neuralgic and rheumatic pain in the eyes and eye balls. There is stiff
drawing sensation when moving the eyes. Severe stitching pain in right eye and orbit with stiffness
in muscle. Rheumatic iritis, scleritis with impairment of vision.
