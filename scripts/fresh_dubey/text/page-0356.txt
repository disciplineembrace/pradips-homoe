Kali Carbonicum 347

Concomitants: With, soreness about genitals; pain from back passes down to gluteal muscles. CONS
TANTBACKACHE with cutting pain in abdomen.
Uterine haemorrhage :

Blood constantly oozing out after copious flow with violent backache.

Relieved by, sitting and pressure.

Back :

There is SEVERE BACKACHE during pregnancy; after miscarriage. Small of back fells weak.
Lumbago with sudden sharp pain extending up and down the back and travelling to thighs.
General Modalities :

Aggravation: After coition, at rest, generally worse in morning, in cold weather, from soup and
coffees, 2.to 4 a.m. lying on left and painful side.

Amelioration: Warm weather, by rocking, during day when moving about.

Relations :
Complementary to: Carbo veg., phos., Sep., Nit. ac., Nat. mur.
It follows: Nux vom. well in stomach and bladder troubles.
Antidoted by: Camph., Coffea.
