PARTICULARS

Headache :
Causation : Milk, loss of sleep.
Location : Begins in forehead extending to occiput (Bry., Nux V., Sepia, Tuber.).

Sensation : As if head would burst. There is also a sensation as if top of head was lifted off and brain
was coming out.

Character of pain : The pain is most excruciating and unbearable. Intense throbbing pain in
forehead.

Modalities : Aggravation—in morning on rising, noise, light, motion, during menses, conversation.
periodically (every 8 days).

Amelioration : Pressure, tight bandaging, profuse urination, cold application.

Concomitant : Headache in anaemic, chilly patients who have tendency to constipation. Headache

associated with intense photophobia, great postration, nausea and vomiting and blindness. Headache
alternating with tonsillitis (Clarke).

Constipation : It is needed in inveterate, intensely chronic cases where the most powerful purgative
fails. It is usually associated with periodical headache and vomiting. The feaeces are dry hard ain
large and they lacerate anus extorting cries with ineffectual urging. Dropsy : It has been used in
dropsy from organic heart disease or weak heart, from chronic liver complaint, far advanced albuminuna
also from suppressed malaria.
