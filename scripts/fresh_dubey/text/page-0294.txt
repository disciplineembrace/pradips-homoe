Ferrum Metallicum 265

Anaemia :

Causation: From loss of vital fluid, abuse of quinine. Anaemic condition due to loss of blood,
especially from haemorrhage.

Due to anaemia, the patient is PALE and waxy, muscles become flabby and relaxed.

There is dropsical condition of skin which pits on pressure. The skin is pale, waxy, yet the face shows
an appearance of plethora. From every little excitement the face becomes flushed.
General Modalities :

Aggragation: At night, at rest especially while sitting still.

Amelioration: Walking about slowly and in summer.

Relations :
Complementary to: Alumina, Cinchona.
Antidoted by: Ars., Hepar, Ipecac., Puls.
Antidotes: Ars., lod., Merc.
