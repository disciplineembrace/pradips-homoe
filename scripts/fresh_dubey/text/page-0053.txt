AMBRA GRISEA

Ambergris A. Nosode
INTRODUCTION

About its origin Dr. H.C.Allen says. “Of
doubtful origin, for years on the borderland as to
its true classification, but probably a nosode or
morbid product found in the belly of the
spermwhale, It has many characteristics of
intestinal or biliary concretions and has been
considered to be of hepatic origin, probably a
fatty excretion from its gall bladder” Dr. N.M.
Chowdhury says, “Ambergris is a fatlike
substance found in the intestines and among the
excreta of the spermwhale.” The Oil of Amber i.e.

Oleum Succium should not be confounded with
Ambergris.

CLINICAL

Constipation. Convulsions. Cough. Dyspepsia. Hysteria. Menstrual disorder.
Leucorrhoea. Neuroses. Nymphomania. Ranula.

SPHERES OF ACTION

It affects predominantly nervous system especially pneumogastric, spinal nerves and
solar plexus. It also acts on gastrointestinal and respiratory tracts and female genital
organs.

PATHOGENESIS |

Acting on nervous system it produces faintness, jerking, twitching and other nervous
hysterical manifestations. It affects female genital organs manifesting as leucor-rhoes.
menstrual disorder and pruritus valvae etc,

CONSTITUTION

Physical make up : It is suited to old people and children especially young girls whe
are excitable, nervous and weak. Lean, thin emaciated nervous persons and thin scrawny
women (Sec. Cor.)

Temperament—Nervous bilious temperament.

Heat cold relation—Patients are aggravated from warm and better from cold i
general.

Miasm—Psora and syphilis are in the background.
