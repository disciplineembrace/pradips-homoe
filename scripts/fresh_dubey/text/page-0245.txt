CONDURANGO

Condor Plant J.P. DINSMORE Asclepiadaceae

CLINICAL

Cancer of stomach, Cancer of Breast. Angular
stomatitis. Epithelioma of lips or anus. Fissures.
Stricture of Oesophagus.

According to the teachings of Hahnemann,
Dr. Burnett proved this drug on himself to find
out its curative sphere. He regards rhagades at
mucocutaneous orifices (Nit. Acid.) and warty
excrescences to be most leading indications for it.
Dr. Burnett and Dr. Dudgeon both reported a
case of cancer of a left breast cured with
Condurango, when accompanied by sores or
cracks at corners of mouth. Condurango has also
cured cancer of the stomach with vomiting of
coffee ground masses, loss of appetite, and
emaciation. It is also useful in cases of stricture
of oesophagus with burning pains behind sternum, where food seems to stick. It
stimulates the digestive functions and thus improves the general health.

Author has relieved a case of cancer of left breast in a lady of fifty-nine. Tumour was
hard & quiet a big in size and was not improved at all under Hydrastis and Phytolacca.
Under Cundurango in tincture doeseit improved miraculously & a lady survived for ten
