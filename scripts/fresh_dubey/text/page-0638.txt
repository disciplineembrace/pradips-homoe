URTICA URENS

“Stinging-Nettle BURNETT Urticaceae

—_

INTRODUCTION

Urtica Urens is a remedy of a very
ancient lineage. We find this remedy
mentioned in the writings of Diascorides,
who extolled it very highly as a great
cleansing agent. In our time Dr. Burnett
has taken it up and given it a very
extensive proving.

CLINICAL

Agalactia. Bee-stings. Burns. Fever.
Gout. Gravel. Pruritus. Rheumatism.
Uraemia. Urticaria. Spleen-affections of.
Menorrhagia.

SPHERE OF ACTION
Skin and mucous membrane, mammae, genitourinary organs, spleen and joints.

GUIDING SYMPTOMS

1) Urticaria— Burning stinging and violent itching of skin, Urticaria disappears
while lying down and reappears while getting up. It may come before rheumatism
with fever. Burning in skin is aggravated after sleep.

2) Burns—It is used in “first degree of burns”"—locally and internally. It should be
thought of even prior to Cantharis.

3) Rheumatism and Gout : Rheumatism alternates with urticaria. Uric acid diathesis.
4) Symptoms return at the same time every year

5) Dr. Burnett used Urtica urens in acute gout associated with fever.

6) Ailments from : Burns. Beestings. Flows. Suppressed milk. Suppressed nettle
rash. Ill effects of shell fish and nettle rash.

PARTICULARS

Female

Uterine haemorrhage. Acrid and excoriating leucorrhoca, Pruritus vulvae, with stinging, itching and
oedema.
