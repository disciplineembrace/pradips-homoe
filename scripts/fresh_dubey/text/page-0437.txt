Typhoid :

Causation: Heat of summer and sun,

Symptoms: Debility and prostration are out of proportion to illness. So the patient SLIDES
DOWN IN BED. THE LOWER JAW HANGS DOWN WITH GREAT DEBILITY.

Deep, stupid sleep; unconscious while awake, loud meaning and muttering.
There is more chill than heat. The chill awakens him in the morning.

Chill with coldness in back and with warmth and burning in face.

Hands are cold and cheeks are hot.

There is intense heat. So the patient wants to uncover. Burning in palms and soles.
Heat at night with palpitation of heart which is felt in the face.

Concomitants : Tongue coated at edges; shrunken, dry, and Jeather like. Tongue is paralysed; very
offensive smell from mouth and in breath.

Ulcerations with fungus like growth and pseudeo membranous deposits in the intestinal tract.
Passage of involuntary foetid stool while passing urine, intermits at every third beat.
Headache comes regularly everyday from 9 a.m. to 1 p.m.
General Modalities :
Amelioration: By lying on left side and uncovering (during fever).
Aggravation: From touch and after sleep.
Relations :
Follows well: After Rhustox., Bryonia and Mercunus.
It antidotes: Opium.
Its antidotes are: All carbonates of sodium and potassium, Camphor and Bryonia.
