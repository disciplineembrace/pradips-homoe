ALLIUM CEPA

Onion HERING Lilaceae

INTRODUCTION

The mother tincture of Allium cepa is
prepared from the fresh red bulbs which
abundantly grow in India. Ordinarily it has no
therapeutic value, but when it is potentised, it
serves as an excellent remedy for any catarrhal
inflammation. It is more or less an acute drug.

CLINICAL

Catarrah, Cold, Coryza, Cough, Diarrhoea,
Influenza, Injury, Laryngitis, Nasal polypus,
Throat affections, Whooping cough etc.
SPHERES OF ACTION

Its main seats of action are conjunctiva,
mucous membrane of nose, respiratory tract, intestine, nerves and veins.

PATHOGENESIS

It causes irritation of conjunctiva and mucous membrane of nose, respiratory and
gastro-intestinal tracts, producing acute catarrhal inflammation and profuse discharge.
It also causes neuralgia and inflammation of veins.

CONSTITUTION

Since it is mainly an acute drug, consideration: .of . constitution is not important. The
patient is, however, very hot with psoric miasm in the background.

GUIDING SYMPTOMS
1. Allium cepa is a left sided medicine. Symptoms go from left to-right.

2. In common cold or cough or headache, which is aggravated in a warm room, better
in open air and worse again on-returning to a warm room, Allium cepa must be
thought of.

3. Profuse watery and acrid nasal discharge with profuse bland lachrymation (rev. of
Euphrasia where nasal discharge is bland and lachrymation is acrid).

4. Desire for raw onion is an indication for the remedy (Cub.).
5. Aggravation in warm room and in the evening; relieved in cold room and open air.

6. Violent laryngitis, cough seems to TEAR AND SPLIT THE LARYNX.
