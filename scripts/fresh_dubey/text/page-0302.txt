Before the headache: Dim vision or blindness.
Modalities:
* Aggravation. From heat of sun and summer.
* Amelioration: By compression, lying down with head high and fay profuse urination.

Concomitants: Vertigo, spreading from occiput, with heaviness of head. Headache with muscular

TREMBLING.

Eye:
Eyelids are heavy and consequently there is ptosis. Patient can hardly open them; double vision;
orbital neuralgia with contraction and twitching of muscles,
Mouth :
There is putrid taste in the mouth. Tongue is thick, coated, yellowish, trembling and paralysed.

Throat :

Difficulty in swallowing, especially warm food. Post-diphtheritic paralysis (Arg. nit., Causticum).
Food and water requrgitate from the mouth and through nose at every attempt to shallow.
Heart :

Snesation as if it were necessary to keep in motion, or else heart's action would cease. Pulse is slow,
soft and weak when quiet, but greatly accelerated on motion.
Diarrhoea :

Gelsemium has got purely nervous diarrhoea.

Causation: Emotional excitement, fright and bad news.

Character of stool: Involuntary, painless and cream-coloured.

Concomitants: Thirstlessness, dullness, dizziness, drowsiness and trembling.

Urine :

Profuse, clear, watery urine with chilliness and trembling and intermittent flow or retention of urine.
Profuse urination relieves headache.

Fever :

Gelsemium is indicated in ANY AND EVERY TYPE of fever — intermittent, remittent or continuous
ever.
