Heart:

For restoring a heart damaged by acute inflammation or from relief of sufferings of
chronic hypertrophy and valvu-lar lesions. Simple hypertrophy of heart. Excited,
tremend-ous action of heart. Palpitation of heart from exertion or talking or walking.
Severe stitching pain in the region of heart extending to nape of neck, left shoulder and
arm. Pulse irregular in force, but regular in rhythm. Sensation of oppres-sion in chest
and heavy weight on it. Marked symptoms of low tension. Threatened paralysis of heart,
post diptheritic. Irritating, dry, sympathetic cough in the acute stage of rheumatic
carditis or chronic organic lesions (Spongia). Pains aggrevated by carriage riding in open
air.
