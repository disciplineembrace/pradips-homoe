Compilation of our Materia Medica is greatly
helped by the careful and scientific observation
of symptoms brought about by the inadvertent
use of the poisonous doses, especially amongst
the painters. In the old school, metallic lead was

of Similia-in relation to the disease.

SPHERES OF ACTION

Plumbum acts especially upon the nervous system i.e., on spinal nerve centres. The
blood and the ailmentary system are also the special seats of action of this drug.

It irritates the spinal nerve centre causing increased sensibility, neuralgic spasm,
paralysis cheifly of extensors, especially of forearm, or upper limbs with partial
anaesthesia or excessive hyperaesthesia. Haematosis is interfered with. Rapid reduction
in the number of R.B.C. and pallor, icterus, anaemia, etc. frequently occur,
