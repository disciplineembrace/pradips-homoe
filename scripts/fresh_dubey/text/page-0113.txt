INTRODUCTION

Baptisia tinctoria is a Short acting and left-
sided rernedy and can undoubtedly be claimed as a
near about specific remedy for typhoid fever in any
stage—says Dr. P.C. Paul of Calcutta. More than
60% of early cases of typhoid point towards
Baptisia. Dr, Burnett says of it, “The king of all
remedies for typhoid. No remedy can supplant it in
the first stage”. Referring to this remedy Dr.
Farrington says: “Typifies a fever which is decided
typhoidly in tendency”. It can also be used as
preventive against typhoid. In its low potency, it
produces antibodies against typhoid bacilli. Dr. Bell
says, “Extended clinical observation has proved its
value when dysentery assumes the typhoid type”.

CLINICAL

Diarrhoea, Dysentery, Dilirium, Enteric fever, Headache, Hectic fever, Throat
affection, Tonsillitis, Uleerated tongue, Urinary affection, etc.
SPHERES OF ACTION

Baptisia acts on blood, mind, nerves, mucous membrane of digestive tract; left side of
the body.
PATHOGENESIS

Baptisia disorganises and decomposes the blood and thereby tends to putrescence,
specially in low fevers as in typhoid, paratyphoid and dysentery, etc. The principal use of
Baptisia is exact typhoid state. In low potency it is capable of producing antibodies
against the typhoid bacilli and thus it can undoubtedly be said to be a specific remedy for
typhoid fever. It can also be used as a preventive medicine against typhoid.
