LOBELIA INFLATA

“Indian Tobacco DR. JEANES Lobeliaceae

INTRODUCTION

Other names for this plant are Asthma root
and Indian tobacco. Taken in big quantity, it
produces intense nausea, vomiting, profuse
salivation and great prost-ration. These are the
key notes of this drug.

CLINICAL

Alcoholism. Asthma. Cough. Dyspepsia.
Dyspnoea. Emphysema. Gastralgia. Heart
com-plaints. Headache. Morning sickness.
SPHERES OF ACTION

Its action is centered on pneumogastric nerve, respiratory organs, epigastrium, heart
etc.

PATHOGENESIS

As it is a vasomotor stimulant it increases activities of all vegetative processes,
acting on pneumogastric nerve. It produces a depressed relaxed condition with
oppression of eldest and epigastrium, impeded respiration, nausea and vomiting.
CONSTITUTION

Physical make-up—It is especially suited to persons of light hair, blue eyes, fair
complexion and persons inclined to be freshy.

Relation with heat & cold—Chilly patient.
Miasm—Psoric & Syphilitic in the back ground.
GUIDING SYMPTOMS

1) Extreme nausea, vomiting, profuse salivation, sweating and marked prostration are
the indications in many com-plaints.

2) Faintness, weakness and indescribable feeling at epigas-trium.

3) Sensation of pressure or weight in chest or congestion as if blood from extremeties
was filling it, relieved by rapid walking.

4) Sensation as if heart would stand still. There is deep seated pain at the base of
heart.

5) Extreme sensitiveness of sacrum, patient cannot bear slightest touch even of a
soft pillow.
