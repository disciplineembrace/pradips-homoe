PARTICULARS
Headache :

Causation : Climacteric headache. Location : Hemicrania especially left side is affected. Sensation
. There is sensation as if blood would start through skin, with heat & redness and sensation as if
something were rushing up into the head, with throbbing. There is intense surging of blood to face and
head.

Modality: Aggravation : In warm room, mental & physical exertion.
Amelioration : Open air, cold water, cold air.

Concomitant: Affected side often looks paler than the other side. Easy flushing and blushing of the
face.

Heart:

Amyl Nitrite is one of the grand remedies for Angina pectoris in our Materia Medica. There is
praecordial anxiety, cardiac oppression, stitching pains and a feeling of constric-tion in the cardiac
region. At the least excitement the face looks flushed and pulsation is felt over the entire body. The
heart's action becomes intensified and almost tumultuous. Intense throbbing of the carotids is also noticed.
Patient craves fresh air. There is sensation of swelling of the front of chest and a bursting out feeling in
ears. Profound and repeated yawning.
