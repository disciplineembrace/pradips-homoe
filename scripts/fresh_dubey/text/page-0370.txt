INTRODUCTION

Lac caninum is an animal product, used as
food of early animal life and, therefore,
corresponds to the, beginning of our innermost
physical nature. In crude and natural state, milk
is a complete food, but when it is potentised it is
an infallible remedy for many diseases, if
symptoms correspond. Persons who dislike milk
will become sick from taking it in. potentised
form. It is one of the ‘Ladies Remedies’.

Breast affection, Backache, Female sexual

SPHERES OF ACTION

Lac caninum acts especially upon the mucous membrane, female uro-genital system,
uterus, cervix, throat and mind.

In the throat it produces an inflammation, particularly of the left side; of course, it
extends to right side, but again settles back to the left. It inflames and congests the
ovaries and uterus.
