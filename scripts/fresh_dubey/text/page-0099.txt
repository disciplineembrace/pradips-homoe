This medicine was originally used by H.

and symptoms observed on patients have

SYPHERES OF ACTION :

Especially it acts upon mucous membr-ances of respiratory tract, digestive tract, and
also acts upon heart, glands and skin.
CONSTITUTION :

No particular look or physical make up is present is Ars-Iod.

Relation with heat & cold : Sensitive to heat & cold. Miasm : Psora & Syphilis in the
background. Diathesis : Tubercular diathesis.

GUIDING SYMPTOMS :
1) Discharges are irritating, Corrosive, profuse & greenish yellow.

2) Chronic nasal Catarrh : Thin, watery excoriating dis-charge from anterior &
posterior nares.

3) Enlarged scrofulous glands.

4) Recurrent fever with drenching night sweats, profound prostration, emaciation
with rapid feeble pulse.

5) Marked exfoliation of skin in large scales, leaving a raw exuding surface
beneath.

6) Epithelioma of the lip.

7) Cancer of breast after ulceration has set in.
