Text Book of MATERIA MEDICA

CHARACTERISTIC INDICATIONS

1. Dr. Schuessler found that Mag Phos has a great affinity for the nerves and muscles.
Thus it acts as an anti-spasmatic and greatly relieves nerve pain, cramps, and
convulsions and considered it as “The Pain Cell Salt”.

It corresponds to pains of all kinds with the exception of burning pains. Pains which
are darting, spasmodic, in character, boring, lightening-like, acccompanied by a
constrictive feeling. They are often changing in locality, and are relieved by warmth
and pressure. .

2. The Mag Phos condition is aggravated by cold and by touching and are relieved by
the application of heat, by pressure and by bending double.

CLINICAL USE
Think of Mag Phos for the following conditions:

Leg cramps in the night time

Injuries with shooting pains (follows Hypericum)
Shooting, radiating pains of inflammation and/or nerve injury
Whiplash and other spinal injuries

Facial tics and TMJ

Radiating tooth pain

Sciatic nerve pain

Menstrual cramps

Spasmotic coughing — croup, whooping cough
Colic in infants

Colitis, irritable bowel syndrome, stomach cramps
Parkinson’s disease

Epilepsy

Spasmodic retention of urine

Stomach cramps and flatulence

Nerve pains, such as neuralgia, neuritis and sciatica, and headaches

NOTE: Mag Phos will often act more rapidly when the tablets are taken with a sip of
hot water. The doses may be taken at frequent intervals until relief is obtained.
