CLINICAL

Asthma, Angina pectoris, Boils, Bone affection, Breast affection, Cancer, Cicatrices,
Constipation, Cough, Diptheria, Eye troubles, Gonorrhoea, Glandular affection,
Haemorrhoids, Headache, Leucorrhoea, Neuralgia, Rheumatism, Respiratory troubles,
Sciatia, Syphilis, Ulcers, etc.

SPHERES OF ACTION

Phytolacca acts chiefly upon the glandular system, particularly on the throat and
mammary glands. It also affects the serous, fibrous and mucous tissues.
PATHOGENESIS

It causes inflammation and irritation of the serous, fibrous, and mucus tissues, skin
and periosteum. On the glandular system, it causes tendency to ulceration.
CONSTITUTION

Physical make-up: Emaciated patient, chlorbtic with loss of fat. Face sunken, pale,
hippocratic, blue rings around the eyes with yellow complexion. Great exhaustion and
profound prostration.

Relation with heat and cold: Very chilly patient.
