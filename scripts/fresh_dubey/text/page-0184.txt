Cantharides 175

Character of urine: Bloody urine; comes drop by drop. Urine scalds the passage; jelry like, shreddy
urine.
There is retention or suppression of urine. Passes only a drop or two at a time.

Modalities:
* Aggravation : During urination, from touch, drinking cold water or coffee.
¢ Amelioration: By rubbing warm application (Clarke).

In gonorrhoea, there is violent burning and tenesmus of the bladder. There is severe cutting burning
pain while urinating. The urine burns like fire in the bladder and about the genitals.

Skin :
Cantharides is usually indicated in burns, scalds and erysipelas.
Sensation: There is burning and raw sensation.

Look of skin: Inflamed with bleb formation. There is vesicular erysipelas with vesicles all over the
body which are sore and suppurating.

Pain: There is severe burning pain with itching and smarting.

Modalities: Worse from touch and relieved by cold application (Boericke). Cantharides can be used
externally also in burns before the blisters form and also when they have already formed. If the skin is
unbroken, Cantharides will promptly relieve pain and often prevent vesication.

General Modalities :
Aggravation : From touch, urinating, drinking cold water or coffee.
Amelioration: By rubbing.

Relation :
Antidotes: Aconite, Camphor and Pulsatilla.
