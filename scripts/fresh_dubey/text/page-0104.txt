Arum Triphyllum 95

PARTICULARS

Mouth : Mouth painfully raw, sore and inflamed. Corners of mouth cracked and bleeding. There is
profuse acrid secretion of saliva corroding the mucous menbranes of mouth. Patients pick and bore into
raw bleeding surface though very painful. He screams with pain but keeps up the boring, This is key note
symptom in diphtheria, scarlatina & typhoid. Tongue cracked and bleeding. Children refuse food and
drink on account of soreness of mouth and throat.

Nose : Arum Triphyllum is one of the important remedies in acute- cold when the discharge is acrid
and fluent. Stuffy nose inspite of the watery discharge, patient must breathe through the mouth. Sneezing
worse at night. Constant picking at the nose until it bleeds. Boring with the finger into the side of the
nose is another characteristic of this remedy.

Throat : Throat is raw, sore, painful on clearing or coughing. Patient cannot swallow. It is medicine
for cleargyman’s sore throat or sore throat on one who speaks a great deal, orators, singers, actors. Voice
is hoarse, uncertain, uncontrollable, chang'rg continually. Complete aphonia after exposure to north west

winds and from singing.
Modality: Aggravation : Talking, speaking, or singing.

General Modalities :
Aggravation : Overuse of voice, talking, singing, northwest wind, heat, lying down, at night.

Relations :
Useful after—Hep, & Nit. Acid in hoarse, croupy cough. After—Caust. & Hep. in morming
hoarseness & deafness and in scarlatina.
