SABADILLA

“Covadilla Liliaceae

INTRODUCTION

Sabadilla first appears in homoeopathic
literature as one of Stapf's additions, The drug
was first proved by Samuel Hahnemann. Dr.
N.M. Choudhury has justified in calling this
remedy as a great sneezing remedy of our
Materia Medica.

CLINICAL

Coryza. Diphtheria. Hay asthma. Headache.
Influenza. Intermittents. Throat-sore, Tonsillitis,
Worms.

SPHERES OF ACTION

It affects the mucous membrane of respiratory tract, lachrymal glands and nerves.

Acting on mucous membrane of the nose & the lachrymal glands, it produces coryza
& symptoms like hay—fever.

CONSTITUTION

Physical make up : It is suited to persons of light hair, fair complexion with a weak,
relaxed muscular system.

Relation with heat & cold ; Chilly patient.
Miasm : It is an anti-psoric remedy.

GUIDING SYMPTOMS
1) Sneezing in spasmodic paroxysms, followed by Lachrymation.

2) Copious watery Coryza with hot flush and red and burning eye lids.
3) Twitching, Convulsion, tremblings and catalepsy, from worms (Cina, Psor.)
4) Illusion :
(i) that he is sick.
(ii) that she is pregnant when only disten-ded with flatus,
(iii) that parts are shrunken,
(iv) that she has cancer in throat.
