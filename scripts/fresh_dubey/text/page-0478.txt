PARTICULARS

Headache :

Causation: Carriage riding.
Location: In occiput.
Sensation: Heaviness in the head, pressing and pulsating pain in the head. Sensation as if head

is made up of wood.

Character: Scalp is sore to touch, followed by numbness, throbbing and bursting headache.
Modality: Aggravation during a thunderstorm, carriage riding (Cocculus).
Concomitants: With headache there is vertigo on rising, in occiput, like sea sickness.

Gastro-intestinal symptoms :

Stomach : Gastralgia of pregnancy, with heartburn and sour eructation.
Nausea with accumulation of water in the mouth.
Character of pain; Pressing and drawing pain in stomach.
Modalities:
¢ Aggravation: Whenever the stomach is empty.
° Amelioration: By constant eating (Anac., Sepia, lod., Chel.).
