CALENDULA OFFICINALIS

Marigold Compositae

INTRODUCTION

Calendula is our favourite marigold. The
mother tincture is prepared from its leaves and
flowers, together with blooms and buds. IT IS A
GREAT HOMOEOPATHIC ANTISEPTIC. It
restores vitality of an injured part. Unlike
Arnica, it has no irritating property and
consequently not capable of producing erysipelas.
It is, therefore, suitable to all cases of injuries
where the skin is broken. It prevents suppuration
and brings about healing promptly. It can also be
used externally with wonderful results.
Calendula is a great healing agent in our Materia
Medica and it ranks first in our Homoeopathic surgical and first aid box for sueh

conditions.

CLINICAL

Abscess, Burns, Carbuncles, Chillblains, Eye inflammation, Glandular affection,
Injuries, Suppuration, Ulcers, Wounds, ete.

SPHERES OF ACTION
It acts on muscles, tendons, nerves, scalp, skin, ete.

PATHOGENESIS

Its action on the muscles, tendons, nerves, etc. produces a condition similar to
lacerated injuries with solution of continuity of skin with suppuration.

GUIDING SYMPTOMS

1. Calendula is almost specific for clean cut, surgical cuts or lacerated wounds or
suppurating wounds, It prevents suppuration, promotes healthy granulation of tissues
and rapid healing by first intention.

2.Pain is excessive and out of all proportion to injury due to probable nerve
involvement.

3. Constitutional tendency to erysipelas.
4, It can be used in the case of cancer as an intercurrent remedy.
5. It is an excellent haemostatic in tooth extraction (Bov., Ham., Phos).

6. Ailments from : Loss of blood and post surgical injury.
