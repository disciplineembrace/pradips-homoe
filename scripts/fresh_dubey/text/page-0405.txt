MAGNESIA PHOSPHORICA

Phosphate of HAHNEMANN MgHPO,7H,0
Magnesium

INTRODUCTION

Phosphate of magnesium is one of the most
important of the twelve tissue remedies. It is a
precious gem derived from biochemical studies of
Dr. Schuessler the father of Twelve Tissue
Remedies. This salt is one of the important
constituents of various cells in our body,
especially blood corpuscles, muscles, brain, spinal
marrow and various other tissues. Disturbance of
its molecules results in pain, cramps and

paralysis. In Homoeopathy it was MASTER
HAHNEMANN who first used it in the treatment

of dysmenorrhoea. He remarked that “Mag. phos.” is one of the greatest, if not the
greatest of the remedies for dysmenorrhoea, but only one of its own kind. N.M.
CHOUDHURY also says that a careful study of Mag. phos. will convince every student
of Homoeopathy, the utter need of discrimination, careful cool judgment and a constant
burning of incense before the Goddess of Materia Medica.
CLINICAL

Colic, Convulsion, Cough, Dentition, Headache, Intercostal neuralgia, Locomotor
ataxia, Menstrual disorders, Neuralgia, Toothache, Whooping cough, etc.
SPHERES OF ACTION

Especially it acts upon brain, nerves, muscles, intestine and uterus.

PATHOGENESIS

It produces neuralgic pain in the head, teeth, etc. which is sharp, cutting, lightning
like, shooting and almost unbearable. It also produces spasmodic pain, especially in the
abdomen.

CONSTITUTION

Physical make up: Mag. phos. is especially adapted to the affection of thin, emaciated
persons of a highly nervous organisation and of dark complexion. Jt affects, right side of the
body. Hering says that it is suited to young and very strong persons and teething children.

Temperament: Nervous temperament.
Relation with heat and cold; Chilly patient.
Miasm: Psora is in the background.
