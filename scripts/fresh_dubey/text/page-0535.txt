Snow Rose SEIDEL Ericaceae

INTRODUCTION

Rhododendron is an evergreen shrub found
growing largely on the mountain tops of Siberia
and is locally named as snow rose. For the
Homoeopathic purpose, the tincture is prepared
from the dried leaves of this plant. This remedy
deserves a place in our Materia Medica by reason
of its extensive use in RHEUMATIC AND
GOUTY COMPLAINTS. In support of the
doctrine of signature, CLARKE SAYS, “Growing
among the fogs and storms of the Siberian
mountains, its provings show that it produces,
sensitiveness to storm and weather changes, and
this gives the grand key-note for it in our Materia Medica”.

CLINICAL

Amenorrhoea, Bone affection, Bunions, Ciliary Neuralgia, Earache, Eye affections,
Flatulence, Lumbago, Neuralgia, Orchitis, Rheumatism, Toothache, etc.
SPHERES OF ACTION

It has a limited sphere of action on fibrous tissues particularly on big joints and the
vertebral column. Serous membrane, pleura and synovial membranes also come under its
action.

PATHOGENESIS

It acts as an irritant and produces excessive secretion from the synovial membranes
in the joint and causing all the signs and symptoms of inflammation, Fibrous tissues get
inflamed producing rheumatic and gouty changes, Pleurisy results when the pleura gets
affected but usually of the wet type.

CONSTITUTION

Temperament : Nervous patients who DREAD A STORM.

Relation with heat and cold: Very chilly patient.

Miasm: Psora is in the background,

Diathesis: Rheumatic and gouty diathesis,

GUIDING SYMPTOMS
1. Dread of a storm is the key-note of this remedy.
