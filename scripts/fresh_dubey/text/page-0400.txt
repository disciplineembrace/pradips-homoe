MAGNESIA CARBONICA

Carbonate of HAHNEMANN (MgCO,),Mg
Magnesium (OH), + SH,O
INTRODUCTION

Magnesia carbonica was used in ancient days
in the form of Gregory's Powder, other
constituents being Rhubarb and Ginger. This
powder was used, to sweeten the stomach; hence
occupied a position of much repute in the
Allopathic Nursery. The properties of Mag. carb.,
from the old school’s stand point are summed up
as “Laxative, antacid and antilithic’. In
Homoeopathy we use it with great advantage for
complaints arising in people who have been
taking the drug to “sweeten the stomach”.

CLINICAL

Acidity, Catarrh, Constipation, Cough, Diarrhoea, Dyspepsia, Flatulence, Menstrual
disorders, Neuralgia, Pregnancy—nausea of, Toothache, etc.
SPHERES OF ACTION

It acts especially on the gastro-intestinal system, liver and also female genital
organs. It also has got action over the nerve, especially the facial and dental nerves.
PATHOGENESIS

It produces gastro-intestinal catarrh with marked hyper acidity and sour diarrhoea.
It also causes nervous exhaustion and sleeplessness.
CONSTITUTION

Physical make up: It is especially suited to women having broken down or worn-out
constitution with uterine and climacteric disorders and with lax fibres and sour smell of
the whole body.

Temperament: Nervous temperament.
Relation with heat and cold: Very much sensitive to cold air.
Miasm: Psora is in the background.

GUIDING SYMPTOMS

1. Aggravation of all the symptoms, especially pains from rest and relieved by walking
about.
