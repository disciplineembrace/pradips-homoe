PARTICULARS

Head

Persistent frontal headache. Feeling of lightness in brain. Eyeballs prominent. Fullness & heat in head
with flushed face.

Eyes
Prominent Eyeballs, Progressive diminution of sight with central scotoma.

Stomach

Desire for sweets and thirst. for cold water. Nausea worse riding in car. Flatulence, much flatus in
abdomen.

Respiratory symptoms
Dry, painful cough with scanty, difficult expectoration and burning In pharynx.
Urinary symptoms

Polyuria with increased flow, some albumen and sugar. Urine smells of violets, burning along urethra,
increase of uric acid. Enuresis in weakly children who are nervous and irritable.

Heart

Weak, frequent pulse, with inability to lie down. Tachycardia. Palpitation from least exertion. Ready
excitability of heart. Severe heart pain.
