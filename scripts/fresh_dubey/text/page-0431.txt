Musk Deer HAHNEMANN Mammalia

INTRODUCTION

It is potentised from the musk which is the
fatty, waxy and gelatinous substance with a very
strong smell and is found in a sack between the
umbilicus and the pre-putial orifice of the mask
deer.

CLINICAL
Hysteria. Hystero-epilepsy.

CONSTITUTION

It is suited to nervous, sensitive and
hysterical persons. Chilly patient; antipsoric
medicine.

GUIDING SYMPTOMS

It is one of the valuable hysterical remedies useful in cases of hystero-epilepsy,
convulsion, globus hystericus etc. where the symptoms are—

(i) Fainting : frequent tendency to fainting from least excitement, while eating,
during menses,
(ii) Sensation of coldness and shivering,

(iii) Tension in muscles, in skin and mind is another keynote of Moschus.
(iv) Spasm of the larynx and spasm of the chest may be present.
(vy) Great flatulence, craving for beer, stimulants and aversion to food.

PARTICULARS

Mind : Uncontrollable laughter. Scolding. Sexual hypo-chondriasis. Anxiety with palpitation ;
starting as if frightened.

Stomach : Spasmodic, nervous hiccough (Ign., Sulph. ac.) Anxiety in chest with gastric symptoms.
Distension of abdomen. Faints when eating. Desire for black coffee, Stimulants. Aversion to food.

Sexual symptoms :

Male : Sexual hypochondriasis. Violent desire ; irivolun-tory emissions. Impotence, associated with
diabetes. Premature senility. Nausea and vomiting after coltion.

Female : Menses too early, too profuse with disposition to faint. Sexual desire, with intolerable
titilation in parts. Drawing and pushing in the direction of the genitals ; Sensation as if menses appear,
