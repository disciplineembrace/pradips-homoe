Teeth :
There is toothache, with sensation as if the teeth are elongated.
Aggravation: By cold air, smoking tobacco, holding cold drinks in mouth.
Amelioration: In the night and by warm drinks.

Meningitis :
Causation; From injuries to head, chronic brain effects to blows, falls and the like.
Location : Basilar meninges.
Sensation: As if the head was drawn backwards.

Character of pain: Violent, crushing, gnawing pain at base of brain, tenderness of the spine.
Sore pain in small of back and Sacrum.

Patient cannot lie on either side.
Along with this there is spasm and mental irritability and delirium.
Violent congestion as if all the blood has rushed to the head.
Modalities:

* Aggravation: At night, when lying down or during rest.

* Amelioration: By pressure, changing position and by sitting,

Gonorrhoea :

Natrum sulph. is an excellent remedy in neglected cases of gonorrhoea when the discharges are
thick, yellowish and green, painless, chronic or suppressed.

There is burning in urethra during and after urination. With itching of scrotum which burns after
scratching.
General Modalities :

Aggravation: Damp basement, damp weather, rest, lying down and music.

Amelioration: Dry weather, pressure, changing position.

Relations :

constitution.
