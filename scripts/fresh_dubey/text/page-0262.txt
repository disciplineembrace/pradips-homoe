CUPRUM METALLICUM

“Metallic Copper HAHNEMANN Cu The Element

INTRODUCTION

Metallic copper, an insoluble substance when
it is pure, is made soluble by means of
trituration. It was Master. Hahnemann who
solved this problem of using insoluble substance,
in the field of therapeutics. This medicine was
first introduced by him as one of the weapons for
the then prevailing cholera epidemic. By the
members of the orthodox school, copper is used as
copper sulphate for therapeutic purposes,
especially for external application. They did not
dare to use it internally because of its chemically
poisonous effect, but Homoeopathy has made it
useful through the process of trituration.

CLINICAL

Angina pectoris, Asthma, Cholera, Chorea, Convulsion, Cough, Cramps, Croup,
Cyanosis, Dyspnoea, Epilepsy Gastric disorders, Gout, Throat affection, Measles,
Meningitis, Neuralgia, Paralysis, Spasm, Whooping cough, etc.

SPHERES OF ACTION
Cuprum met. acts prominently upon the alimentary canal and profoundly influences
the nervous system and various nerve centres.

PATHOGENESIS

Cuprum met. causes gastro-intestinal inflammation, colic, diarrhoea and vomiting.
Acting on the nerve centres it gives rise to SPASMODIC AFFECTIONS CRAMPS,

paralysis, convulsions, beginning in fingers and toes; violent contractive and intermitting
pain with tonic and clonic spasm etc.

CONSTITUTION

Physical make-up: “It is suited to fair haired people and the carbo-nitrogenoid
constitution, women who have borne many children” (Clarke).

Relation with heat and cold: Symptoms are aggravated from cold air.

Miasm: It is an anti-psoric remedy WORKING FROM WITHIN OUTWARDS.
