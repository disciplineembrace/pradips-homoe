NITRIC ACID

“Nitric Acid HAHNEMANN HNO,

INTRODUCTION

Of the trio of powerful acids, viz., Sulphuric acid (H,SO,)
Hydrochloric acid (HCL) and Nitric acid (HNOg), the last one leads
the list in severity and its corrosive effect. But when it is
potentised, it becomes an excellent remedy. It is a constitutional
remedy, suitable for both chronic and acute diseases. All the three
acids mentioned above are dangerous in their crude form, but when
potentised, they posess very useful therapeutic value. Nitric acid is
a chemical product manufactured synthetically and has a wide
range of commercial use.

SPHERES OF ACTION

Nitric acid acts on blood, skin, mucous membrane glands, bones and gastro-intestinal
tract.

PATHOGENESIS

The special seat of ation of Nitric acid is at the junction of skin and mucous
membranes of the body, such as mouth, eyes, nose, rectum, anus, vagina, urethra, etc. It
disorganises the blood causing decomposition; it produces intense irritation, resulting in
inflammation, destruction, ulceration and even gangrene of the parts. Its action on
gastro-intestinal tract sets in diarrhoea, haemorrhage, etc.

CONSTITUTION

Make-up of the body: Nitric acid is especially suited to thin, weak and debilitated
Persons with rigid musculature, dark complexion, black hair and eyes. Ugly looking
appearance; with broken down cachectic constitution; anaemic and emaciated; old people
with morning diarrhoea (Phos.).

Temperament: Nervous and irritable temperament.
Relation with heat and cold: Chilly patient.
