PARTICULARS

Respiratory Symptoms :

Antim Ars. has been found very useful in emphysema (Sienega, Amm. Garb) with excessive
dyspnoea and cough cough with much mucus secretion and is aggravated by eating and lying down. It
preferably affects upper left lung Dr. N.M. Chowdhury says, “After pneumonia and pleurisy when
all the acute symptoms have subsided and only dyspnoea remains a few doses of Antim Ars.
will be very useful.”
