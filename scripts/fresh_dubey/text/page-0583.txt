PARTICULARS

Eyes :
Location: Usually left eye IS AFFECTED.
Sensation: Eyes feel too large for the orbit.

Character of pain: Violent pain about the eyes, pressive pain on turning them, pupils dilated with
severe pain in and around the eyes. Eyeballs sensitive to touch, sharp, stabbing and sticking pain through
the eyeballs.

Modalities: Worse from touch, motion, noise turning the eyes, from cold, damp and rainy
weather.
Neuralgia :

Of trigeminal nerve.

Location: Left side, e.g., left orbit, left eye, left malar bone, left sided teeth.

Character of pain :; Shooting, burning and tearing type of neuralgic pain.

Periodic type of prosopalgia with stabbing pain running into the back of the head.

Modalities: Aggravation: From morning till sun-set, motion, noise and COLD DAMP RAINY
WEATHER.

Relieved: By pressure.

Toothache :

Causation: Toothache from tobacco smoking.

Character of pain: Sharp, stabbing, sticking pain, tearing toothache, very much sensitive to touch.

Modalities: Aggravation from cold air, water, when thinking of it and relieved BY EATING OR
LYING DOWN.

Headache
Causation: Abuse of tobacco.
