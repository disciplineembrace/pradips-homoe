Typhus (Apoc., Bapt., Crot., H., Lack., Nit. A., Pyr., Sel.) An acute infectious and contagious
disease, caused by RICKETTSIA, marked by high fever, great mental and physical
depression and a macular and papular eruption. It lasts for about two weeks.

U

Umbilical hernia (Cocc., Plumb.) One in which bowel or omentum protrudes through the
abdominal wall under the skin at the umbilicus.

Union by first intention (Calend.) The immediate healing of a wound without suppuration
or the formation of granulations.

Unmerited insults (Staph.) Undeserved insults.
Unsightly scars (Kali Br.) Ugly scars, displeasing to eye.
Unwieldy (Cal. c.) Not easily moved.

Uvula (Phyt.) The central, tag-like structure hanging down from the free edge of the soft
palate.

V

Vaginismus (Mag. P.) Painful spasm of the vagina preventing satisfactory coitus.

Varicose ulcers (Calen, Ham. V.) Ulcers of indolent type which occurs in the lower third of
a leg afflicted with varicose veins.

Vegetable analogue (Nat. M., Phos.,) Parallel or similar to the medicine prepared from
vegetable kingdom.

Vehement (Bry.) Showing strong feeling, impetuous, ardent, passionate.

Veil (Tab.) Curtain.

Venerated (Plat.) Respected.

Venous plethora (Carb. A.) Congestion causing distention of veins.

Verses (Ant. Cr.) Poems.

Vexation (Apis. M., Ars. A, Aur. M., Caust., Plat., Petr.) State of irritation or distress. A state
of being annoyed or worried.

Vicarious menstruation (Bry., Crot. H., Phos.) Blood loss during menstruation at some site
other than the vagina as haemorrhage from nose, breast or eyes ete.
