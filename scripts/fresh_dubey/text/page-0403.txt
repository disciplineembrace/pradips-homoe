MAGNESIA MURIATICA

Chloride of Magnesia HAHNEMANN

Mg C.L. 2

INTRODUCTION

Magnesia Muriatica is the most neglected liver
remedy in our Materia Medica. It is particularly a
woman’s remedy who suffers from hysterical
complaints with uterine diseases and indigestion.

Cardiailgia, Constipation, Dyspepsia.
Diarrhoea, Dysmenorrhoea, Headache. Hysteria.
Leucorrhoea. Liver complaints. Toothache.

SPHERE OF ACTION

Gastro intestinal tract, liver, nerves, uterus,
heart, urinary bladder.

CONSTITUTION

Physical make up—It is especially suited to hysterical nervous women who suffer
from hepatic and uterine disorders. Puny, rachitic children during dentition, Relation

with heat & cold—chilly patient. Miasm—Psora.
GUIDING SYMPTOMS

1) Patient, though chilly, desires fresh open air which relieves.
2) Great sensitivenes to noise (Phos., Theridion., Nux Vom.)
3) Affections of the right side with liver affections.

4) Stool dry, knotty, difficult to evacuate, like sheep dung crumbling at verge of

anus (Ammon Mur., Nat.Mur.)

5) Great tendency to head-sweat. (Cale. Sil., Sani.)

6) Pressing pain in enlarged liver, pain radiates to spine. Aggravation by lying on

right side, better by hard pres-sure.

7) Profuse dark pitch like menses with cramps, backache, pain in thighs and

indigestion.

8) Leucorrhoea after every stool, two weeks after menses,

9) Craving for sugar.
10) Intolerance of milk.
11) Aggravation while sitting.

12) Palpitation relieved by motion or lying on left side.
