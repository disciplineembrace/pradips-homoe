Fever :

The drug can be thought of in any kind of pyrexiai conditions, particularly when the exciting factor is
expousure to damp wet weather, drenchihg in rain, etc.

* Causation: Due to cold air, damp weather, rainy weather.
* Mode of onset; Onset is very slow.

* During fever: Dulcamara causes profuse secretion from mucus, membranes of the body. Due
to this action, there is profuse ness of discharges, there is also profuse catarrhal discharge from nose,
which is thick and yelbwish. There is dry burning heat all over the body with chilliness towards the evening
arid with thirst. There is dull headache during fever with catarrhal discharge from the nose.

With fever, there is loose cough and profuse yellowish watery stool.
* Modalities: Aggravation from cold, damp rainy weather or sudden change in warm weather.
Amelioration in warm room.

General Modalities :
Aggravation: From cold in general, damp rainy weather, cold air, suppressed eruption and sweat.
Amelioration: Better by moving about and from warmth dry weather, warm air and walking.
