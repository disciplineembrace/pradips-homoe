PARTICULARS

Urinary complaints

It has got marked action on urinary system. It is to be thought of in nocturnal enuresis of delicate
children, enlargement of prostate in old men, catarrh of blad-der after suppressed gonorrhoea and even
in any morbid condition, when urine is strong smelling.

Character of Urine—Urine is dark, brown and scanty with urinous odour highly
intensified. There is excess of uric acid in urine.

Rheumatism & gout
Causation—Suppressed gonorrhoea & syphillis.
Location—All joints are affected ‘but especially knee symptoms may go from left to right.

Character of pain—Pains tearing, stitching in large joints especially of big toe. Pains suddenly
change their locality. There is gouty concretions. Nodositis in joints, due to depos-ition of uric acid,
cracking sound in joints. (Coc., Graph., Nit. acid)

Modality—Agg.—Open air, uncovering at night, motion. Ame.—By heat.
Concomitant—Very strong smelling offensive urine and generally dark. Rheumatism alternating
between the extremeties & heart.

General Modalities
Aggravation—Motion, open air, uncovering at night, lying on right side.

Relations

Similar to—Cop., Nitr., Fer., Thuja especially in enuresis after Nitr. has failed. Berb Vulg., Lith C.
in arthritic complaints. Think of Benz Acid when Colch. fails in gout and after abuse of Cop. in

Suppression of gonorrhoea.
Incompatible—Wine which aggravates urinary, gouty and rheumatic affections.
In Enuresis Benz. ac. can be compared with the following drugs:
(1) In wormy children : Cina
(2) After first sleep : Caust, Kreos,, Sep.
(3) After Catheterisatiori : Mag. Phos.
(4) In old people : Secale Cor.
(5) When the best selected remedy fails, with a history of sycosis : Medo.
(6) Obstinate cases with a family history of eczema : Psor.
