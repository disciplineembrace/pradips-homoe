Calendula Officinalis 165

PARTICULARS

Injury :
Causation: Clean cut injury, amputation, surgical operation, lacerated wounds, during labour,
extraction of teeth, fracture of bones, etc.

Character of injury: Calendula it used in all cases of loss of soft parts, when union cannot be
effected by means of adhesive plaster. Rupture of muscles or tendons, laceration and perineal tear during
labour; wounds penetrating articulations with loss of synovial fluids.

External wounds with or without loss of substance; torn and jagged looking wounds; post surgical
operation; to promote healthy granulation and prevent excessive suppuration and disfiguring scars.

Exhausted from loss of blood (China, Ham.) and excessive pain. Traumatic and idiopathic neuroma;
neuritis from lacerated wounds (Hypericum), after amputation (Cepa, Calc. phos.). Bleeding from
extraction of teeth (Am., Bov., Ham. and Phos).

For old neglected, offensive injuries, threatening to a gangrenous condition, Calendula is ideally
suited.
Ulcers :

Irritable, inflamed, sloughing and excessive secretion of pus; painful as if beaten (Arn.), ulcers of
varicose vein. Calendula has remarkable power to produce local exudation and helps acrid discharge and

makes ulcers healthy and clean. It has good reputation for external application in any cut or open wound
surface or in any. ulcer. Calendula mother tincture, lotion or liniment can be applied externally.

General Modalities :
Worse in damp cloudy weather.

Relations :
Antidoted by: Amica.
Calendula is complementary to: Hepar sulph.
Follows well: Ars. alb.
Followed well by: Arn. and Hepar.

N.B. : Hot calendula lotions are generally preferable to a cold one. Hot calendula fermentations are
better than poultices in the case of abscesses.
