INTRODUCTION

It is a plant found in Europe. The fresh
bulb is taken for preparation of mother
tincture. It has received the name
Colchicum autumnale because it blooms
during autumn and usually the symptoms
are also aggravated during autumn. So it
is a useful remedy for the autumnal
dysentery. It is also an indispensable
remedy for gout and rheumatism.
Dr. Hughes says that this medicine has a
diuretic action and it increases elimination
of uric acid of by the kidneys. Gout is a
metabolic disease pertaining to defects in purine metabolism. It explains its usefulness
in cases of gout. The Allopathic school has used and abused this drug for any gouty

condition, says Tyler.

SPHERES OF ACTION
Colchicum acts on disgestive tract, heart, pericardium, kidneys and joints.

This agent irritates the digestive organs and produces nausea, vomiting and loss of
appetite, colic, diarrhoea and painful cramps. It also produces inflammation of intestine,
leading to sloughing which ultimately passes out of scrapping of intestine in stool with
blood and mucous. It also produces inflammation of joints with sensitiveness to touch.
