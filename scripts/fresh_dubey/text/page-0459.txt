NUX MOSCHATA

Nutmeg HELBIG

Myristicaceae

INTRODUCTION

Nux moschata is a very useful and effective
remedy derived from the vegetable kingdom. The
mother tincture is prepared from the pulverised
dry kernel invested in the shell. It was
introduced into Materia Medica by Helbig. The
tree is a native of East and West Indies and
grows well in this equatorial region. The tree
branches freely and is of medium height.
Although it is suitable for all kinds and classes of
people, it is particularly adaptable to children,
old people and women during pregnancy.

CLINICAL

Abdominal disorders, Abortion, Apoplexy, Asthma, Cough, Debility, Deltoid
rheumatism, Eructation, Flatulence, Gastralgia, Haemorrhage, Headache, Heart

troubles, Menstrual disorders, Hysteria, Rheumatism, etc.

SPHERES OF ACTION

The main seats of action of this drug are mind, nervous system, skin and mucous
membrane, gastro-intestinal tract, respiratory organs, respiratory tract, uterus and

ovaries.

PATHOGENESIS
It acts on.

(a) Nervous system and mind producing stupor, insensibility and unconquerable

sleep; weakness of memory and changeable mood.
(b) On skin and mucous membrane, causing great dryness.

(c) On gastro-intestinal tract : resulting in great distension of abdomen and

diarrhoea and other troubles,

(d) On respiratory tract, setting in cough and hoarseness. In addition to rheumatic
affections, menstrual disorders, etc. are also within its domain of action.

CONSTITUTION

Make-up of the body: It is suited especially to women, and children, with dry skin who
rarely perspire, weak old people who are delicate, sensitive and dyspeptic having

hydrogenoid constitution (Clarke).
