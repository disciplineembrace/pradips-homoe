* Cramps; With cholera there are cramps which commence in hands and feet and spread all over

¢ Look of the face : The face becomes BLUE, PALE, FEATURES SUNKEN, EYES, SUNKEN,

* Collapse: Due to profuse diarrhoea and vomiting, there is collapsed condition. The body is cold
to touch with corresponding blueness, fairly purple lips blue and cold. Sensation of a lump of ice on
vertex, extremities are cold as if dead; patient looks AS IF DYING WITH RAPID SINKING OF THE
VITAL FORCE.

* Sweat: During cholera, the sweat is well marked. COLD AND CLAMMY SWEAT ALL OVER
THE BODY, ESPECIALLY UPON FOREHEAD,

* Desire: During cholera there is intense desire for large quantity of very cold water, refreshing
things and acids.

¢ After cholera: complete prostration and excessive weakness.
* Modality: Aggravation after eating and drinking and by least motion.

* Concomitants: Before and during menses, there is cholera like symptoms (Ammon. carb.
Bovista).

In short—extreme coldness, blueness, weakness arid excessiveness of all the discharges, make one
to think of Veratrum album, in cholera.

Veratum album differs from CAMPHOR in cholera. There is “DRY CHOLERA” with coldness,
blueness, scanty sweat, vomiting and purging in Camphor. In camphor, the patient sinks down and
dies without exhaustive discharges. Scanty discharges with coldness, blueness and collaspe indicate
Camphor, whereas as a contrast, COPIOUS, PROFUSENESS, BLUENESS AND COLDNESS ARE
PRSENT IN VERATRUM ALBUM. Thus Veratrum album and Camphor can be easily differentiated.

Menses :
Veratum is a good drug for menstrual toubles, if the following symptoms are present :-
Time: Too early.
Quantity: Too profuse.

Character: Very much exhausting, makes the patient so weak, that she can scarcely stand for two
days at each menstrual nisus.

Pain: Dysmenorrhoea, with cutting pain in abdomen. Faints from least exertion.

With menses, there is COLD SWEAT ON FOREHEAD WITH COLLAPSED CONDITION AND
GREAT WEAKNESS.

Concomitants: Cholera like symptoms before and during menses.

Rheumatism :
Causation: From warmth of bed and during wet weather.

Character: Neuralgia-like pain in the extremities. Severe pain makes the patient jump up from the
bed, especially at night, pains like electric flashes.

Modalities: Aggravation from warmth of bed and during wet weather.

Amelioration: By continued motion (Rhus tox.) but continued motionproduces heat and this heat
causes further aggravation.
