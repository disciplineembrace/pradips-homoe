Diarrhoea :
* Causation: Fatty food, fruits, cold drinks, ice-cream, etc.
* Time: Only or usually at night (China).

* Character of stool: Watery stool, sometimes unconsciously evacuated. Greenish, yellow and
slimy. NO TWO STOOLS ARE ALIKE, very much changeable.

* Concomitants: Dry tongue with no thirst.
* Modality: Aggravation: At night, by fatty food, ice-cream, tea, etc.
