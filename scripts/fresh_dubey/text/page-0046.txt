ALOE SOCOTRINA

Socotrine Aloes HELBIG Liliaceae

INTRODUCTION

Aloe socotrina is an excellent remedy like
Nux vomica in re-establishing physiological
equilibrium after much drugging where disease
and drug symptoms are mixed to form a common
pool. For this wonderful medicine, Dr. N.M.
Choudhury has masterly remarked that “To
write a Materia Medica without Aloe is to write a
novel without a hero.”

Its importance and usefulness become
evident as soon as we launch in our business of
curing the sick.

CLINICAL

Abdominal disorders, Anal affection, Colic,
Constipation, cough, Diarrhoea, Dysentery, Haemorrhoids, Skin affections, ete.

SPHERES OF ACTION

It acts especially upon the nervous system, portal system, blood and skin. It has got
special action upon the liver and intestine.

PATHOGENESIS

When acting on mucous membrane of gastrointestinal tract, it produces diarrhoea,
dysentery and haemorrhage from the haemorrhoidal vessels. It affects the intestine
especially rectum producing loss of control of sphincter ani. It also causes congestion of
various parts, especially abdomen, pelvic organs and head.

CONSTITUTION

Physical make-up: Especially suited to old people, women of relaxed, phlegmatic
habit. Indolent weary persons leading sedentary life, averse to either physical and
mental labour.

Temperament: Bilious temperament.
Relation with heat and cold: Hot patient.
Miasm: Psora is in the background.

GUIDING SYMPTOMS :

1. Profuse jelly like of mucus from all mucous membranes, especially of intestine and
throat (Kali bi., Podo., Helleborus).
