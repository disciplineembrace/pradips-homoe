Fever: Fever with besotted countenance. Face red and bloated with half closed eyes,
There may be low temperature or of 105-106°F. but tells he is not sick. Wants nothing.

Pulse :Full and slow.

Thirst :Unquenchable.

Heat : All over the body. Hot perspiration.

Delirium : Constantly talking. Patient thinks he is not at home.

Bed feels so hot she cannot lie on it. Moves often in search of a cool place. Wants to
be uncovered.

Concomitant: Fever characterised by stupor, snoring, respiration, twitching of
limbs and sleepiness.

General Modalities :

Aggravation : During and after sleep (Apis, Lach.) ; while perspiring ; from warmth;
; stimulants.

Amelioration : From cold ; constant walking.

Relations :

Antidotes, for poisonous doses : strong coffee, Nux, Ipe, Passi, Ber and constant
motion.

When symptoms correspond, the potencies may antidote bad effects of Opium
drugging.
Compare : Apis, Bell., Gels, Nux Mos, Morph.
