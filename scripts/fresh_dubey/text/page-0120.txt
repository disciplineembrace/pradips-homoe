BARYTA MURIATICA

Chloride of Barium HAHNEMANN

The symptoms of Baryta Mur are like those of the other
Barium Salts especially are very similar to Baryta Carb but
it has a distinct character of its own. It is a deep acting
constitutional antipsoric medicine.

Aneurism. Asthma. Convulsions. Earache. Glan-dular
affections. Idiocy, Mania. Nymphomania. Pancreatitis.
Paralysis. Hypertension. Tinnitus. Tonsillitis. Narrowing of
cardiac orifice. Sclerosis of brain and spinal cord.

SPHERE OF ACTION & PATHOGENESIS

It has strong action on heart and arteries. It acts on
glands especially parotid and pancreas, It also acts on lower
alimentary canal especially rectum, on muscles and joints
giving stiffness and weakness as from overwalking. The
white blood Corpuscles are increased.

CONSTITUTION

Make up of the body—It is suited to dwarfish children both mentally and physically
and to the aged. Relation with heat & cold—Chilly patient. Temperamenit—trritable

temperament.

Miasm—Chiefly Antipsoric but other miasms Sycotic & Syphilitic are also in the

background.
Diathesis—Scrofulous diathesis.

1) Disposition to enlargement of glands and recurrent tonsillitis from exposure to cold.
2) Convulsions with consciousness with electric shocks. (Nux Vom., Stram.)
3) Induration and narrowing of the cardiac orifice with pain immediately after

eating.

4) Arteriosclerosis when there is high systolic pressure with comparatively low

diastolic tension.
5) In every form of mania, when sexual desire is increased.

6) Loss of voluntary muscular power but sensibility intact.

7) Profuse flow of saliva and offensive odour from mouth.
