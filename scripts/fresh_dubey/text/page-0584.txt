Spigelia 575

Location: Left sided headache, starts at the base of brain, spreading over the head and finally
jocating in in left eye, orbit and temple of left side.

Sensation: As if a band is around the head.

Character of pain ; Violent pain in left parietal bone. Violent throbbing and pulsating type of pain.
There is sharp, stabbing and sticking pain through the eyeball back into the head. The head is much
sensitive to touch.

* Aggravation : Headache starts at sunrise reaches its height at noon, declines by the
sunset; aggravates from motion, noise and touch.

¢ Amelioration: by lying on right side with head high.

Heart

Rheumatic affections of the heart. Painful complaints associated with valvular troubles, especially
growing out of rheumatic pericarditis and endocarditis of rheumatic character.

lrregularity of heart beat with violent palpitation.
Visible, audible, violent and roaring heart with systolic blowing at apex.
There, is stitching needle-like pain in chest, synchronous with pulse. Pulse is weak and irregular.
There, is great dyspnoea with pain in chest, must lie on right side with head high.
Precordia region is very much sensitive to touch.
There is thrusting pain in the chest like knife.
Modalities:
* Aggravation: From motion, touch and from colt weather.
¢ Amelioration: By lying on right side with head high.

General Modalities
Aggravation: From motion, noise, touch, turning the eyes and from every shaking.
Amelioration: Lying on right side with head high and in spring.

Relations
Antidotes: Merc.
Antidoted by: camp., Puls.
