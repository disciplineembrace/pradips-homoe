TARENTULA HISPANIA

Spanish Spider NUNEZ Aranedacae

INTRODUCTION

Tarentula is a deep and long acting medicine.
This spider is very poisonous in nature. Those
who are bitten by this spider or those who
imagine them-selves to have been bitten by it
develop “Tarantism” which means dancing
mania.

CLINICAL

Chorea. Epilepsey. Hysteria. Paralysis
agitans. Nymphomania. Multiple sclerosis.
Pruritus vul-vae. Tabes dorsalis. etc.

SPHERES OF ACTION

Many symptoms of body are confined to right side. Its main action is over the
nervous system espe-cially the termini of nerves, genito-urinary system.

GUIDING SYMPTOMS
1) Restlessness : Cannot keep quiet in any position, must keep in motion though
walking aggravates all symptoms.
2) Constant movement of the legs, arms, trunk with inability to do anything.

3) Hyperaesthesia—Least excitement irritates followed by languid sadness, especially

of tips of finger.
4) Very sensitive to music. It relieves nearly all symptoms but sometimes it
excites violently.

5) Craves raw food ; aversion to food & meat.

6) Thirst for cold water.

7) Extreme sexual desire leading to mania.

8) Annual Periodicity of symptoms is marked. (Ars, Garb. Veg, Lach, Siilph, Thuja)

9) At every menstrual period tongue, mouth & throat get intolerably dry especially
while sleeping.

10) Cough only relieved from smoking.

Mind : Anxiety of mind and body. Cardiac anxiety is promi, nent, Strong aversion to
striking colours such as green, red & black. Very sensitive to music. It relieves all
symptoms, but sometime it excites violently. Loss of shame, lascivious, uncontrollable
sexual desire.
