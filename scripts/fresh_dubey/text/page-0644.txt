SPHERES OF ACTION

Veratrum album acts especially upon the system of nutrition affecting profoundly the
vegetative sphere. It also acts upon the blood and nervous system.

PATHOGENESIS

It causes disorganisation of blood and seperates it into its constitutent components.
The circulation becomes embarassed and as a result, there is general torpor of the
vegetative system. It gives rise to choleric condition, general coldness, prostration,
collaspe, copious watery vomiting and purging, spasmodic colic, cramps and spasms. Its
action on the nervous system results in excitation of the cerebral nerves producing
thereby delirum and mania, in addition to great exhaustion of nerve power.

CONSTITUTION

As it is mainly an acute remedy, no importane is to be attached to be physical make-
up or constitution as such of the patient. But it is especially suited to the complaints of
People too young and too old (i.e. extremes of age)
