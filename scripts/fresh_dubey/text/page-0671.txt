THE TWELVE TISSUE SALTS

Their Place and Function in the Human Economy

In order to obtain the most satisfactory results from Dr. Schuessler’s Biochemic
System of Medicine, one should first of all become acquainted with the tissue salts
individually. A knowledge of the properties and the field of action of each of these
remedies will be found invaluable when the symptoms of any given case are being
considered. In the following brief review, an attempt has been made to bring out the
dominant characteristics of each of the tissue salts and thus to make quicker and easier
the right choice of remedy.
