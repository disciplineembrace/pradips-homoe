IPECACUANHA

Ipecac HAHNEMANN Rubiaceae

—_

INTRODUCTION

Ipecacuanha is an interesting drug standing
on the ceasefire line between the absolutely
opposite ways of therapeutics. Old school usually
studies, experiments with and uses drug for their
physiological actions and lays down maximum
non-lethal doses for adults and children, while
Homoeopathy uses them for their exactly
opposite effect and in dynamic dose for vital
stimulation. Ipecac is interesting as being one of
the drugs that Allopathy uses to cure
Homoeopathically. It is used in both ways, in fact
to PROVOKE AND TO CURE VOMITING.
According to Hahnemann Ipecac is a short acting medicine.

CLINICAL

Anaemia, Asthma, Bone affection, Chorea, Convulsion, Constipation, Colic, Cough,
Diarrhoea, Deafness, Dysentery, Eye affection, Fever, Gall-stone colic, Gastric ulcer,
Haematemesis, Haemorrhage, Menstrual disorders, Nausea and vomiting, Pregnancy
disorders, Stomach disorders, Whooping cough, etc.

SPHERES OF ACTION

The chief action is on the ramification of the pneumogastric nerve, on medulla
oblongata, on mucous membrane of respiratory and gastro-intestinal tract and arterial
vascular system.

When acting on pneumogastric nerve, it produces spasmodic irritation in chest and
stomach, the former gives rise to ASTHMA and latter to NAUSEA AND VOMITING.
When acting on mucus membrane, it produces a catarrhal condition, pulmonary
congestion, even hepatisation, nausea and vomiting.

CONSTITUTION

Physical make-up: It is especially suited to stout persons of lax fibre; fair people;
women and children; emphysematous persons; persons who have a history of epistaxis
and other forms of blood loss.

Relation with heat and cold: Chilly patient.
Temperament: Irritable temperament.
