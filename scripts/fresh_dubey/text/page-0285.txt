Eupatorium Perfoliatum

Boneset HAHNEMANN Compositae

INTRODUCTION

Commonly known as Boneset or Ague-weed,
Eupatorium perfoliatum is a remedy with which
every Homoeopath is more or less familiar
especially in the management of acute fevers like
Dengue, Influenza, Malaria, etc. In regard to its
common name “Boneset”, DR. N.M. CHOUDHURY
SAYS “The name Boneset is very apt in as much as
it conveys to the mind the supreme characteristic
of the drug viz., it soothes painful bone. So
important is this symptom that its absence may be
regarded as a contra-indication for Eupatorium”.

Bilious fever, Bone pains, Cough, Dengue, Influenza, Intermittent fever, Soreness,
Headache, Rheumatism, etc.

SPHERES OF ACTION

Eupatorium perfoliatum acts principally upon the gastro-hepatic organs and
bronchial mucous membrane and fibrous tissue.
PATHOGENESIS

It produces, as one of its most characteristic conditions, bilious derangement with
tenderness of hepatic region and vomiting of bile. It also produces sorenss and aching of
muscles and bones which accompany some forms of febrile diseases like malaria and
influenza.

CONSTITUTION

There is no particular constitution as such of this patient. However, the remedy is
more adapted to diseases of people, having worn out constitution due to excessive abuse
of aleohol, cachexia, from prolonged or frequent attacks of bilious or intermittent fevers.

GUIDING SYMPTOMS
1. Brusised feeling as if broken all over the body (Arn., Pyrogen, Ham.).

2. Bone pains of all description appear in the remedy, Pains affecting back,
head, chest, limbs, especially the wrists which feel as if dislocated. The more
general and severe the pain, better the remedy is indicated and adapted.
