AVENA SATIVA

Oat Gramineae

CLINICAL:

Alcoholism. Bad effects of morphine habit.
Debility. Neurasthenia, Palpitation. Sexual
excess. Sleeplessness.

It has selective action on brain & nervous
system favourably influencing their nutritive
function. Avena has been used empirically in
substantial doses i.e. 5-15 drops of the tincture,
preferably in hot water in cases of nervous
exhaustion, sexual debility and the morphine
habit. It acts as a best tonic for debility after
exhausting diseases,. It is a good medicine for sleeplessness especially of alcoholics.
There is inability to keep mind on any one subject, especially when due to mas-turbation
or sexual irregularities. Author has found very useful especially in old age group when
given with hot milk. In case of sleeplessness it can be compared with the following
medicines :

1) Coca : Can find no rest anywhere but sleepy.

2) Coffea : Wakeful ; on a constant move sleeps till 3 a.m. after which only dozing
sleepless, on account of mental activity, flow of ideas with nervous excitability.

3) Kali phos : Sleeplessness with want of nerve power due to over work, excitement
and worry.

4) Passiflora : Sleeplessness in Asthmatics and hypertensive patients.
5) Piscidia : Insomnia due to worry in neurotics.
