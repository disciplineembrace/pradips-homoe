Baby :

Sycotic baby, sickly, WITHERED APPEARANCE with; premature aged expression in the face. There

The baby craves for sweets which cause diarrhoea. Green diarrhoea like chopped spinach, after
weaning or green watery diarrhoea or turning green when left on diaper.

General Modalities :

Aggravation; Cold air, cold food, eating sugar, sugar candy, ice-cream, imaginations and prolonged
mental exertions, warmth in general, lying of left side and bright light.

Amelioration: Open air, bathing with cold water, cold application, tight bandaging, dark room,
eructation.
Relations :
Antidoted by: Natrum mur., particularly for the bad effects of cauterisation with silver nitrate.
Antidote to: Nit.ac., Sep., Puls., Calc. carb.
Inimical to: Coffea.
