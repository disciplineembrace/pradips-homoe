INTRODUCTION

From water of a mineral spring of Ottawa,
proved, as to its deleterious effects by Dr, J.G.
Gundlach who, with his family drank it for more
than a year, and who was still suffering from its
effects five years later. The evaporated,
triturated and potentised salt, was proved in the
usual way under the auspices of the American
doctor, Sherbina, Sanicula is chiefly the
children’s remedy of our materia medica. It is a
deep acting, constitutional polychrest remedy
and should be prescribed in higher potencies.

Clinical: Car sickness. Constipation. Dandruff. Diarrhoea. Emaciation. Excoriations.
Foot sweat. Indigestion. Leucorrhoea. Marasmus. Ossification too early. Prolapse of

SPHERE OF ACTION

It has action all over the body especially gastrointestinal tract, nervous system,
female sexual organs & skin,

CONSTITUTION

Physical make up—Sanicula child looks old, dirty, greasy & brownish. Skin about
neck wrinkled, hangs in folds, scaly dandruff on scalp, eyebrows and beard.

Heat cold relation—Not stamping.
Temperament—Mentally head strong and obstinate.
Miasm—Psoric & Sycotic miasms are in the background.
Diathesis—Scrofulous, diathesis.

GUIDING SYMPTOMS
1) Dread of downward motion (Bor.)
2) Child headstrong, obstinate, cries and kicks ; does not want to be touched.
3) Head & neck sweat profusely and while sleeping wets pillow far around (Cale., Sil.)
4) Premature ossification of bones.

5) Tongue : Large, flabby, burning, must protrude it to keep it cool; ringworm on
tongue (Nat. M.)
