Sarsaparilla is also suitable for headache and periosteal pain usually from abuse of mercury, syphilis

Furthermore, for rehumatic affections caused due to gonorrhoea, checked by cold, wet weather or

General Modalities :
Aggravation: By touch, pressure, motion, going upstairs and washing.
Amelioration: By warmth,.rest, cold diet and loosening garments.
Relations :

Complementary to: Mercury, Sepia either of which follows well.
Compare; Berb. Vulgaris, Lyco., Nat. mur and Phosphorus.
