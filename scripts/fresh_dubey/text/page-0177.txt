It differs in cholera from Cuprum and Veratrum album.

In Camphor, there is great PROSTRATION, BLUENESS, COLDNESS of the body and the body is
cold and dry, yet the patient wants to be uncovered. And all the discharges are SCANTY.

in Cuprum, there is not so much prostration and coldness but there is more cramping and more
convulsive tendency. So, the more the cramping the better the Cuprum is indicated,

In Ver. Alb., the more copius the discharge from bowels and the more profuse the vomiting and
sweat.

So scanty, cold and dry is Camphor; cold and copiousn discharge is Ver-.alb.

Fever :

In eruptive fever, especially in measles, the face is pale, blue, cold and hippocratic face. The body is
cold to touch, still the patient likes to he uncovered.

When eurptions do not appear or in repercussed eruption of measles and scarlatina, in all Sequelae
of measles, Camphor is a great remedy.
General Modalities :

Aggravation: By motion, at night and in cold air.

Amelioration : When thinking of exsiting complaints and warm air.

Relations :

Camphor antidotes nearly every vegetable medicine, tobacco, and opium.
Compare: Veratrum, Secale and Cuprum in cholera.
