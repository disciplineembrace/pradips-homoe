AETHUSA CYNAPIUM

Fool's Parsley NENNING Umbelliferae

INTRODUCTION

AEthusa Cynapium is one of the best remedies for the
vomiting in children especially during dentition and in hot
summer weather due to intolerance of milk. It is a thirst
remedy and is most important for Cholera infantum and
also for Epilepsy in babies.

CLINICAL

Brain fag, Cholera infantum, Convulsion, Cough,
Delirium, Dyspepsia, Epilepsy, Headache, Hiccough, Idiocy,
Infantile paralysis, Intolerance of milk, Disorders of
stomach, Vomiting, etc.

SPHERES OF ACTION
It acts on nervous system, glandular system, mucous
membrane and skin.

PATHOGENESIS

It is a frightful poison having narcotic properties as well as paralysing effects. The
principal use we make of the drug arises from its action on the stomach.

CONSTITUTION

Make-up of the body: Features expressive of pain and anxiety. Herpetic eruption on
end of the nose. Sunkenness in the face with marked linea nasalis. Physically the child
is so weak that he is unable to stand or hold up the head.

Temperament: Mentally the patient is idiotic, confused and unable ito think.
Relation with heat and cold: Hot patient.
Miasm: It touches all the 3 miasms in acute state.

GUIDING SYMPTOMS

The characteristic, uncommon and peculiar symptoms by which one can make
selection of this remedy homoeopathically by individualising a patient are:

1. Intolerance of milk in any form-e.g. mother’s milk, cow's milk, bottled and
tinned milk. It is vomited in large curds and after vomiting weakness causes—
drowsiness.

2. Indigestion of teething children. Vomiting is violent, sudden, of frothy milk-white
substance or yellow fluid followed by CURDLED MILK AND CHEESY MATTER.
