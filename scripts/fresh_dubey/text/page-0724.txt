Hypochondriacs (Alum, Anac., Mez., Nux. M.) A morbid concern about the health and
exaggarated attention to any unusual bodily or mental sensations; an unfounded belief
that one is suffering from some disease.

Hysterical women (Bar. C., Gels, Hyos, Plat, Nux-M, Sep., Valeriana) Women prone to
hysteria.

Hysteria (Actaea; Crocus, s; Caulo; MagMur. Nux Mosch; Platind): A neurosis usually
arising from mental conflict and repression and characterized by the production of a
diversity of symptoms e.g. tics, paralysis, anaesthesia etc. The disorder is characterized
by dissociation.

Ichorous (Anth, Psor, Carb. A.) Watery acrid discharge.

Icicle (Merc. S.) Tapering ice-formation, produced by freezing of successive drops trickling
from the point of attachment.

Ieterus (Ant. T.) Jaundice.

Idiopathic neuroma (Calen) An old general term for any neoplasm derived from cells of the
nervous system, the cause of which is unknown.

Ill at ease in Society (Coco) Feels embarrassed uncomfortable in Society.

Illusions (Actaea. R., Sabad.) A false perception; the mistaking of something for what it is
not.

Imbecility (Nat. C.) Weak mindedness or.stupidity.

Impending paralysis (Ant. T.) Imminent paralysis.

Inaptness (Sulph. Ac.) Awkwardness.

Incarcerated (Cooa) Confined; imprisoned; denoting, for example, an irreducible hernia :
imprisoned wind.

Incessant (Kali Br. Kreos, Lac-D, Med, Tabac, Rumex, Merc. C.) Continuous.

Incipient: Initial stage.

Incipient consumption (Med.) Tuberculosis in early stage.

Incipient phthisis (Millif) Initial stage of tuberculosis.

Incoherent speech (Hyos.) Inconsistent speech.

Indecisive (Ign, Puts.) Hesitating; Unable to take decisions.

Indignation (Colo, Staph) Anger excited by supposed meanness, injustice, wickedness or
misconduct.

Indolent (Aloe, Cap, Con, Lack; Sep.) Slothful, lazy.
Indolent circulation (Agar.) Sluggish circulation.
