ARUM TRIPHYLLUM

Indian Turnip DR. JEANS Araceae

INTRODUCTION

Arum Triphyllum is the most important of
the Arums. It is one of the sovereign remedies
for an acute cold. It should not be given in low
potency or repeated often as bad effects often
follow.

CLINICAL:

Aphonia. Coryza. Diphtheria, Headache.
Mouth sore, scarlatina. Typhoid. Clergyman’s
sore throat. Glandular swellings. Tongue
cracked,

SPHERES OF ACTION & PATHOGENESIS :

It prominently affects left side. It has marked action on mucous menbranes of mouth,
throat, larynx. It also acts on kidneys, brain, blood and skin. It is an irritant poison
causing excitability & irritability of mind and body. It causes inflammation of the mucous
surfaces and destruction of the tissue. Discharges are acrid causing painful, sore,
rawness & burning of parts.

GUIDING SYMPTOMS :

1) Constant picking at the nose until it bleeds, boring with the finger into the side of
the nose.

2) Pick lips until they bleed, corners of mouth cracked & bleeding.

3) Acrid, ichorous discharges, excoriating mucous surfaces.

4) Painfully sore, raw and burning of parts.

5) Nose feels stopped, inspite of the watery discharge.

6) Aphonia complete, after exposure to northwest winds (Aco., Hep.) and from singing
(Arg. Nit., Caust.)

7) Fluent Coryza with nostrils raw.

8) Children refuse food & drink on account of soreness of mouth & throat.

9) Tongue and buccal cavity raw and bleeding with profuse acrid salivation.
10) Ailments from—Exposure to northwest wind, getting cold, overuse of voice.

11) Mind—Children excessively cross & stubborn. They are nervous, restless and
irritable. Persistently boring into nose or picking at lips, fingers at one spot until it
is sore or bleeds.
