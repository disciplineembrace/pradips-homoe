SPHERES OF ACTION

It has profound, action on nerves espe-cially Vagi and Heart. It also acts on eyes, G.L.
Tract, and urinary system.

It causes cerebal irritation followed by marked irritation of functions of Vagi. It
produces muscular prostration, free secretions. It produces (brings forth) symptoms,
which are experienced by people who had taken tobacco first time in their lives such as
nausea, vomiting, salivation, giddiness even one may faint with prostation and cold

sweating.

GUIDING SYMPTOMS :
1) Deathly nausea, vomiting, and giddiness with death like pallor.

3) Icy coldness of surface; covered with cold sweat.
4) A weak, faint sinking feeling at pit of stomach.
