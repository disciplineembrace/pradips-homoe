Radium bromatum is indicated in many sorts of skin affections. There is intense itching with
burning of skin as of a fire. Itching eruptions on face with oozing, worse shaving. Scaly circumscribed
eruptions on flexor side of the forearm worse scratching, better dry heat and in open air. Small pimples
and succession of small boils especially on forehead and chest.

It is a very effective remedy in keloids, pigmented moles naevi, Acne rosacea, corns & ulcerations
with tendency to delayed healing. Chronic eczematous dermatitis of hands caused by Radium and X-ray
burns. Pruritus vulvae better after warm bath, crack & fissures, scaly excrescences that itch and burn most
violently.

Modality :

Agg.—At night, scratching, warmth of bed. Ame.—Hot bath and in open air.
