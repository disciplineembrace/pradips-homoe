GUIDING SYMPTOMS :

1) Uric-acid diathesis.

2) “Soreness” is the one of the great
characteristics of Lith. Carb. Like Arnica,
bones, joints, muscles whole body sore as if beaten.

3) Rheumatic & Gouty symptoms: Lithium Garb, has Rheumatic and gouty
diathesis. This is an invaluable; remedy in chronic rheumatism when
accompained with heart or eye symptoms and recurring attacks of acute
inflammation of the small joints. Another peculiarity of this rheumatism consists in
the appearance of an erup-tion on the affected part. There is great tenderness,
swel-ling and redness of the joints.

Modalities :

Worse : At night or in morning.

Better : Rising or moving about ; hot water.

4) Heart symptoms : Heart symptoms when associated with rheumatic
symptoms. There is Rheumatic soreness in cardiac region. Throbbing, dull stitch
in cardiac reg-ion. Throbbing and fluttering in heart, extending to back.
Modalities :

Aggravation : Bending forward. Before urination.
Amelioration : After urinating.

heart. Pain in right kidney & ureter.
