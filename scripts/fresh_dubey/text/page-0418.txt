MERCURIUS CORROSIVUS

Corrosive Sublimate BUCHNER HgCl,

INTRODUCTION

Merc. cor. is a very small drug, but its action
is very remarkable in a few diseases. The key-
note which runs through the whole drug is
GREAT TENESMUS, either in the rectum or
urinary bladder, or in both.

It was proved by our Master Hahnemann
with the help of physio-chemical process, by
mixing one part of mercury and two parts of
chlorine.

Mercurius corrosivus very much resembles
Mercurius in many of its pathogenesis, but the
depth of action, severity of the case, excoriation
and burning, rapid spreading of ulcer and tenesmus are much more in Merc. cor.

Apthae, Bone affection, Diarrhoea, Dysentery, Eczema, Gonorrhoea, Kidney affection,
Labour, Mumps, Puerperal fever, Paralysis, Syphilis, Tenesumus, Throat affections,
Tongue affections, Ulcerated intestine, Urinary troubles, Uvula elongated, etc.

SPHERES OF ACTION

It acts on mucous membrane, intestine, urinary bladder, eye, bones etc. It also has
got action over the serous membranes, especially peritoneum.

It causes irritation and inflammation of both mucous and serous memmbranes
producing tenesmus of rectum and bladder, suppression of urine.

GUIDING SYMPTOMS

1. Great tenesmus persists before, during and even after stool. Tenesmus not
relieved by stool; stool scanty mucus mixed with stool.

2. Tenesmus of the bladder and rectum at the same time; urine passes in drops
with much pain,

3. Throat intensely inflamed, swollen, burning with swollen gums, which bleed easily.

4. Ulcers are deep and spread very rapidly.

5. Desire for cayenne pepper, ice, cold water.

6. Aliments from : Syphilis, sun and summer weather.
