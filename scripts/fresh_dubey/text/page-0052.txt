Skin :
Dry type of skin eruptions, The,skin withers, becomes dry and is subject to eruption, thickening,
ulceration and bleeding. Tettery type of, skin eruption. The skin is damaged and fissured from its dryness.
Dryness and burning of the skin run through the remedy.
Time: Aagavated in winter.

Character: The skin itches even when there is no eruption; more itching from warmth of bed. The
patient scratches until the skin bleeds, then becomes painful. The eruptions are worse in the warmth of
bed. The striking feature of this remedy is chronic dryness of the skin.

Sweat is rare and if occurs, it is scanty. Alumina is not suited to those with copious exhaustive sweat.
It is thus the very opposite of Calcarea carb. which sweats profusely; but Alumina is no doubt, very much
exhaustive due to spinal and-paralytic affection, but does not sweat.

Modality: All skin symptoms are WORSE IN WINTER.

General Modalities :

Aggravation: In cold air, during winter, from eating potatoes, in the morning on waking, full and
new moon.

Amelioration: In summer weather, from warm drink.

Relations :
Complementary to Bryonia.
Alumina is the chronic of Bryonia.

Alumina is one of the chief antidotes for lead poisoning, painter's colic (Plumbum). The action of
Alumina is slow in developing and the remedy must not be changed quickly.

The following medicines have relation to the moon's phases :-

Aggravation: At New Moon; Alumina, Ammon. carb., Calcarea carb., Causticum, Cuprum met.,
Sepia, Silicea.

Aggravation: At full Moon; Alumina, Cal. carb., Graphites, Nat. carb., Nat.mur. Sepia,
Silicea, Sulphur.

Aggravation during increasing Moon: Arnica.
