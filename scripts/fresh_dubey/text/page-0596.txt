STICTA PULMONARIA

S. Lilienthal

Lungwort

INTRODUCTION

It is a little known and a rarely used remedy,
put that does not render it any the less important
for those cases that will call for sticta, will need
sticta, none but sticta will help.

CLINICAL

Anosmia, Atrophic Rhinitis. Catarrh.
Cough. Headache. Housemaid’s Knee. Rheumatism.

SPHERES OF ACTION AND PATHOGENESIS
It Chiefly acts on respiratory tract & joints;
but also having action on head, gastrointestinal
& genitourinary tracts.
GUIDING SYMPTOMS :
1) Respiratory Symptoms :

Nose : Feeling of fullness at the root of the nose is the charac-teristic symptom
of Sticta. There is dryness of nasal mucous membrane not due to lack of
secretion, but secretions dry up very rapidly leaving thick crusts behind them. The
patient blows his nose, but no dishcarge comes out, There may be incessant
sneezing, hay fever etc.

Cough :The Cough of Sticta is also mostly dry, but loose in the morning. It is an
important drug in cases of cough left after measles. Throat is raw with posterior
dropping of mucus and dry hacking cough. (Sang, Eup. Perf, Puls.)

Modalities : Aggravation—Inspiration, towards evening and when tired.
Concomitant—Rheumatism precedes catarrhal symptoms

2) Headache : Sticta is suited to catarrhaj headache before discharge appears.
There is dull headache with heavy pressure in forehead and root of the nose.
Sensation as if scalp was too small may be there,

3) Rheumatism Causation : Fall.

Location : Specially right shoulder, Knee Joints and neck are affected. (Cheli, Mag.
Garb. Sang., Strontia).

Sensation : There is drawing & shooting pains in joints, Joints are red, painful &
swollen.
