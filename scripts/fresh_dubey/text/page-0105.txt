 ASAFOETIDA

Narthex asafoetida

FRANZ

Umbelliferae

INTRODUCTION

In common language it is called
the Devil’s Dung, a name very
apporpriate because of the very
offensive smell. It was introduced in
our Materia Medica by Franz in
1822 and Hahnemann himself
contributed to the proving. Its
influence is most marked on the
nervous system producing hysteria,
chorea & neuralgia. It is suited to
nervous, hysterical & syphilitic
people.

Asthma. Bone-diseases of. Chorea. Dyspepsia. Flatulence. Hysteria. Iritis, Lactatton-

diseases of. Neuralgia. Ozaena. Syphilis. Ulcers.

1) Hysteria from suppression of habitual discharge such as sudden checking
of long standing expectoration or of chronic diarrhoea or menses or skin
affections.lt has globus hystericus i.e. patient feels as if a ball ascends from
stomach to oesophagus. This is attended with excessive distension of abdomen and
sensation as if the peristaltic motions were reversed with eructations

smelling like garlic even regurgitation of food.

2) Asafoetida affects left side generally : Left side of abdomen, left side of neck & nape,

left arm & left leg. (Lach., Lil. Tig., Thuja).
3) All discharges are horribly offensive and ichorous.

4) Intolerance of all dressings ; sensitivness to contact is a leading characteristic in

periosteal inflammations, ulcers & caries of bones.

5) An empty gone feeling in the stomach at 11 A.M. (Sulph., Zinc) with a strange
pulsation in the pit of the stomach.

6) Deficient milk with oversensitiveness after confinement (Lac Defl.)

7) Modalities.

Aggravation—After eating, drinking, at night, rest, warm, left side.

Amelioration—By pressure, in open air.
