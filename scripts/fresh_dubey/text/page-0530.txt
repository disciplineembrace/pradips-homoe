INTRODUCTION

Rumex, the yellow dock, or curled dock, is a
common British weed. This medicine was used by
the Allopaths for the cure of itch, but we,
Homoeopaths, use this medicine for diseases like,
laryngitis, bronchitis, asthma and diarrhoea. No
doubt, it is indicated in skin diseases as well
when the symptoms agree. The leaves of this
plant from which the drug is obtained and
potentised are found to contain Bin oxalate of
Potash. So, most of the symptoms manifested
during proving, though partial, resemble those of
potassium salts.

CLINICAL

Abdominal disorders, Aphonia, Asthma, Bronchitis, Catarrh, Cough, Coryza,
Diarrhoea, Hoarseness, Phthisis, Rheumatism, Skin affection, Throat and Lung
affections, Urticaria, etc.
