Diarrhoea :

* Causation: Due to suppression of skin eruption. after taking cabbage, sour krout, during
pregnancy and stormy weather.

* Character of stool; Yellowish, watery, gushing out, with itching of anus.
¢ Sensation: With diarrhoea empty feeling in stomach.
¢ Modality: Diarrhoea only in the daytime (Only during night— China. Puls., Chel.).

Skin:

The skin of Petroleum is rough, ragged, exfoliated and bleeding. “Cracks and fissures all over the
body” or in affected area, is the key-note symptom of Petroleum. Every little injury suppurates.

Dry type of skin eruption on tip of fingers, rough, cracked, which is painful and sensitive to touch.

It has got power to throw out vesicles and the tendency of vesicles is to form thick yellowish crust
with considerable moisture.

All the eruptions itch violently; he cannot rest until he scratches the skin off, and the parts become
moist, bloody, raw and inflammed. Scratching seems very comfortable.

Various forms of eczema especially of scalp and occiput.

Herpetic eruption about the mouth, lips, genitals.

Foul smelling sweat all over, the skin.

Modality: Aggravation: Skin symptoms are worse in winter and better in summer.
The eruptions of PETROLEUM are like that of Graphites.

Both have got cracks and fissures but the oozing in Petroleum IS THIN AND WATERY but
in Graphites it is STICKY AND TRANSPARENT (Kent).

General Modalities :

Aggravation: From carriage riding, before and during a thunderstorm, in winter and in dampness.
Amelioration: Skin symptoms are beter in summer, warm air, lying with head high and dry weather.
