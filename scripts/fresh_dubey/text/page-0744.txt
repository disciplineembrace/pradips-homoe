Glossary 741

Bung-hole : Wide mouth of pipe.
Bunion : Hardness of skin.

Cachexia: General lack of nutrition and wasting due to a chronic disease.
Cadaverous smell: Smell like that coming from a dead body.

Calculi: Plural of calculus; stone.

Calvarium: Skull-cap. Upper dome-like portion of the skull.

Calisthenics : Instrumental exercise to improve health.

Canine hunger: Hunger like that of dog.

Canthi: Corners of eyes.

Carbuncle: Boil with many openings.

Carbogenoid constitution: Constitution with intolerance of carbon or carbon product.
Cardiac: Relating to cardia, heart.

Caries: Decay of bone leading to its death and destruction.

Carrion like : Like the decaying flesh of dead body, very repulsive.
Carphology : Involuntary picking of bed clothes.

Catarrhal ischuria: Ischuria or suppression of urine due to catarrh in bladder.
Chagrin : Grief; sorrow.

Chalazae: Heard nodosities of eyelids.

Chancre: Hard tumour usually due to syphilis.

Celebacy: The state of complete sexual abstinence.

Chemosis: Oedema of conjunctiva forming a swelling round cornea.

Chilblain: Ulceration of fingers due to exposure to severe cold.

Chlorosis : A state of pale or green discoloration of skin usually due to anaemia.
Cholera asiatica : Severe form of cholera usually epidemic.

Cholera morbus : Strong diarrhoea usually with severe colic and profuse vomiting leading
to collapse and death.

Chorea: Involuntary movement of a part.

Chordee: Painful erection of penis usually due to gonorrhoea.
Cicatrix : Scarring of skin usually due to an injury or burn.
Clenched thumb : Thumb in the fully flexed position.
