PARTICULARS

Respiratory symptoms :

Nose : It is a very useful remedy for chronic nasal catarrh, hay fever, etc. There is irritation and
tingling in nose with constant desire for sneezing. Discharge is thin, watery, irritating excoriating
from anterior and posterior nares. In chronic nasal Catarrah, the nose is swollen, membrane is sore
and excoriated with profuse, thick yellow discharge. Modality : Nasal symptoms are aggravated by
sneezing. Throat: Especially indicated in diptheria, tonsillitis, and chronic follicular pharyngitis. There is

marked burning in Pharynx with dryness. Difficult swallowing, foetid breath, enlarged swollen glands are
the other indications in throat complaints.

Chest: Dry hacking cough with little expectoration. Cough is also loose, croupy, suffocating &
spasmodic, Expectoration is copious greenish—yellow and purulent. Asthma from 11 p.m. until 2 a.m.
It is indicated in tuberculosis when there is hoarse, racking cough with profuse, purulent expectoration.
Debility, emaciation, tendency to diarrhoea and fever with night sweats are present.

Modalities : Aggravation from motion, talking, in warm room.

Skin : It has many types of eruptions on the skin, such as, boils, pustules, rash and scales, moist
eruptions, eczema, itching eruptions, herpes and psoriasis. Skin is dry, scaly and itching. There is
marked exfoliation of skin in large scales, leaving a raw exuding surface beneath. Acne hard, shotty,
indurated base with pustule at apex. Eczeme of the beard, watery, oozing itching & worse by washing.

Relations :
