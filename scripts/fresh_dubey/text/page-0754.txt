Glossary 751

Scald : A burn caused by a hot liquid or hot moist vapour.
Scirrhus: Hard cancer with predominance of fibrous tissue.
Scrofulous: A glandular swelling which predisposes to tuberculosis.
Scurf: Dandruff; branny desquamation.

Sclerosis: Induration or hardening of chronic inflammatory origins; jnduration with
hypertrophy of connective tissue.

Scrawny: 1. Very thin, skinny and bony.
2. Stunted or scrubby.

Sebaceous: The gland which secretes the substance whose ducts open into hair follicles.
Secundine: The after Urth, or the placenta.

Septicaemia: A morbid condition due to presence or reproduction of pathologic bacteria in
the blood.

Serous membrane: The lining membrane of any one of the, great splanchnic or lymph
cavities.

Sedentary habits: Inactive habits.

Shattering: Run down: broken down.

Shingles: Herpes zoster.

Similimum: That which is most similar in nature, character and action.

Slimy: Slippery.

Somnambulism: Habitual walking in sleep.

Snappish: Cross or irritable; uncivil.

Snuffles: Catarrhal discharge from the nasal mucous membrane in infants.

Somersault: Turning the body one full revolution with heels over head.

Sneaking: Cowardly; secret; underhand.

Sopor: Deep sleep.

Sordes: Foul matter collected on lips and teeth in low fevers.

Spermatorrhoea: An involuntary discharge of semen, without orgasm.

Stasis : Stagnation of blood or other fluid.

Stercoraceous: Consisting of, or contain faeces.

Staining linen: Making the towel dirty with faeces or urine.

Strangury: Difficulty in micturition; slow and painful discharge of urine

Stupor: Partial or nearly complete unconsciousness.

Strangling: Killing by squeezing the throat with hands nose, etc., causing suffocation or
choking in any manner.

Strumous: Scrofulous; goitrous.
