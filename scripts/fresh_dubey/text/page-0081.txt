APOCYNUM CANNABINUM

Indian Hemp FREITAG Apocynaceae

INTRODUCTION

Apocynum is reputed to be a very good
remedy for dropsy, hydrocephalus and
ascites and as such it occupies a place as

important as that of Apis.

CLINICAL:

Ascites. Amenorrhoea. Cough.
Dropsies. Heart affection. Hydrocephalous.
Metrorrhagia. Urinary affection. Vomiting.

SPHERES OF ACTION & PATHOGENESIS :

It acts on urinary organs digestive
tract and uterus. Acting on urinary organs, induces diuresis and removes the dropsical
effusions It depresses the heart, kidneys and intestines relaxing sphincters. It also

produces intense gastric disturbance.

GUIDING SYMPTOMS :
1) Excretions diminished, especially urine and sweat.

2) Dropsy with thirst (Ace. Acid, without thirst, Apis Mel) but water disagrees, causing
pain or is vomited ; when associated with heart, liver & kidney diseases.

3) Hydrocephalas with open sutures ; stupor, sight of one eye lost, constant and
involuntary motion of one arm and one leg ; forehead projected. (Helleborus—

leftarm & leg,—Bryonia)

4) Amenorrhoea in young girls with bloating or dropsical extension of abdomen and
extremities.

5) Metrorrhagia with continued or paroxysmal flow and fluid or clotted blood, nausea,
vomiting, palpitation, pulse quick, feeble when moved ; vital depression, fainting

when rising from pillow.
6) Cough, short & dry or deep and loose during pregnancy (Con.)
7) Ailments from : Dropsy after typhus, typhoid, scarlatina, cirrhosis, after abuse of
quinine, alcohol.
It can be compared with Acetic acid, Apis mel, and Ars, Alb. in dropsy.
