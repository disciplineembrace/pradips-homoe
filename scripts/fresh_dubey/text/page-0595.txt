Staphisagria is ideally suited to mechanical injuries from sharp cutting instruments or post-surgical
operations, with stinging and smarting pain like the cutting of a knife.

Arthritic nodosities appear in joints, especially of fingers, inflammation of phalanges with sweating
and suppuration; backache which is aggravated at night in bed and in the morning before rising, etc. are
also successfully dealt with by this drug.

General Modalities :

Aggravation: By mental affections, from anger, indignation, grief, mortification, loss of vital fluids,
tobacco, onanism, sexual excess and from least touch on affected parts.

Compare: Causticum, Colocynth, Ign., Lyco., Puls., Staphisagria acts well after each of them.

Causticum, Colocynth and Staph., follow well in the order named.
Inimical: Ranunculus bulbosus, either before or after.

Note : An application of the dilution of one part of Staphisagria tincture with four parts of water will
kill the lice or the head vermin. It should also be taken internally in potentised form.
