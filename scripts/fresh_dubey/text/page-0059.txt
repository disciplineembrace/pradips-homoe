AMMONIUM MURIATICUM

Sal Ammoniac NENNING NH,Cl

CLINICAL:

Anosmia. Bronchitis. Constipation. Coryza.
Cough. Diarrhoea. Haemorrhoids, Leucorrhoea.
Menstrual disorder.

SPHERES OF ACTION & PATHOGENESIS :

It affects mucous mem-branes increasing the
secretion and retaining them especially in chest
and gall ducts. It causes irregular circulation,
blood seems to be in constant turmoil and
thereby producing ebullitions, burnings and

localized throbbing.

It has also marked action on liver and female genital organs. It causes ulceration and
ulcerative pains also dislocation like pains and tension as if tendons were contracted.
CONSTITUTION :

Make up—It is especially adopted to those who are fat and sluggish or body large and
fat, but legs too thin. Women having large buttocks.

Temperament—Melancholic and disposition to tears. Relation with heat & cold:
Chilly patient Miasm : Psoric and syphilitic.
GUIDING SYMPTOMS :
1) Body large and fat but legs too thin.
2) Symptoms accompanied by cough and profuse glairy secretions.

3) Diarrhoea and vomiting even bloody discharge from the bowels during menses
(Amm.Garb.,Ver.alb.,Puls.) with flow more profuse at night.

4) Leucorrhoea : Like white of an egg, (Alum., Borax, Calc. P.) preceded by gripping
pain about the navel; brown, slimy, painless, after every urination.

5) Like other salts of chloride, it has crumbling from the verge of anus. (Nat. M., Mag.
M.) Hard crumbling stools require great effort in expulsion,

6) Coryza—Discharge is thin, watery & acrid corroding the inside of the nostrils and
the upper lip (All.C., Ars alb., Ars.Iod.)

7) Sensation of coldness in the back, between scapulae(Lachn.)

8) Tension in the Joints, as from shortening of the muscles. Contractibn of hamstring
tendons.
