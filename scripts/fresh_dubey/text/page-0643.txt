VARIOLINUM

Pus from small pox pustules

INTRODUCTION

Variolinum is a drug belonging to the nosode
family. The pus from the smallpox pustules is
taken and potentised. It has only fragmentary

and partial provings and as such full therapeutic
uses are not known.

CLINICAL

Chicken pox, Herpes Zoster, Smallpox, -
prevention of.

GUIDING SYMPTOMS

1. Generally Variolinum is used as a

preventive medicine for smallpox. It bears the same relation to smallpox that
antitoxin does to diptheria.

2. As far as the curative value of this drug is concerned, it can be given in all cases of
variola—whether single, confluent, haemorrhagic or malignant and in varioloid and
vericella, if and when they appear even after vaccination is given.

3. But as a preventive against smallpox as well as a protection against it, this remedy
is far superior to crude vaccination and is absolutely safe and free from any
sequelae especially of septic and tubercular infection.

4. It protects from, modifies and cures smallpox.

5. It is also most efficacious, infallible and suitable in smallpox both for curative and
preventive purposes in any potency—low or high.
