CONSTITUTION
Calearea carb. is adapted to “LEUCOPHLEGMATIC” constitution.

Make-up of the body: It is suited to FAIR, FATTY AND FLABBY patient who
becomes easily fatigued and tired on walking, with much sweating, cold extremities and
sour smell of the body. There is TENDENCY TO OBESITY in youth. Slow in movement,

Make-up of the body of baby: Children who crow rar. chalky look with red face, large
belly like an inverted saucer with LARGE HEAD OPEN FONTANELLES AND SUTURES,
flabby muscles, pale skin, soft bonesi who SWEAT EASILY, SPECIALLY ON BACK
SIDE OF THE HEAD AND NECK and swollen glands.

Girls who are fleshy, plethoric and grow too rapidly.

Relation with heat and cold: CHILLY PATIENT, DISPOSED to CATCH COLD
VERY EASILY.

Miasm: It is one of the greatest anti-psoric and antisycotie remedies.
Diathesis: Scrofulous, rachitic and tubercular diathesis.

1. The patient has GREAT SENSITIVNESS TO TAKE COLD; gets frequent coryza
which is chronic in nature and psoric in origin; CANNOT TOLERATE COLD (in
acute stage, Belladonna).

2. Disposed to grow fat, corpulent and uniwieldy.

3. Sweat: Sweat of Calcarea carb. is very characteristic. HEAD SWEATS
PROFUSELY, WHILE SLEEPING WETTING the pillow far around. Profuse
perspiration mostly on back of head and neck or chest and upper part of the body.
Sweat of single part, head, scalp, nape of neck, chest, axillae, sexual organs, hands,
palm, knees, feet, sole, etc. with coldness. The sweat is Sour Smelling. Rawness of
soles of feet from perspiration; blisters and offensive foot sweat.

4. Coldness in general and the single part (Kali bi); head, stomach, abdomen, feet and
legs habitually cold and damp, as if they had cold damp stockings on; continuously
cold in bed; averison to cold open air. “It goes right through her”; Sensitive to cold
damp air; the patient has great liability to take cold.

5. Feels better in every way when constipated, stool has to be removed mechanically.
(Aloe., Sepia, Silicea, Thuja.).

6. Calcarea is an excellent remedy for polypi and exostosis.

7. The patient desires and likes to be magnetised. (Phos., Silicea).

8. All discharges smell sour e.g. vomitus, diarrhoea, sweat, eructation; sour odour of
the whole body (Mag. Carb., Hepar., Rheum.,).

9. Longing for eggs, especially during either sickness or convalescence; craves
undigestible things (Alumina, Nitric acid, Cina, Cicuta, Psorinum); has aversion to
meat (Graphitis, Alumina, Pulsatilla, Muriatic acid); great longing for fresh aif
(when in a room) which inspires, benefits and strengthens (Puls., Sulphur).
