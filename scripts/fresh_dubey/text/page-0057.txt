PARTICULARS

Headache:

‘Causation: Exposure to cold, wet stormy weather.

Location: Headache is felt more in the forehead.

Sensation: Sensation of fulness as if the forehead would burst.
Modalities:

* Aggravation: From cold, cold washing and during menses.
* Amelioration: By pressure and lying on painful side.
