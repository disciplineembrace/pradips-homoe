CICUTA VIROSA

~ Water Hemlock HAHNEMANN Umbelliferae

INTRODUCTION

Cicuta virosa can be nick-named as
‘CONVULSION’. It is very valuable remedy for
violent convulsion from any cause with loss of
consciousness. This drug has been derived from
vegetable kingdom. The whole plant is very
poisonous and is fatal to animals. The mother
tincture is prepared from fresh root of the plant.
When the poisonous property is transformed into
dynamic power after potentisation, it serves as an
excellent remedy for various nervous disorders
manifested in the form of epilepsy, chorea,
spasm, hysteria, convulsion, etc.

CLINICAL

Convulsion, Epilepsy, Hysteria, Meningitis, Numbness, Paralysis, Skin affection,
Tetanus, etc.
SPHERES OF ACTION

The cheif centres of action of this drug are cerebro-spinal nervous system, especially
medula oblongata, gastro-intestinal tract and skin.

It causes irritation of the cerebro-spinal nervous system producing local and
generalised spasm and paralysis, tetanus, epileptic, and epileptiform convulsions,
trismus and tonic and clonic spasm in general.

CONSTITUTION
There is no particular constitution or look of the patient as such.

Temperament: Nervous temperament.
Relation with heat and cold: Chilly patient.
Miasm: Psora is in the background.
GUIDING SYMPTOMS
1. According to Clarke, the two grand key-note symptoms of Cicuta virosa are:

(a) Twitching and spasmodic jerks.
(b) Bending of the head backwards or opisthotonos.
