HAMAMELIS VIRGINICA

Witch Hazel HERING Hamamelaceae

INTRODUCTION

No remedy has wider spheres of usefulness in
ease of haemorrhage and disordered blood-vessels
than Hamamelis virginica. It also has a decided
relation to the effects of mechanical injuries including
burns of the first degree. As a local application it
ranks with Arnica and Calendula. It is widely said to
be the “ACONITE OF THE VENOUS CAPILLARY
SYSTEM”. When there is oozing out of venous blood
from any where in the body, either from piles or
varicose veins or from nose, bowels, uterus, lungs,
open injury, ete, the great anti-haemorrhagic
homoeopathic medicine that we can think of first, is
Hamamelis.

CLINICAL

Bone affection, Bruises, Bums, Cancer, Consumption,
Enteric fever, Eye affection, Gastric Ulcer, Haematemesis,
Haematuria, Haemorrhage, Haemorrhoids, Injury, Leucorrhoea, Menstrual disorders.
Epistaxis, Rheumatism, Scurvy, Testicles inflammation, Threatened abortion, Ulcers, Varicose
veins, etc.

SPHERES OF ACTION
It acts on VEINS, CAPILLARIES, liver, portal system, rectum and generative organs.

PATHOGENESIS

Acting upon the coats of veins, it causes relaxation with consequent engorgement and
even structural lesions and thereby produce PASSIVE VENOUS HAEMORRHAGE
FROM ANY PART.

CONSTITUTION

There is no particular constitution but Ham. is suitable for those who are having
HAEMORRHAGIC DIATHESIS and suffer from diseases like PHLEBITIES, VARICOSIS.
HAEMORRHOIDS and VENOUS HAEMORRHAGE.

GUIDING SYMPTOMS
1. Intense soreness of affected parts is the key-note of this remedy,

2. Bleeding from any orifice or from open wound; blood being DARK PASSIVE AND
NON-COAGULABLE.
