proved by Central Council For Research in

Materia Medica, Kali Mur. unites with albumen,
forming fibrin, which is found in every tissue of
the body except the bones. In other words this
Salt is found in blood corpuscles muscles, nerve
and brain cells as well as in the intercellular
fluids. In its physiological character it is closely
related to sodium chloride, many of the properties of which it shares.

OF ACTION
Kali Mur. acts on blood corpuscles, muscles, nerve & brain cells.

Kali Mur acts well in the second stage of inflammations of mucous membrane &
serous membranes when the exudation is of a fibrinous character usually white in colour.
It is also of great use in catarrhal affection & glandular swellings.

PARTICULARS

Eyes : Discharge of white mucus from the eyes, or yellow, greenish matter and yellow purulent
scabs. Superficial flat ulcer of the eye. Trachoma. Corneal opacities.

Ears : Chronic catarrhal conditions of the middle ear. Snapping and noises in the ear.
Deafness from swelling of external ear, throat troubles. Great effusion about the auricle. Glands about the
ear swollen. Threatended mastoid.

Nose : Catarrh. Phlegm white thick ; Vault of pharynx covered with adherent crusts. Nosebleed.
