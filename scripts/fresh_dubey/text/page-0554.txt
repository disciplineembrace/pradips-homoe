Sambucus Nigra 545

There is no thirst during heat or sweat stage. Fever paroxysms are preceded by dry cough. The
patient may also have oedematous swelling in parts of the body, especially the legs.

General Modalities :
Aggravation: During rest, after eating fruits,uncovering, after midnight and from 2 to 3 a.m.

Amelioration: Sitting up in bed and motion. Most of the pains occur during rest and disappear while
jn motion.

Relations :
Compare: China, Ipecac, and sulphur.
Relieves: Ailments from abuse of Arsenicum.
Follows well: After opium and in bad effects of fright.
