ABIES CANADENSIS

Hemlock Spruce GATCHELL Coniferae

INTRODUCTION

It is one of the remarkbale remedies in the
treatment of chronic gastric affections and uterine

disorders.
CLINICAL

Indigestion. Liver disorder. Uterine disorder.

SPHERES OF ACTION & PATHOGENESIS

It acts on stomach and uterus, heart, lungs. It
produces catarrhal condition of stomach.

GUIDING SYMPTOMS
1) Great debility with strong desire to lie down and rest most of the time.
2) Gnawing, hungry, faint feeling at the epigastrium.
3) Great appetite, even tendency to eat far beyond the capacity for digestion.
4) Craving for meat, pickles, radishes, turnips, coarse food etc. is peculiar.

5) Chilliness is very characteristic especially when associated with peculiar cravings
and prostration.

6) A remedy for uterine displacement with sore feeling at fundus of uterus relieved by
pressure.

PARTICULARS

Stomach

Gastric symptoms are the most prominent Symptoms to guide us in our selection of Abies Can.
There is canine hunger which gnaws in the stomach causing, a sort of fainting feeling at the epigastrium.
The patient does not know how much he eats and very often there is excessive loading of the stomach
which causes distention of stomach & abdomen with severe palpitation of heart.

Female genital Organs

Uterine prolapsus with sore feeling at fundus of uterus, relieved by pressure. Prostration; wants to lie
down all the time. She thinks womb is soft & feeble.
