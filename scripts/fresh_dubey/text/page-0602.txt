SPHERES OF ACTION

Its action is centrifugal, from within outwards. It has great affinity for the skin. No
remedy has more general, positive and persistent action upon the skin than Sulphur. It
acts pre eminently on the nervous system, also affecting the venous capillary system and
through it affecting profoundly, the entire vegetative sphere. Mucous membranes also
feel most severely the influence of Sulphur, especially of eyes, bronchi, urethra and
rectum. The portal system also comes under its special influence.

PATHOGENESIS

Subjected to venous plethora. It has portal stasis manifesting as redness at the muco-
cutaneous junctions e.g. nostrils, lips, anus, etc. Affecting the skin, it produces heat and
burning with severe itching.
