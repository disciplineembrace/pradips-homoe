Drosera Rotundifolia

Tuberculosis of bone : Drosera is a good drug for spinal caries with extreme deformity.
Sciatica : There is pressing pain in the hip region; aggravation from pressure, stooping, lying on
painful side and relieved after rising from bed.
General Modilities :
Aggravation.: After mid-night, lying down, on getting warm in bed, drinking, singing and laughing.

Relations :
Antidotes: Camphor.
Complementary to: Nux vomica.
Follows well: After Samb., Sulph., Ver.alb.
Followed by: Calc., Puls., Sulph.
Compare: Cina, Coral, Cup., Ipecac and Samb., in spasmodic coughs.

*~ Master Hahnemann advises that only a single dose of the 30th potency c ures within 7 or 8 days and
not to repeat which spoils the cure.
