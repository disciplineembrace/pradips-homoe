724 Text Book of MATERIA MEDICA

Nymphomania (Murex, Plat.) Extreme erotism or sexual desire in women; correlated with
satyriasis in men.

Nystagmus (Physo) Rhythmical oscillation of the eyeballs, either horizontal, rotatory or
vertical.

Obscene things (Lit. T.) Lewd things.

Obscuration (Ruta) Darkness, indistinctness.

Old sinners (Agnus) Debauch (See Debauch.).

Old toper (Carbo. V.) A person addicted to alcoholic drinks for a long period.

Onanism (Arg. M., Calad, Lack, Plat, Staph.)The word used by some, though incorrectly, as
synonymous with masturbation. Coitus interruptus.

Ophthalmia Neonatorum (Arg N., Syph, Thuj) Purulent discharge from the eyes of infant
commencing within 21 days of birth. Only 6% of total cases are gonorrhoeal, but all are
notifiable.

Opisthotonos (Cicuta, Nat. S., Ver. V) A tetanic spasm in which the spine and extremities
are bent with convexity forward, the body resting on the head and the heels.

Oppression (Millif.) Heaviness or sensation of weight.

Orbital neuralgia (Chel.) Pain of a severe throbbing or stabbing character in the course or
distribution of the nerves supplying to orbits.

Orchitis (Spong.) Inflammation of the testis.
Otorrhoea (Merc, s., Psor.) A discharge from the external auditory meatus.

Ozaena (Amman C., Aur. M., Nit. Ac.) A foul odor present in certain cases of atrophic.
syphilitic and other forms of chronic rhinitis.

P

Painter’s colic (Alum) Lead colic; ie. severe abdominal pain, with constipation,
symptomatic of lead poisoning.

Pallid (Amyl. N.) Pale.
Panaritium (All c., Amman, c.) Whitlow; paronychia.

Papescent (Bism) Pasty.

Paralysis agitans (Merc.) Parkinson’s disease. A syndrome of mask-like expression,
shuffling gait, tremor of the limbs.

Parched (Bry.) Hot & dry.
