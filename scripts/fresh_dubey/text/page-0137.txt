BLATTA ORIENTALIS

Indian Cockroach DR. D.N. RAY of Calcutta Orthopetera
INTRODUCTION

It accidentaly relieved asthma in a patient
who took tea in which these insects were boiled
down. It is very useful in acute attacks of asthma
especially when given in low potencies.
CLINIGAL

Bronchial asthma. Bronchitis.

SPHERE OF ACTION
It has special action on bronchiotracheal tree.

GUIDING SYMPTOMS
1) It acts best on stout and corpulent patients.

2) In acute case of asthma, especially when associated with bronchitis, threatened
suffocation due to rattling of mucus, oppression of breathing, restlessness, profuse
perspiration & aggravation from lying down are some of the indications.

3) Useful incases of bronchitis and phtihsis where there is much dyspnoea.

4) Indicated after Arsenic when it is insufficient ; Especially useful in acute cases
when Arsenic has given temporary relief. (Thuja Oc. is chronic of Arsenic in Br.
Asthma.)

5) It is also suited to cases which aggravate in rainy weather.
