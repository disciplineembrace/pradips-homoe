VINCA MINOR

~ Lesser Periwinkle ROSENBURG Apocynaceae

—_

INTRODUCTION

This remedy was proved by Rosenburg
on four healthy persons who took the
tincture in 20—60 drops doses. The most
important field of its action is located on
skin, hair & female genital organs. It has
been successfully used in eczema, plica
polonica, alopecia & crusta lactea and to
con-trol the uterine haemorrhages.

On the head, there is numerous humid
eruptions which itch burn intensely; from
these a moisture oozes that mats the hairs
together. Corrosive itching of scalp with irrestible desire to scratch. The skin of the body
is highly sensitive with redness and soreness from slight rubbing

Another use of this remedy is to control uterine haemor-rhage. There is excessive
menstruation with great weakness. It also controls haemorrhages either from fibroids or
climaxis Haemorrhages are usually passive in character.
