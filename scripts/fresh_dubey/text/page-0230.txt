Clematis Erecta 221

PARTICULARS

Headache :

Boring pain in temples with confusion and heaviness of the head which aggravates while bending
head backward and amelioration in open air.

Toothache

Toothache from tobacco. Teeth feel too long (Mezerium.) Worse at night and better by holding cold
water in the mouth and drawing cold air in. (rev. of Staphy.)

Urinary symptoms

The remedy is very useful for urinary troubles. There is frequent, scanty dribbling urination with

burning and sense of constriction in urethra. Patient has to wait a long time before he can Urinate. The
flow is slow or interrupted. Pain worse at night.

Skin

It is one of the very good eruptive medecines, It produces vesicular eruptions which resembles Rhus.
(Anac., Grot. Tig., Fago, Rhus Yen., Xerophyllum.) Vesicles filled with yellow fluid with induration under
the vesicles. Eruptions that come out upon the occiput, face and hands which look red, moist with burning
and itches intensely. Eruptions red and moist during the increasing moon, pale and dry during the
decreasing moon. Patient has aversion to be washed. Aggravation while washing in cold water, in warmth
of bed, at night. Amelioration in open air, from scratching, from sweating.

Relationship
Autidotes : Bry., Camph.
