Nux Moschata 453

Female genital organs :

There is suppression of menses, or instead of menses, ther may be leucorrhoea, During menstrual
, the mouth, throat and fongue become intolerably dry especially when sleeping. The patient

awakes with absolutely dry tongue.

Quite often there may be discharge of flatus from vagina, Physometm (Lac. can., Lyco.,
Sanguinaria).

Pain, nausea and vomiting during pregnancy or from wearing pessaries. There may also be toothache
during pregnancy.

Frequently, Nux moschata is an infallible remedy for rheumatic affection, which may be caused by
getting feet wet, or from exposure to draughts of air while heated. Rheumatism of the left shoulder. (Fer.
met.). Rheumatic affections are aggravated in cold, wet weather or keeping in cold wet clothes.

Backache caused by riding in a carriage is also relieved by this remedy. The patient must lie down
after least exertion which causes much fatigue.
General Modalities :

Aggravation: Cold, wet.windy weather (Rhod.); weather changes, cold food, water and cold washing;
carriage driving (Cocculus, Sepia); lying on painful side (on painless side, Puls.).

Amelioration: In dry, warm weather, warm room; wrapping up warmly.

Relations :

Nux moschata antidotes mercurial inhalation, lead colic, oil of turpentine, spirituous liquors. and
especially the bad effects of beer.
