RAPHANUS

Radish NUSSER Cruciferae

INTRODUCTION

This is one of our new remedies. The tincture
is prepared from fresh root immediately before
flow-ering and also from the whole plant.

CLINICAL

Diarrhoea. Flatulence. Globus hystericus.
Hysteria. Insomnia—sexual. Nymphomania. Post
operative gas pains,

GUIDING SYMPTOMS

1) Incarceration of Flatulence é
Accumulation & retention of flatus, no flatus emitted upward or downward’.
Abdo-men distended, tympanitic, hard & painful to pressure especially
hypogasirium. Distension of abdomen, fol-lowed by griping as if stool would occur.
Burning in epigastrium followed by hot eructations.

2) Diarrhoea : Stool brown, liquid, frothy, profuse & expel-led with much force.
(Gambogia, croton tig.) It may be yellowish brown & extremely fluid with colic.

3) Urine: Turbid with yeast like sediment. Profuse, thick urine like milk.
4) Nymphomania causing sleeplessness with aversion to her own sex.

5) Mind : Mentally Raphanus patient is characterised by a great dislike to her own
sex. There is sadness with aver-sion to children especially girls. It is a good
medicine for hysteria with globus symptoms.
