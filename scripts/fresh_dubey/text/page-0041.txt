AGNUS CASTUS

Chaste Tree Verbenaceae

INTRODUCTION

Agnus Castus was used in early days by both
men & women to suppress the sexual desire and
hence homoeopathically it becomes a medicine for
sexual weakness with no desire & power, It
belongs to the same category as Selenium,
Staphysagria and Acid Phos, although properly
speaking it typifies a condition more desperate
than that presented by any one of those
remedies.

CLINICAL :

Agalactia, Impotency. Leucorrhoea. Sterility.
Gonorrhoea—secondary. Sprains.

Spheres of Action & Pathogenesis : Agnus produces its chief effect on sexual
organs of both the sexes. It lowers sexual vitality, with corresponding mental depression
and loss of nervous energy.

GUIDING SYMPTOMS : Factors ABA
1) Prematurely old age-from abuse of the sexual powers.

2) Complete impotence with relaxation, flaccidity, coldness of genitalia. No sexual
power or desire.

3) Impotency after repeated attacks of gonorrhoea.

4) Deficient secretion or suppression of milk in nursing women (Asaf., Lac.Can.,
Lac.Defl.)

5) Leucorrhoea transparent but Draining linen yellow; passes imperceptibly from the
Very relaxed parts.

6) Mind : Absent minded. Unable to recollect things. He has to read a sentence twice
before he can comprehend, He becomes melancholic apathetic, distracted from
abuse of sexual powers. Illusions of smells, herrings, musk. There is great sadness
with a fixed idea of approaching death. This fear is not of immediate death as with
Aconite, but the patient thinks it is sure to come after a while, and there is no use
in doing anything. Nervous debility in unmarried persons. Tachycardia caused by
tobacco in neurotic young men.

7) Ailments from—Sexual excesses. Repeated attacks of gonorrhoea or gleet. Sprains
or Overlifting.
