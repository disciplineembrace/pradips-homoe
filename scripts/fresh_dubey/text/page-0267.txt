15) Menses :

Causation : Suppressed from over exertion or being over heated (Getting wet-Puls.)

Time ; Too early.
Duration : Prolonged.
Quantity : Profuse.

Character : Black, membranous, clotted, flowless when moving about.

Concomitant :

(a) During menses : labour like pains from back to pubes ; mental symptoms are

better.

(b) After menses : swelling of breast with milk secretion.
(c) In those ladies who are blonde, pale and chlorotic.

Modalities-

Menses aggravate while sitting and lying, at night and relieved while walking.
16) Comparison between Cyclamen and Puisatilla.

Similarities :

(1) Both are predominantly womens’ remedies.
(2) Both are suited to blondes,—pale and anaemic.

(3) Both are thirstless.

(4) Both are weepy and dislike company.

(5) Both present changeability of mental symptoms.
(6) Both desire lemonades and to both pork disagrees.

(7) Both are. late sleepers.

Dissimilarites :

Cyclamen
1) Aversion to open air which aggravates
2) Mentally peevish, irritable & morose,

3) Aggravation from cold and relieved
by warmth in general.

4) Menses — Too early, too profuse black
and clotted.

5. Better during flow.

6. Secretions — neither thick, nor bland
nor yellowish-green

Pulsatilla
1) Craves open air which relieves
2) Mild, gentle, timid, yielding disposition

3) Relief from cold and aggravation
from warmth.

4) Menses — Too late, pale & scantly.

5) Worse during flow.
6) Secretion — Thick, bland &

yellowish-green
