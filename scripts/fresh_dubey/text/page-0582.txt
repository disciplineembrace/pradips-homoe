SPIGELIA

“Pink Root HAHNEMANN Loganiaceae

INTRODUCTION

The Demerara pink root, a grass from which
this drug has been prepared was used in the days
of Galen and Aristotle as a worm expeller
antihelminth. The leaves of this shrub was
pounded together with some sweetening agent and
was used in crude physiological doses to expel
round worms particularly in children. Hence
Master Hahnemann unto whom we owe the
introduction of this medicine to the Homoeopathic
system, named it Spigelia Anthelmic.

CLINICAL
Angina pectoris, Ciliary neuralgia, Cold, Constipation Depression of spirit, Eyes—
pain in, Glaucoma, Headache, Heart affections, Neuralgia, Toothache, Worms, etc.

SPHERES OF ACTION

Spigelia acts especially upon the fibrous and muscular tissues of eyes and heart. The
nerves especially the fifth Cranial nerve—Trigeminal nerve—is affected by this medicine.
The head, particularly the left side is affected.

When affecting the eyes—left being more common, it produces congestion, redness
and intolerable pain with dilated pupils; even the headache of left side centres on the left
eye. Heart produces violent contractions resulting in roaring heart. Trigeminal neuralgia
is one of the main pathogenetic effects when it affects cranial nerves. The fibrous tissues
produce a state of inflammation, rheumatic, arthritis, etc.

CONSTITUTION

Physical make-up: Spigelia is especially adapted to anaemic, debilitated subjects who
are brokendown, pale with light hair and yellow skin. In children who are afflicted with
ascarides and luimbrici.

Temperament; Nervous temperament.

Relation with heat and cold: Chilly patient. Even open air is unbearable.
Miasm: Psora is in the background.
Diathesis: Rheumatic diathesis.
