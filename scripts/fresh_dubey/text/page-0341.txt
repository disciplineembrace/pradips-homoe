lodum Baby :

Especially suited to those children who are dark and have black hair and eyes.
The baby is VERY HOT, rather the HOTTEST BABY, there is aggravation from warmth in general.

Baby has scrofulous diathesis. All glands of the body are enlarged. There is hypertrophy and
induration of glandular tissues, e.g., thyroid, testes, liver, spleen, lymph nodes of the abdomen, etc.

The baby has ravenous hunger; suffers from hunger, must eat every few hours. Anxious and worried
if ihe does not eat. Feels relieved while eating and when stomach is full.

The baby suffers from MARASMUS in spite of continuous eating. Great emaciation, progressiveh
increasing, The baby eats freely and will yet lose flesh all the time. There is withering throughout the baby.
The muscles dwindle. The skin shrinks and wrinkles and the face of the baby looks like that of an cid
person; but glands under the arm and groin are enlarged and hard.

The baby suffers from a bad type of constipation. The stool is of clay-coloured or white; constipation
is relieved by drinking cold milk.

There is also dry type of cough; child grasps the larynx while coughing.

General modalities :

Aggravation: Warmth, wrapping up the head. Motion and exertion of all kinds aggravate.
Amelioration: Walking about in open air, by eating.

Relations :
Complementary to: Lycopodium.
Acts best in goitre when given after full moon or when the moon is waning—Lippe.
Should not be given during lying in period, except in high potencies—Hering.
