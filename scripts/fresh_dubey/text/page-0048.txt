Aloe Socotrina 39

Dysentery :
There is inflammatory condition of lower portion of colon and rectum.

° Character of stool: Bloody discharge with much jelly-like mucus and less blood. Sometimes
the patient passes nothing but large quantity of jelly-like mucus only.

* Before stool: Rumbling sound in the abdomen which is full of gas.

* During stool: Cutting, griping pain in the lower portion of abdomen.
¢ After stool; Profound weakness and prostration.

® Modalities: Aggravation after eating and drinking.

Constipation :
® Causation: Sedentary life.

¢ Character of stool: Hard lump without any urging to stool. Solid stool and masses of mucus
pass involuntarily. There is entire loss of sensation in anus, no feeling during passage of stool.

Piles :
* Causation: Sedentary life and portal congestion.
* Character: Bleeding piles.
* Appearance: Like a bunch of grapes; bluish in colour.

* Character of pain: Constant bearing down pain in the recturny iwith intense itching and
burning in anus, preventing sleep. Very much tender, hot and sore with loose stool.

Agagravation: From heat.
Amelioration: By cold application.

General Modalities :

Aggravation: Early in the morning, sedentary life, hot dry weather, after eating and drinking,
standing or walking.
Amelioration: Cold water, cold weather, discharge of flatus and stool.

Relations :

Complementary to Sulphur. It is antidoted by Sulphur, but it resembles Sulphur in many syrnptoms
and is of equal importance with Sulphur in chronic diseases with abdominal plethora.
