Urine :

Great tenesmus of bladder. The patient sits for a long time to urinate. Frequent urging to urinate.
If the desire is not attended to, there is sensation of congestion in the chest. Urine is milky, scanty and
very hot.

Heart :

All the heart symptoms are associated with and arise as reflex symptoms of uterine troubles.

Sensation: As if the heart was grasped in a vise (Cactus) as if all the blood has rushed to the heart
which feels and seems like bursting.

Great palpitation with fluttering, faint, hurried and anxious feeling about the apex.

Pain: Sharp pain in left chest, shifting in nature, flying from one part to another and can be covered
with tip of a finger.

Pain in the cardiac region with feeling of load on chest.
Pulse : Pulse is very much rapid and irregular, with rapid heart beat, 150 to 170 per minute.
Modality: Aggravation after eating, lying on either side.
Concomitants: Pulsation all over the body. Full distended feeling, as if blood would burst through the
vessels. Heart symptoms alternate with uterine troubles.
Sweat :
There is coldness of the whole body, extremities cold and covered with cold sweat.

General Modalities :
Aggravation: By consolation and in a warm room.
Amelioraton: In open fresh air.

Relations :
Compare: Cactus, Murex, Sepia.
Antidoted by: Platina, Nux v., Puls.
