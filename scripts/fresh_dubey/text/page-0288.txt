INTRODUCTION

Euphrasia is called, in common language,
“THE EYEBRIGHT”. Its name is significant and
a most appropriate one as is seen in both human
provings and clinical practice. According to
Grauvogl the “signature” of “a black spot in the
coralla which looks like the pupil” marked
Euphrasia as an eye medicine to the ancients
and the Homoeopathic experiments have fully,
confirmed its old time reputation.

CLINICAL

Cold, Cough, Cataract, Conjunctivitis, Eye
affection, Fevers, Headache, Influenza, Lachrymation, Measles, Menstrual troubles, Nasal

catarrh, Nose affection, etc.

SPHERES OF ACTION
Especially acts upon the mucous membrane, particularly of the eyes, lids and upper
portion of the respiratory tract.

It produces catarrhal inflammation of the mucous membrane, inflaming the
conjunctival membranes, especially causing PROFUSE ACRID LACHRYMATION which

is the chief characteristic of the drug.

GUIDING SYMPTOMS

1. Catarrhal affections of mucous membrane especially of the eyes, nose and
upper respiratory tract.

2. Profuse acrid lachrymation with profuse and bland coryza (rev.—Alium
cepa),

3. The eyes water all the time and are agglutinated in morning

4. Cough only in the daytime (Fer. met. Staphysagria).

5. Menses only one day or lasting only for one hour.

6. Profuse offensive expectoration of mucus by voluntary hawking.

7. Bad effects from fall, contusion, or mechanical injuries of external parts of the eye,

8. Aggravation in the evening, WARMTH and indoors,
