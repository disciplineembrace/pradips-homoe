* Symptoms during meningitis; There is stupefying stunning headache from the neck to the head
with heat and pulsation. Head is inclined to bend backward, boring the head into the pillow. There is
congestion and burning sensation in the head and brain. Spasmodic distortion of face and lips, grinding
of teeth, with inclination to bite. There may also be irritability and acuteness of the sense of taste, smell,
touch, sight and hearing.

Urine retained or there may be involunatary micturition.

Skin : Skin shows a uniform, smooth, shining scarlet redness. Skin is dry, hot and burning,
imparts a burning sensation to examining hand. Painful sensitiveness of the skin to contact. There may
be eruptions over the skin. The eruptions resemble measles or scarlatina. Erysipelatous inflammation is
also found.

Boils : In boils, Belladonna must be thought of in first stage before the pus formation takes
place. The indications are, “REDNESS, SWELLING and HEAT of the part, THROBBING pain
SENSITIVE TO TOUCH, aggravation from touch and amelioration by application of heat.”

Prolapse : There is sensation of pressing downward as if contents of the abdomen would push out
through the vulva. Worse in the morning and relieved by standing and sitting erect.

General Modialities :

Aggravation: From touch, motion, noise, draught of air, looking at bright shining objects, after 3
p.m., night, after mid-night, while drinking, uncovering, the head, summer sun, lying down, least jar and

motion and stror smell.
Ameliorarion: Rest, standing or sitting erect, in warm room.

Relation :
Complementary to: Calcarea.
Belladonna is the acute of Calcarea, which is often required to complete a cure.
It follows well: Ars.alb., Cham., Hepar sulph. and Merc. sol.
Is followed well by: Dulc., Hepar sulph. Hyos. Lach. and Rhus. tox.
Antidotes: Coffea and Hyos.
