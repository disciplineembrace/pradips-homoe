Calcarea Carbonica 155

Bone Symptoms : The bone symptoms of Calcarea carb. are very striking and ,zominent. These
symptoms are marked mostly in RICKETY CHILDREN.

Bones are soft and weak and they DEVELOP VERY SLOWLY. The bones are unable to hold the
weight of the bulky, flabby body and, therefore, OFTEN CURVATURES OF BONES, especially of spine
and long bones occur. The extremities are crooked, deformed and bones are irregularly developed.
Pathological fracture of the bones, especially of long bones is a frequent phenomenon. Since the
bones are weak, there is late learning to walk. The HEAD IS LARGE AND THE FONTANELLES
AND SUTURES REMAIN OPEN WITH DELAYED OSSIFICATION. Teeth do not appear in time and
consequently there is difficult and DELAYED DENTITION IN CHILDREN. Calcium and phosphorus are
deficient in bones.

Bechr claims that Calcarea is superior to any other medicine in caries of vertebrae.

Calcarea carb.—Old man : Old man with fatty degeneration of heart (Phos,. Aurum met., Kali
carb.). They suffer from palpitation and dyspnoea on little exertion. Pyaemic state with cold abscesses in
deep muscles (Merc., sol.)

Calcarea carb—Young man : He GROWS VERY RAPIDLY with tubercular infiltration especially
of the upper lobe of the right lung along with other characterstic symptoms of the drug.

Calcarea carb.—Young girl : She too GROWS RAPIDLY and suffers from anaemia and chlorosis
and other characteristic menstrual symptoms, with typical constitution and sweat with coldness.

Calcarea carb.—Baby : The child is FAIR, FATTY and FLABBY. Face is red and muscles are
flabby.
The head is large and the abdomen is swollen and looks like an inverted saucer.

The bones are weak and soft. They develop very slowly. The weak bone is unable to sustain and hold
the weight of the body and, therefore, there is LATE LEARNING TO WALK. THE FONTANELLES AND
SUTURES REMAIN OPEN FOR A LONG TIME. There is delayed ossification. There are curvatures of
the bones, especially of spine and long bones. The extremities are crooked and deformed, bones develop
irregularly. There te DIFFICULT AND DELAYED DENTITION. Teeth do not appear in time.

Usually Calc. carb. child is constipated but there maybe sour and offensive diarrhoea during
dentition with sour vomiting. The whole body smells sour. So also all excretions. The mile-stones of the
baby’s life are delayed. The baby is also somewhat deficient and mentally idiotic.

The baby sweats profusely and takes cold readily as a consequence. The sweat is well-marked in the
BACK OF THE HEAD, NECK OR CHEST and upper part of the body. HEAD SWEATS PROFUSELY
WHILE SLEEPING, WETTING THE PILLOW FAR AROUND (next to Calc.—Sil.).

The baby has tubercular diathesis with enlarged glands. The baby wants to be magnetised (Phos.)

General Modality :
Aggravation: Cold air, wet weather, cold water, from washing, in morning, during full moon.
Amelioration: Lying on painful side (Bryonia., Puls.).

Relations :
It antidotes: Bismuth, Dig., Nitric acid, Nux vom. and Sulphur.
Antidoted by: Acetic acid, China, Nitric acid, Camphor and Ipec.
Calcarea is the chronic of Belladonna.
Complementary to: Belladonna.
