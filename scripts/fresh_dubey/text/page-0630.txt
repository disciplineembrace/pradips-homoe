standing of its symptomatology one should
consider the physiology of the gland. The
administration of thyroid gland in large
doses acclerates protein and fat metabolism
and causes increased elimination of
nitrogen, phosphorus, chlorine ete.

tre-mor, emaciation and glycosuria are
observed. The removal of the thyroid gland
