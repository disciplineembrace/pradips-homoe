Female genital organs :
Menses : Euphrasia is suitable for amenorrhoea and dysmenorroea.
Time: Late or may be regular.
Quantity: Scanty.
Character of menses: Very much painful.

The menstrual flow lasts ONLY ONE DAY or sometimes only one hour with PROFUSE ACRID
LACHRYMATION AND PROFUSE BLAND CORYZA.

Fever : It is indicated especially in eruptive fever like measles.
Stage: Suitable in the first stage of measies.
Time: The fever occurs mostly during the day.

During fever, there is chill with rash. There is profuse lachrymation with profuse bland coryza. The
eyes water all the time. Fever is often associated with catarrhal headache as if the head is going to burst
which is more in the evening.

There is loose cough with rattling in chest and copious expectoration.
Look of face: The face is very much hot with coldness of body.
Sweat: Sweat especially at night, during sleep:, most profuse on chest.
Aggravation: In the evening, after exposure to south wind.

Note : Euphrasia is a wonderful medicine in measles though not so frequently indicated as Pulsatilla.
But if it is given when the symptoms agree, it will back a violent attack of measles and tur it into a very
simple form, making the patient feel better, brings out eruption, control the fever and relieve the cough,
coryza and other catarrhal symptoms.
General Modality :

Aggravation: In the evening, in bed, indoors, warmth, moisture, after exposure to south wind
and whery touched.

Amelioration: From coffee and in darkness.

Relations :
Antidoted by: Pulsatilla.
