INTRODUCTION

Hydrastis is a slow and deep acting remedy.
The mother tincture is prepared from fresh root of
the plant which is grown.abundantly in the United
States of America. It is called “Yellow Root” or
“Indian Turmeric” and thrives in various parts of
India. It has got particular affinity for the LIVER
AND GASTRIC AFFECTIONS resulting in poor
digestion and obstinate constipation. The power of
Hydrastis over SMALL POX is seen in modifying
the disease, abolishing its distressing symptoms,
shortening its course, lessening its severity and
greatly mitigating its bad effects and completely protecting the patient from its dangerous
consequence. This remedy is required in many trophic disturbances where there is emaciation,
catarfhal condition and ulceration, even malignant ulcerations.

CLINICAL

Alcoholism, Asthma, Cancer, Catarrh, Constipation, Corns, Dyspepsia, Ear affections,
Fistula, Gonorrhoea, Gastric affections, Haemorrhoids, Jaundice, Liver affection,
Leucorrhoea, Throat troubles, Ulcer, etc.

SPHERES OF ACTION

It acts on mucous membrane, especially of respiratory tract, gastro-intestinal tract,
glands, liver and skin.
PATHOGENESIS

Acting on mucous membrane, it relaxes them and produces a THICK, YELLOWISH,
ROPY, AND STRINGY, VISCID SECRETION, particularly in the catarrhal conditions,
which may be anywhere from the body. It produces a malignant or CACHECTIC
DYSCRASIA and also alters the course and severity of small-pox.

CONSTITUTION

Make-up of the body: Hydrastis is specially suited to old, easily TIRED PEOPLE,
cachectic persons with great debility and scrofulous constitution, which has been broken
down by alcoholism. The skin has a jaundiced look, mottled and puckered, with yellowish
white colour of the face.

Temparament: Lymphatic temperament.
