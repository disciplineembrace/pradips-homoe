HELLEBORUS NIGER

" Christmas Rose HAHNEMANN Ranunculaceae

INTRODUCTION

Helleborus is yet another of our Master’s
immortal monuments derived from the vegetable
kingdom—Chirstmas Rose, belonging to the
natural order of Ranunculaceae. This is called
Christmas Rose because the flowers bloom in
mid-winter. The mother tincture is prepared from
the dried root. In its crude form and in
physiological dose, it is poisonous but when it is
potentised, the poisonous effect is transformed
into a wonderful dynamic power capable of curing
brain, urinary and abdonimal symptoms.

CLINICAL

Apoplexy, Brain affection, Cholera, Concussion, Convuilsion, Diarrhoea, Drospy,
Headache, Hydrocephalus, Meningitis, Tetanus, Typhoid, Urinary affection, etc.

SPHERES OF ACTION

The main seats of action of Helleborus are brain, nervous system, meninges, kidney,
serous membrane, urinry organs and gastro-intestinal tract.

Acting on brain and meninges it produces dropsical effusion leading to hydrocephalus
and meningitis and many other reflex symptoms. It acts on kidney resulting in scanty
and high coloured albuminous urine; it irritates the digestive tract causing inflammation
of stomach and intestine thereby producing jelly-like mucus and diarrhoea. It also causes
cerebral and general paralysis and through pneumogastric nerve, respiration is retarded
and there may be cardiac paralysis.

CONSTITUTION

Physical make-up: It is more suited to weak and delicate persons, especially children
who are prone to brain troubles with serous effusion.

Temperament: Irritable temperament.
Relation with heat and cold; Chilly patient.
Miasm : Psora is in the background.
