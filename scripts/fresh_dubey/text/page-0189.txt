GUIDING SYMPTOMS

1) Diseases of elderly persons with marked venous plethora, blue cheeks, blue lips and
great debility.

2) Aversion to open, dry, cold air.

3) Circulation feeble, Stagnated and vital heat sinks to a minimum.

4) After appearance of menses so weak, she can hardly speak. (Alum. Coc.).

5) Menstruation, leucorrhoea, diarrhoea are all exhausting. (Ars.-are all offensive
Pgor.).

6) Glands : indurated, swollen, painful; in neck, axillae, groin, Mammae ; pains
lancinating, cutting, burning (Con.)

7) Easily strained from lifting, even small weights ; straining and overlifting easily
produce great debility ; ankles turn when walking.

8) Joints weak : easily sprained by slight exertion (Led.)

9) Hearing confused ; cannot tell from what direction a sound comes.
