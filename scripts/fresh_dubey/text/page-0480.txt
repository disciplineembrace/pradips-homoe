INTRODUCTION

Phosphoric acid is another of Hahnemann’s immortal
jegacies in Materia Medica Pura. He gives direction for its
preparation and for its potentization upto the trillionth fold
dilution. It is an invaluable remedy well deserving the name
NERVE TONIC says Dr. Hughes in his Pharmacodynamics.
Like all other acids, it has marked debility and prostration.

CLINICAL

Asthma, Brain-fag, Cough, Debility, Diabetes, Diarrhoea,
Enteric fever, Flatulence, Gout, Headache, Locomotor ataxia,
Mental weakness, Perspiration, Disorders of pregnancy,
Rheumatism, Sexual organs, Spermatorrhoea, Urinary disorders,
Vertigo, etc.

SPHERES OF ACTION

It acts on brain, nerves, blood, kidneys, mucous membrane, sex organs and bones.

PATHOGENESIS

It produces both mental and physical debility but the mental symptoms are first to
develop. The remedy runs from mental to physical, from brain to muscles (In Muriatic
acid, the muscular prostration comes first and the mind seems to be clear until long after
the muscles are prostrated i.e. debility runs from body to mind). It also produces
haemorrhage, digestive disorders and inflammation of bones.

CONSTITUTION

Make-up of the body: It is best suited to persons of originally strong constitution, who
have become debilitated BY LOSS OF VITAL FLUIDS, sexual excess, violent acute
diseases, chagrin or long succession of moral emotion, grief, care or disappointed
affection, Pale sickly complexion, eyes sunken and surrounded by blue margins.

Temperament: Mild disposition and yielding temperament.
Relation with heat and cold: Chilly patient.
Miasms: Psora, syphilis, sycosis are in the background.

GUIDING SYMPTOMS

1. Patients who were originally strong but have become debilitated due to LOSS OF
VITAL FLUIDS, sexual excess, violent acute diseases, chagrin or long continued
mental emotions such as grief, care and disappointed love.
