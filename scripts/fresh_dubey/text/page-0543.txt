RUTA GRAVEOLENS

Rue HAHNEMANN Rutaceae

INTRODUCTION

Amongst many remedies in our Materia
Medica, for injuries caused by mechanical devices
and appliances, Ruta is one possessing prime
importance. This is prepared from a shrub-like
plant indigenous in Europe and is widely
cultivated in the gardens. The juice of the plant is
capable of causing irritation and inflammation of
the skin when touched. The mother tincture is
prepared from the fresh herb collected before
blooming. The irritating and inflammatory
character of the plant is transformed into an
excellent therapeutic property after undergoing
the process of potentization.

CLINICAL

Amblyopia, Bone diseases, Bruises, Chest affections, Constipation, Enuresis, Eye
affections, Facial paralyasis, Fracture, Haemorrhage, Injury, Prolapse of Uterus,
Rheumatism, Sciatica, etc.
SPHERES OF ACTION

It acts on bones, periosteum, joints, eyes, urinary bladder, uterus and skin.

Its action on the bones, periosteum and joints produces symptoms of a rheumatic
nature, periostitis and exostosis; in the eyes, errors of xaccommodation and vision, also
causes prolapse of rectum, urinary troubles, metrorrhagia and miscarriage.

CONSTITUTION

There is no particular constitution of the patient as such. However, there are
scrofulous exostosis and warts, especially on the palm of the hand.

Relation with heat and cold: Chilly patient.
Miasms: Psora and sycosis are in the background,

1. Sore, lame, bruised feeling over the entire body, as after a blow, fall or any sort of
mechanical injury especially of bones and periosteum.
