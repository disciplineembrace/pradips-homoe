Agnus Castus 33

PARTICULARS

Male sexual Organs :

It has more marked action on male sexual organs than female sexual organs. Aqnus should be
thought of when the sexual life is entirely annihilated due to abuses & excesses of former years and due
to repeated attacks of gonorrhoea in the past. The patient becomes a complete impotent with
relaxation, coldness, flaccidity of the genitalia. There is no sexual power and desire, even voluptuous
fancies excite no erection. It is also medicine for secondary gonorrhoea. Gleety discharge with absence of
sexual desire & erections. Testes cold, swollen, hard & painful.

Female sexual Organs :

Breast symptoms : Agalactia—There is deficient secretion or suppression of milk in nursing women
(Asaf., Lac. Can., Lac. Defl.) This condition is often associated with great sadness & an idea of

approaching death.
Leucorrhoea : There is transparent, imperceptible discharge from the very relaxed parts which

stains the linen yellow.

Sterility : This remedy is very good for sterility if a woman had indulged extensively in secretvice. She
has no sexual desire, aversion to coition, sexual thrill is absent with suppression of menses.

In sterility Agnus Castus can be compared with Borax, Lycopodium, Nat. Carb, Platina and Sepia.
Borax : In Borax hot, acrid leucorrhoea causes death of sperms thereby preventing pregnancy.
Lycopodium :Coition is prevented by dryness of vagina (Nat Mur., Lyssin).

Nat Carb :There is discharge of mucus from vagina after an embrace causing sterility.

Platina : Here coition is prevented by extreme sensitiveness of the vagina (Thuja).

Sepia :In Sepia also, there is aversion to opposite sex, to coition, even young newly married Sepia
lady has no desire for intercourse. Vagina painful especially on coition
