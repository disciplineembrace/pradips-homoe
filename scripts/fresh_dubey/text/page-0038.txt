INTRODUCTION

Agaricus muscarius is a gift of vegetable
kingdom. It is very much suited to nervous
affections of old people, drunkards who often
become delirious and get epileptiform fit.
Agaricus finds a respectable place in the Materia
Medica as an excellent remedy for neruous

affections.

CLINICAL

Bad effects of alcohol Chilblains, Chorea,
Delirium, Debauch, Epilepsy, Enteric Fever,
Gangrene, Headache, Itching, Lumbago, Prolapse,
Rheumatism, Skin affections, Sexual excess,
Suppressed eruption, etc.

SPHERES OF ACTION
It energetically acts upon nerves, mind, brain,

circulation, skin and mucous membrane.

PATHOGENESIS

It irritates nerves and brain producing various delirious conditions, epileptiform fit
and exaggeration of nervous functions other reflex symptoms.

Constitution :

. is best adapted to old people with weak indolent circulation, light hair, lax skin and
muscles,

Temperament: Nervous temperament.
Relation with heat and cold: Chilly patient.
Miasm: Psora and Syphilis are in the background.

GUIDING SYMPTOMS
1. Delirium with constant raving.
2. Involuntary movements while awake; spasmodic motion and chorea.
3. Uncertainty in walking; stumbles over everything on the way.
4. Twitching and trembling run through the whole remedy.
