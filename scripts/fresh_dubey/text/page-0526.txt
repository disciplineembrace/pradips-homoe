ranunculus Bulbosus 517

 ——e

Herpes zoster appearing with, preceded or followed by intercostal neuralgia. The vesicles may have a
bluish appearance with great burning.
Corns on soles of feet are very sensitive to touch, with smarting and burning pain.

Modality: Aggravation: From touch and from cold air.

General Modalities :

Aggravation: In open air, MOTION (slightest movement), contact, atmospheric changes, wet stormy
weather and in evening. Cold air brings on all sorts of ailments. Aggravation during inspiration and lying

Amelioration: By sitting rather bent forward and leaning a little to the left.

Relations :
Incompatible: Alcohol, Wine, Sulph. Staph.
Antidoted by: Bry., Carmph., Puls., Rhus.
Ranunculus bulbosus is a remedy for the writers’ cramp and professional neurosis.

Bryonia has stitching pain relieved by lying on affected side whereas the pain is aggravated by lying
on affected side in the case of Ranunculus bulbosus.
