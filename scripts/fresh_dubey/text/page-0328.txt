Hyoscyamus, one of the TRIO OF

Poisoning, provings and _ experience of

Hyoscyamus is said to be especially poisonous to fowls, hence the name, but for other
animals, it acts like a purgative. Old school uses Hyoscyamus as a “Cerebral depressant”
in acute mania, delirium tremens, febrile delirium and insomnia. It is mostly used by
them in asylum practice.

CLINICAL

Angina Pecforis, Bronchitis, Brain affections, Chorea, Coma vigil, Cough, Delirum,
Diarrhoea, Enteric fever, Epilepsy, Haemorrhage, Headache, Mania, Meningitis,
Neuralgia, Paralysis, Pneumonia, Urinary disorders etc.

SPHERES OF ACTION

Hyoscyamus acts upon the nervous system, sensorium, muscular system, mucous
membrane and skin.

PATHOGENESIS

It irritates nerve centres, producing increased cerebral activity, but non-
inflammatory in type, delirium, hysteria and delirium tremens. It also irritates mucous
membranes and skin. Its pathogenetic action besides producing epilepsy, melancholia
and mania also covers coma vigil. Non-inflammatory increase of cerebral activity may
also be noticed in a few cases. It produces a sensation of fulness and heat of the head
With a flushed face without pronounced hyperaemia.

CONSTITUTION

Physical make-up: Like other members of the TRIO, Hyoscyamus too is more or less
an acute drug and as such, consideration of constitution is not of much importance.
However, these patients are usually light-haired with pale or bluish face.
