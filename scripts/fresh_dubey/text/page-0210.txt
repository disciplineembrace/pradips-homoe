INTRODUCTION

Chamomilla is an annual wind plant growing
wild in cultivated fields amongst wheat and
paddy. But finally it serves the purpose of an
excellent remedy for all ailments BROUGHT ON
OR AGGRAVATED BY ANGER. Just as the
weed grows in a paddy field causing untold
miseries, anger and pain to the farmers and
grows at the cost of a useful product so also the
patient displays similar manifestations and .
develops uncivilised habits. But when the whole ——
plant is crushed, mother tincture is prepared and
subsequent potencies are succussed, it becomes a wonderful remedy capable of
annihilating many diseases, especially of children, when given on the basis of symptom.
similarity. It is an “ANGER AND PAIN REMEDY” which causes TURMOIL IN
TEMPERAMENT.
