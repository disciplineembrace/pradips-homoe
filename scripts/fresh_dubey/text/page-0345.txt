General Modalities : .
Aggravation: Periodically, winter, dry weather and least motion.
Amelioration : By rest, pressure and closing eyes.

Relations :
Complementary to: Cuprum met.

Compare: With Antim. tart, Ipecac has more nausea, clean tongue, but Antim. tart, has mere
retching and vomiting and thick coated tongue.

It antidotes: Arn., Ars., Nux vom.
Antidoted by: Alum., Apis., Amn., Ars., Dulc., Ferrum., Op., Sulph., Ant. tart.
