COCCULUS

“Cocculus Indicus HAHNEMANN Menispermacene
Indian Cockle

INTRODUCTION

For this poisonous’ vegetable plant
Hahnemann says, “This vegetable substance,
used hitherto only for destroying some noxious
agents and for stupefying fishes so that they may
be caught by hand, was first employed by myself
as a medicine”. It is the picrotoxin alkaloid
content of Cocculus which possesses the toxic
effect when given in crude, form and serves the
therapeutic purpose when administered in
dynamic doses.

CLINICAL

Brain affection, Cholera, Colic, Convulsion,
Fevers, Headache, Leucorrhoea, Memory weak, Menstrual troubles, Mental excitement,
Paralysis, Rheumatism, Sea-sickness, Sleep affection, from loss of sleep, Somnolence,
Vertigo Vomiting etc.

SPHERES OF ACTION

It chiefly affects the cerebro-spinal system, voluntary muscular system and
sensorium.
PATHOGENESIS

Acting on the cerebro-spinal system, it produces great debility. It causes paralytic
weakness of the spine and especially of its motor nerves resulting in weakness of the
legs. Knees give out when walking. Soles of feet feel as if asleep. Convulsions, both clonic
and tonic are a special characteristic of its action. It produces vomiting of cerebral origin
rather than gastric.

CONSTITUTION

Physical make-up: Especially suited for women and children with light hair and eyes,
who suffer severely Curing menstruation and pregnancy, unmarried and childless
women. Also adopted to book-worms, sensitive romantic girls with irregular
menstruation, onanist and persons debilitated by sexual excesses,

Temperament: Mind and sluggish temperament, nervous persons.
Relation with heat and cold: As a whole there is aggravation from sun.
Miasm: Psora is in the background.
