Text Book of MATERIA MEDICA

9. Tissues

Abscess, easily bleeding after matter has begun to form, chief remedy, to
promote the discharge of pus (Kali mur., before matter forms).

Swellings of the glands (if Kali mur. does not abort them and matter has
formed).

Suppurating glands, with thick, yellow, offensive discharge of matter.
Scrofulous, enlarged glands.

Neglected cases of injury, with suppuration.

Ulceration and caries of bone.

10. Modalities : Symptoms are always worse at night and during full moon, in the open
air. Worse from suppressed foot sweat or chilling of the feet. Better by heat and in
warm room.

CLINICAL USE

Silicea is useful in the following diseases or conditions, where the above general
conditions are present :

Chronic and acute suppurations and ulcerations

Fistula, boils, carbuncles, tonsillitis, glandular swellings, whitlows, indurations,
scrofula, styes on eyelids, caries and necrosis of the bones, gum-boils on the jaw,
abscess of the labia, with tendency to fistulous openings, fistulous ulcers of the
breast, lachrymal fistula, suppurative otitis media, ulcerations of the throat with
thick, yellow discharge

Diseases resulting from suppressed foot sweats
Constipation

Fetid foot-sweat

Ozena

Chronic cough and cold

Glandular swellings which become hard and threaten to suppurate
Epilepsy especially nocturnal and change of moon
Rheumatism

Gouty deposits.

Chronic dyspepsia

Skin unhealthy and easily suppurates

Nails dry and brittle

Falling of hair

Atrophy of parts

Headache
