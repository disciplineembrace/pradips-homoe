INTRODUCTION

If Opium is the painless remedy, Oxal ac. may
be considered its antithesis : it causes pains
excruciating beyond description, and it has this
grand keynote distinction, that its pains are all
aggravated when the patient thinks of them. And
not only that, thinking of pains and conditions will
bring them on when they are not actually present.

SPHERE OF ACTION
It acts on digestive tract, genitourinary organs, spinal cord and nerves and left lung.

PATHOGENESIS

It is both corrosive and true poison. It causes pains excruciating beyond description,
corrosion of passages, destruction of mucous membrane. It influences the spinal cord,
and produces motor paralysis, coldness of surface, numbness, mottled skin, blue
nails are present in all degrees of its action and are leading notes for its use. It
may even cause death by exhaustion.
