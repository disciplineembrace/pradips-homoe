ARGENTUM METALLICUM

Pure Silver HAHNEMANN The Metal

INTRODUCTION

Argentum metallicum is prepared from
the precipitated Silver. It is mainly a left-
sided remedy. Almost all the heavy metals
have been proved to be excellent remedies
after the process of potentisation and so does
the Silver too. Hahnemann.was shrewd
enough to understand the curative value of
this metal and enunciated the process for
the benefit of all generations of mankind.

CLINICAL

Brain-fag, Diabetes, Exosfosis, Heart
affections, Laryngitis, Loss of voice, Male genital organs, Prolapse of uterus, Rheumatism,
Throat affections, etc.

SPHERES OF ACTION

The main centres of action of this remedy are mucous membrane of throat and
respiratory tract; affects all cartilages, and hence all joints and bones; cartilages of ear,
nose, euslachian tube, male and female genital organs, etc.

PATHOGENESIS

It causes irritation of mucous membrane of throat and respiratory tract, producing
hoarseness and loss of voice and cough; it also produces sexual weakness in male and
prolapse with pain and haemorrhge in female. As it affects the cartilages, bones and
joints it produces bruised pain, tenderness and weakness.

CONSTITUTION
Make-up of the body: Usually the patient is tall and thin, with constitutional effects
of onanism and abuse of mercury.

Temperament: Irritable temperament.
Miasm: Syphilis is in the background.

GUIDING SYMPTOMS

1. Hoarseness of professional singers and public speakers with total loss of voice.

2. Laughing excites cough (Drosera, Phosphorus, Stannum), and produces profuse
mucus in larynx.

3. Great weakness of the chest (Stannum).
