Glandular Symptoms

Spongia causes enlargement and hardness of the gland?. It has got special action on Thyroid gland
& testes.

Thyroid

It is an infallible remedy in cases of goitre with swelling and induration of thyroid gland. Thyroid gland
is swollen even with chin; with suffocative paroxysms at night. The patient may awaken in a fright and
feels as if suffocating; as if he had to breathe through a sponge.

Testes : It is one of the best remedies for. chronic orchitis esp. after maltreatment and suppressed
gonorrhoea. Tests are swollen with bruised, squeezed & swollen, painful spermatic cord.

Heart symptoms :

It is a great remedy in angina pactoris and organic diseases of heart, such as Valvular insufficiency
and hypertrophy of heart where the symptoms are as follows : in angina pec-toris there is contractig pain,
heat, faintnes, suffocation, anxiety & sweat; worse after midnight. The patient wakes up suddenly after
midnight with pain, suffocation and great anxiety.

Cardiac symptoms are usually attended with gasping respiration and asthmatic symptoms. Violent
palpitation with pain before or during menses.

General modalities :

Aggravation : At night, after sleep, cold air, cold food and drinks, lying with head low. Amelioration
: Warm food and drink.
