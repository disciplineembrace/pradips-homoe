Temperature of the body is not high but has constant sticky profuse sweat.

Weak and small pulse, frequent and intermitting. There is dryness of mouth,, throat; gray-white
coating on the tongue with red streak in the middle.

Concomitants: Ecchymosis, bluish red spots on the parts on which the patient lies. Muttering delirium
with dim sleepy eyes. Miliary rashes appear first upon neck and then on the back, chest and at last upon

the feet.
Modalities:
* Aggravation by exertion, talking and loss of vital fluid.
* Amelioration: From keeping warm and from warmth of bed.

General Modalities :

Aggravation: From mental affections, loss of vital fluids,especially seminal, self abuse, sexual excess,
talking causes weakness in chest (Stannum).

Amelioration: By sitting, standing and warmth of bed.

Relations :
Phosphoric acid acts well before or after China in colliquative sweats, diarrhoea and debility.

After Nux. in fainting after a meal.
