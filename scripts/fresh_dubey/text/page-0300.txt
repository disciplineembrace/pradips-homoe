GELSEMIUM

Yellow Jesamine W.E,. PAYNE Loganiaceae

—

INTRODUCTION

Gelsemium is said to be the “KING
OF POLIO”, by virtue of its being a great
paralyser. It is a drug of importance in
Homoeopathy not for its great number of
symptoms but due to its well marked and
clearly characteristic symptoms which
are very often met within our day to day
practice.

CLINICAL

Ataxia, Brain affection, Cerebro
spinal meningitis, Cold, Cough, Constipation, Convulsion, Epilepsy, Dengue fever,
Diarrhoea, Diphteria, Eye affections, Fevers, Headache, Heart affection, Hysteria,
Locomotor Ataxia, Menstrual disorders, Myalgia, Neuralgia, Paralysis, Rheumatism,
Sleep disordered, Tremor, Urinary affection, Vertigo, Writer's cramp, etc.

SPHERES OF ACTION

It acts on brain, spinal cord, occiput, neck, motor nerves, eye lids, vision, mucus
membrane, sexual organs and blood. It is a right-sided remedy.

PATHOGENESIS

Acting on the roots of motor cerebral nerve centres, motor area or the spinal cord and
both voluntary and involuntary motor nerves, Gels, produces a general state of paresis,
mental and physical. This is followed by cutaneous anaesthesia. The mind is sluggish
and stupefied; the whole muscular system is relaxed; head and limbs feel heavy; even
ptosis and diplopia may occur. Muscles refuse to obey the order of the will. It has
functional paralysis of all descriptions. Tremor i. the key-note of this great remedy, and
gradually total paralysis may take place, Hence, Gels, is said to be a GREAT
PARALYSER.

important. But is usually more suited to children, old people and anaemic girls.

Temperament: Nervous and hysterical temperament.
