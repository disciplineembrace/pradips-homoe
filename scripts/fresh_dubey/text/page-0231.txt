COCA

Erythroxylon Coca CLOTAR MULLER Linae

INTRODUCTION

Coca is used by the natives of South
America as we use coffee, tea and tobacco. So it
is like tea and coffee in arresting tissue
changes and enabling those who take it to
undergo unusual fatigues. It contains an
alkaloid called cocaine,

CLINICAL

Angina Pectoris. Asthma. Cough. Debility.
Dyspnoea. Carries of teeth. Insomnia. Mountain
sickness. Nervous exhaustion.

GUIDING SYMPTOMS

1) The mountaineers’ remedy—Useful in complaints incidental to mountain
climbing such as palpitation, dyspnoea, anxiety & insomnia.

2) Suited to persons wearing out under the physical and mental strain of a busy life,
and who suffer from exhausted nerves & brain (Fl.Acid., Kali Phos., Nux Vom.)

3) Shortness of breath in old people and in those who use tobacco and whisky in excess.
4) Want of breath in those engaged in athletic sports.
5) Longing for alcoholic liquors and tobacco for the accustomed stimulants.

6) Violent palpitation : From incarcerated flatus (Arg.N., Nux V.) from over exertion.
from heart strain (Arn., Bor., Caust.)

7) Sleepy but can find no rest anywhere.

8) Mind : Coca patients are extremely bashful & timid. They are always ill at ease in
society. Melancholic from nervous exhaustion. They are sad, irritable & delights in
solitude and obscurity. They lose all sense of right and wrong.

9) Ailments : Bad effects : From mountain climbing or ballooning (Ars.) Bad effects
of stimulants, alcohol, tobacco.

General Modalities :

Aggravation : From Ascending, in high attitudes. Amelioration—From wine, from riding in open
air.
