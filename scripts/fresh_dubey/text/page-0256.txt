CONSTITUTION

Physical make-up: Especially indicated in strumous, debilitated, broken down

constitution, particularly in drunkards with tendency to carbuncles or blood boils.

Temperament: choleric temperament.

Relation with heat and cold: Affected by heat, wants open air.
Miasm: Psora is in the background.

Diathesis: HAEMORRHAGIC DIATHESIS.
