Inebriates (Crot. H. Eup.): Habitual drunkards.

Ingrowing toe nail (Nit. Ac.+ Sil) A toe nail, one edge of which is overgrown by the nail fold,
producing a pyrogenic granuloma.

Insular patches (Nat. M.) Patches of the nature of an island,
Intersecting rings (Sen.) Rings that cut each other.

Interstitial inflammation (Phos. Ac.) Inflammation in a small space, gap or hole in the
substance of an organ or tissue.

Intussusception (Plum.) A condition in which one part of the bowel slips into the lower part,
causing interstitial obstruction. It occurs most commonly in infants.

Inveterate (Nit. Ac.) Obstinate.

Iracible (Ma V.) Irritable, Hot tempered.
Iridectomy(Led) Excision of a portion of the iris.
Irresolute (Ign.) Who cannot take firm stand.
Ischuria (Dulc.) Retention or suppression of urine.
Isomorphic (Phos.) Equal or Similar in form or shape.

Jolting (Ham. V.) Shaking with jerks from seat, as on rough road journey.
Jovial (Cap.) Merry. Very cheerful and good-humoured.

Keen (Lye.) Very sharp.

Keratitis (Crot. H.) Inflammation of the cornea.

Keratoiritis (Crot. H.) Inflammation of both cornea and Iris.

Knuckles (Med.) A prominence produced by the head of any of themetacarpal bones.
Krout (Sour) (firv Fetr.) Dried leaves of cabbage, soaked in saline water.

Labyrinthine vertigo (Meniere’s disease) (Theridion) Distension of membranous
labyrinth of middle ear from excess fluid. Pressure causes ischaemia and failure of
function of nerve of hearing and balance, thus there is fluctuating deafness, tinnitus &
vertigo.
