HELONIAS DIOICA

Unicorn Plant TULLY Melanthaceae

INTRODUCTION

This plant is also called the “Blazing Star’,’.
Medicine is prepared from the fresh root
gathered before flowering. Just like Murex, this
remedy is chiefly a woman’s remedy and is
regarded as a great uterine tonic.

Albuminuris. Anaemia. Breast affection.
Diabetes. Menstrual anomalies. Urinary
complaints. Uterine disorder.

SPHERE OF ACTION
It has specific action over the female sexual organs including kidney, mind, muscles.

GUIDING SYMPTOMS

1) Generally called for women worn out with hard work mental or physical or

weakened by luxury and indolence and who complain of tired feeling in the back
and limbs.

2) A consciousness of a womb. Feels it moves when she moves, it is so sore and tender.

3) Feels complaints more when thinking about them, always better when occupied.
(Calc. Phos. Medo. Oxalic Acid)

4) Sensation of heaviness and soreness in pelvis.

5) Mind : Profound melancholy and great lowness of spirit. Irritable and fault finding
nature. She can not endure least contradiction or receive least suggestion. Restfess,

wants to move around as she feels better when mind and body are employed. These
mentals are always associated with uterine disorder.

6) For the bad effects of abortion and miscarriage.

PARTICULARS

Female genital organs :

Menses :

Time : Too early.

Quantity ; Too profuse.

Character of flow: Passive, dark, clotted and offensive.
