yeratrum Album 639

—-—

intermittent fever :

In Veratum album we got a very congestive and pernicious type of intermittent fever, with
extreme coldness of the body, great thirst, face cold and collapsed, skin cold and clammy, great

prostration, cold sweat on forehead and deadly pallor on face.

General Modalities :

Aggravation: From least motion, after drinking, before and during stool, when perspiring and after
fright.

Amelioration : When walking and by warmth.
Relations :

After camphor in cholera.

After Ammon carb, and Bovista in dysmenorrhoea.
