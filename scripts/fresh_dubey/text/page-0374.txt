LAC. DEFLORATUM

" Skimmed Milk DR. SWAN

INTRODUCTION

This remedy owes its origin to Dr. Swan who
in his turn got the idea of potentising skimmed
milk upon reading “Donkin’s Skimmed Milk
Treatment of Diab-etes and Bright’s Disease”.

CLINICAL

Constipation. Dropsy. Diabetes. Headache.
Vomiting. Globus hystericus. Menses suppressed.
Obesity. Lactation defective.

CONSTITUTION

Physical make up—pale, sickly, anaemic
look, wornout, wasted persons with sallow
complexion. Relation with heat & cold: Chilly
patient. The patient is chilly all the time even in
warm room, near fire. Miasm : Psora and syphilis in the background.

GUIDING SYMPTOMS
1) Great prostration is felt whether she does anything or not, great fatigue when
walking.
2) It is one of the top most medicines for sick headache.
3) Constipation : Stool large, hard, painful, extorting cries.
4) Aversion to milk.

5) “Milk sickness”—It is most useful and frequently needed remedy for those who
cannot take milk, who suffer from various complaints like disordered stomach,
headache etc.

6) It is used in cases of defecient milk secretion in young mothers, (to dry up milk-
Asaf., Lac Can.)

7) Menses : Delayed or suppressed by putting hands in cold water, drinking a glass of
milk will promptly suppress flow until next period.

8) Great restlessness, extreme and protracted suffering from loss of sleep (Coc., Nit.
Ac.)

9) Globus hystericus : Sensation of a large ball rising from stomach to throat, causing
8ense of suffocation (Asaf., Kal.)
