Murex Purpurea 425

”_

PARTICULARS

Menses :
Suited in dysmenorrhoea.
Time: Irregular.
Quantity: Too profuse.
Duration: Usually long.
Character: Protracted, large clots with violent bearing down pain in abdomen.
Concomitants: With extreme sensitiveness of external genital organs and violent sexual excitement.

Leucorrhoea :

Serous, greenish, thick, becoming sanguinous. There is reappearance of sanguinous leucorrhoea
while at stool.

Sensation: Sensation of pelvic bones getting loose.

Modality: Leucorrhoea relieves mental depression. Patient is happier when leucorrhoea is
worse.

Prolapse :

It is an admirable remedy for the prolapse of uterus, when the displacement is associated with SORE
PAIN IN UTERUS; a distinct sensation of a womb. The pain extends upwards from the right side of uterus
and after crossing the body it goes to the left mammae. There is bearing down sensation as if internal
organs would be pushed-out. The patient must sit down and cross limbs to resist pressure.

In this respect, Murex is very similar to Sepia. The differentiating features consist in its profuse and
excessive haemorrhage, whereas in Sepia the flow is usually scanty. Furthermore, during the period of
haemorrhage, she passes large clots.

Both have got bearing down sensation as if the internal organs would be pushed out and she must sit
and cross legs to resist the pressure. Along with prolapse, in Murex, there is INTENSE SEXUAL DESIRE,
while in Sepia, there is COMPLETE LACK OF SEXUAL DESIRE OR RATHER aversion to it especially
with prolapse. Sepia is also chilly, apathetic and indifferent.

Sexual symptoms :

There is violent excitement in sexual organs. She is afflicted with almost a frantic desire for sexual
gratification, having excessive desire for an embrce. There Is almost unbearable sexual desire.

Least contact of parts causes violent sexual excitement.

General Modalities :
Aggravation: From least touch.

Relations :
Compare: Platina, Lilium, Sepia.
