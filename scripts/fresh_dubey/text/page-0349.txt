General Modalities :
Aggravation: From beer, morning, hot weather and undressing.
Amelioration: Skin symptoms are better in cold weather, by lying down.

Relations :
Compare: Hepar and lodum in croupy affections.
It antidotes: Merc., Merc. iod.
Antidoted by: Ars., lach., Puls.
Follows well: After lodum in croup when hoarse cough, with tough membrane.
