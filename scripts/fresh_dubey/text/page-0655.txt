VIPERA

German Viper Ophidia

INTRODUCTION

Vipera affects the blood and blood
vessels, leading to haemorrhages &
inflammation of the vessels themselves.
Viper poisoning causes a temporary
paraplegia of the. lower extremities
extending upwards. It is indicated in
phlebitis, lymphangioma, varicose veins,
enlargement of liver, polyneuritis &
polio—myelitis etc.

The characteristic indication of this
remedy is varicose veins & acute phlebitis
is “bursting sensation in affected limbs & unbearable pain when limbs are
allowed to hang down”. Patient is obliged to keep the extemities elevated. Veins are
swollen, sensitive & painful with bursting sensation.

The next important use of this remedy is in enlargement of liver when there is
violent pain in the enlarged liver in jaundice & fever. Pain radiates to shoulder & hip.
