CANTHARIDES

"Spanish Flies HAHNEMANN Cantharideae

INTRODUCTION

This medicine is prepared from the Spanish
Fly by reducing into a course powder and
saturating it later with alcohol to prepare the
mother tincture. It was first introduced into the
parlance of Homoeopathy by our Master
Hahnemann. To stress its importance, Dr. Tyler
remarks “ANOTHER VEHEMENT DRUG OF
STORM AND STRESS” tempered by
Hahnemann. Cantharides in its crude form is a
powerful irritant and is hardly ever used
internally. After Homoeopathic proving, the same
irritant drug is used for an irritant disorder on
the basis of Homoepathic Law “Similia Similibus Curantur”.

CLINICAL

Bladder affection, Burns, Dysentery, Diarrhoea, Gonorrhoea, Kidney troubles,
Neuralgia, Pleurisy, Pregnancy troubles, Haematuria. Skin affection, etc.

SPHERES OF ACTION

It acts especially on urinary organs, urinary bladder, urethra, ureters, skin,
respiratory system and gastro-intestinal canal.

PATHOGENESIS

It especially affects the urinary and sexual organs and prevents their function by
setting up violent inflammation. It also causes inflammation of the whole gastro
intestinal canal, especially of lower bowels, Inflammation produced by Cantharides on
lower bowels, kidneys, ovaries, meninges, pleura or pericardium is usually
ASSOCIATED WITH BLADDER IRRITATION.

1. “Burning” in every part of the body; both internal and external, run through the
whole remedy.

2. Constant and intolerable uriging to urinate before, during and after urination
is the key-note of Cantharides.

3. Burning and cutting pain in urethra during micturition,
4. Oversensitiveness of all parts.
5. There is haemorrhage from nose, mouth, intestine, genital and urinary organs.
