Ceanothus is a spleen remedy par excellence
and suited to anaemic patients where liver and
spleen are at fault. It is good antidote to malaria.
Drs. Burnett, Hale and P.C. Majumder
proved its efficacy in a number of cases of
malaria and in other diseases where spleen is
affected. Its indications are pain, inflammation,
enlargement of spleen with fullness in the left
hypochonodrium, cchilliness and __ shivering
principally down’back and dyspnoea and
palpitation on exertion. All symptoms are aggravated by motion and lying on left side.

Liver :
Pain & fullness in region of liver worse lying on right-side.

Urine:

Constant urging to urinate. Urine is green, frothy contains bile, sugar. There may be
diarrhoea with bearing down in abdomen & rectum.

N.B. : In Ch. cases of Malaria, Kalazar specially when spleen is enlarged, it is beneficial when given
in low potency.
