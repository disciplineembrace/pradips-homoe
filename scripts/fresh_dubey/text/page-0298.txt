INTRODUCTION

Fluoric acid is a colourless liquid obtained by
distillation. It unites with water violently and dissolves all
ordinary metals. But heavy metals like platinum, gold,
silver, mercury, lead, etc. are not soluble in Fluoric acid. It
is a strong irritant and causes burns. It is a DEEP and
LONG ACTING remedy.
