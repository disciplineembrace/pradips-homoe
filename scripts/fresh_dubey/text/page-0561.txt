SPHERES OF ACTION

It acts on urinary organs, genitals, rectum, skin, bones, and right lower part of the
body.
PATHOGENESIS

It acts on kidney and urinary organs producing renal colic, renal and bladder stone
and consequential urinary troubles. It also acts on skin causing various kinds of
eruptions, rash, cracks and fissures. Furthermore, rheumatic affections, periostea! pains,
etc. are also within the range of its action.

CONSTITUTION

Look of the patient: Lean, thin and debilitated persons with dark hair and shrivelled
skin which lies loose in folds. In children the face looks like that of an old man with
enlarged abdomen, dry and flabby skin, with various skin eruptions and cracks and
fissures, especially in hands and feet.

Relation with heat and cold: Easily affected by cold in general,
Miasm: It covers all the three miasms, psora, syphilis and sycosis.
Diathesis: LITHIC and scrofulous.

GUIDING SYMPTOMS
1. It is a RIGHT SIDED REMEDY (Lyco., Caust., Sang., Bry., Kali. carb.).

2. THERE IS SEVERE, UNBEARABLE PAIN AT THE CONCLUSION OF
URINATION, just as urine ceases to flow (Berb. vulgaris, Med., Thuja).
