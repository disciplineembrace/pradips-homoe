INTRODUCTION

This is one of the twelve tissue remedies
introduced by schussler. Homeopathic proving
was done by Dr. J.B. Bell on himself & others in
1874. It is a great polychrest & has got a
powerful hold on the human economy. Cale fluor
is long and deep acting constitutional remedy for
hard stony glands, varicose & enlarged veins &
malnutrition of bones ete. It is found in the
surface of bones, in the enamel of teeth, in elastic
fibres & in the cells of epidermis.

CLINICAL

Adenoids, Aneurism, Affections of Bone, of
Glands, Cataract, Catarrrh, Constipation,

SPHERES OF ACTION

It acts on bones, enamel of teeth, skin, connective tissues & vessels. Also acts on
lymph nodes, glands & fibrous tissues etc.

It causes relaxation & dilatation of elastic fibres of any portion of the vessels, of
connective tissues or of the lymphatic system leading to haemorrhoidal tumors, varicose
veins, vascular tumors, indurated glands, uterine displacements, weakening of
abdominal walls & hard, knotty tumor on the surface of the bones.

GUIDING SYMPTOMS

1) This remedy acts favourably on hard, stony glands & varicose veins.

2) One of the powerful remedies for tumor in the female breast specially when it is
hard & knotty.

3) In osseous growths & in suppuration of bones, it acts favourably after Silicea &
Calc. Phos. have failed.

4) This has got marked action on the lower jaw producing necrosis & suppuration.
Complications of Wisdom teeth.

5) Periosteal swelling due to congenital hereditary syphilis.
