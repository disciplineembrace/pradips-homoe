ANTHRACINUM

A nosode (A product of Anthrax poison)

INTRODUCTION

Anthracinum was introduced intoHomoeopathic
practice by “Lux” long before the experiment of
“Pasteur”. Though it is not a polychrest medicine
having limited sphere of action still it has got very
important and respected place in Homoeopathy
because it has got power to make surgeon's knife
useless and relieves the most serious cases of
Carbuncle, Gangrene, Felons, Blood boils etc.

CLINICAL:
Acne. Boil. Carbuncle. Insect stings. Gangrene.

Malignant pustules. Felons. Septic fever. Splenic
fever. Dissecting wounds. Anthrax poisoning by
foul breath, Foul gas ete.

SPHERES OF ACTION :
Anthracinum has its centre of action over the connective tissues. It covers all the
inflammatory conditions of connective tissue in which there exists some purulent focus.
It also acts on skin.
GUIDING SYMPTOMS :
1) Atrocious burning pains as if hot coals were applied to parts. (Ars.)

2) Discharges are ichorus, putrid and offensive from mouth, nose, throat, nostrils,
vagina, rectum, ulcer, carbuncle etc. Horribly offensive (Ars., Pyr.).

3) Haemorrhages are black, thick, tar like, rapidly decomposing, from any orifice.

4) Tendency towards malignancy and rapid formation of gangrenous condition.

5) Fever, rapid loss of strength, great prostration, sinking pulse, delirium and fainting.
6) Affected parts turn black and blue.

7) Red lines from the area of infection marking the course of lymphatics.

8) Rapid spread of infiltration and oedema of cellular tissue. Excessive thirst with
inability to swallow.
COMPARISONS :

We may compare Anthracinum with Arsenic Album, Tarentula Cubensis & Secale Cornutum in
relation with the above mentioned complaints.
