been introduced into the Homoeopathic Materia

syphilitic discharges and used it in the treatment
of syphilitic patients. He has., of course, done it
empirically after potentising according to the
Doctrine of Dynamisation. At present, it. holds the
place of the “KING OF ANTI-SYPHILITICS”
amongst the nosodes so far proved.

Dr. Hering remarks, “No patient having
syphilitic history in the past can be declared
cured until and unless this nosode is administered at least once in the course of the
treatment. So much is its importance if adequately given”.
