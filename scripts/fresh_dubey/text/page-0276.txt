DROSERA ROTUNDIFOLIA

Sundew Droseraceae

INTRODUCTION

It is a drug prepared from vegetable
kingdom. It was first introduced by our

Master Hahnemann, but mastered equally
well by Dr. Tyler as she remarks in her
Drug Picture in the following words. “A
few years ago, I came to the startling
conclusion that the only two people

who really knew anything about
Drosera were Samuel Hahnemann

and myself.” She proved her statement in

her subsequent writings when she unfolded the therapeutic effects of this wonderful drug
in the sphere of respiratory affections, particularly the tubercular infection. Later on,
clinical experiences confirmed that Drosera is not a drug with its spheres of action to
whooping cough only but is capable of fighting the tubercular infection too, particularly of
bones and glands.

SPHERES OF ACTION

Acts markedly upon the respiratory organs by its influence on the pneum o-gastric
nerve. It also affects bones and glands.

PATHOGENESIS
It produces spasmodic dry cough; stimulating whooping cough. Drosera can break
down resistance to tuberculosis and should, therefore, be capable of raising it (Dr.Tyler).
