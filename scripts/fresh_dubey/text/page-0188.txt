Capsicum 179

Intermittent fever :

Capsicum is a valuable remedy in intermittent fever.

The chill begins in the back. Thirst commences long before chill commences. There is great thirst,
but drinking causes shivering and increase chilliness. The chill is relieved by heat, must have something
hot to cover up.

During the stage of chill, he becomes sensitive to noise. There is no thirst both in heat and sweat
stages.
General Modalities :

Aggravation: From open air, uncovering and draughts of air.

Amelioration: While eating and from heat.

Antidotes : Cina.
Compare: Lyco and Puts.
