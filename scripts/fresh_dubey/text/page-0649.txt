VERATRUM VIRIDE

Green Hellebore BURT Melanthaceae

INTRODUCTION

Veratrum viride is a small but powerful drug.
It is mostly used in severe acute cases, such as
severe form of headache, sunstroke, basilar
meningitis, convulsion, acute rheumatism, etc. It
has direct action over heart, causing depression
of heartbeat and low pulse rate. So never misuse
it and never repeat it very frequently just to
control the heart’s action or bring down the
pulse. Like any other remedy it can only be given
for the totality of symptoms.

Hale was chifly instrumental in introducing
Veratrum viride into Homoeopathy, using it particularly in pneumonia.

Belladonna has got muchsimilarity with Veratrum viride. So, never substitute it
when Veratrum viride is indicated. Individual and comparative study of these two drugs
are necessary for a correct prescription.

CLINICAL

Apoplexy, Asthma, Brain affection, Chorea, Convulsion, Erysipelas, Headache, Heart
affection, Hyperpyrexia, Fever, Meningitis, Pneumonia, Rheumatism, Sunstroke, etc.
SPHERES OF ACTION

Veraturm viride acts on heart, blood vessels, pneumo-gastric nerve, lungs and joints.
It particularly acts on the cervical portion of spinal cord and the base of the brain.

Acting on the heart, it lowers both systolic and diastolic blood pressure. It also on the
internal organs such as the base of brain, chest, spine, stomach, etc. and produces
congestion.

CONSTITUTION

Since Veratrum viride is mostly an acute drug, consideration of constitution is not of
much importance. The patient is however, full blooded, plethoric, with bloated and livid
face.
