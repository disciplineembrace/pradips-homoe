Leucorrhoea :

Character of leucorrhoea: Albuminous, starchy, very MUCH HOT as if warm water were flowing,
very much ACRID leucorrhoea, preventing pregnancy, lasting two weeks, Leucorrhoea white as mucus or
like the white of an egg.

Baby :

Oversensitive children. Face of infant pale and clay coloured. Children have small vesicles around the
mouth and on the forehead.

This type of children often get summer complaints. There are small ulcers appearing around the
anus. Stool frequent, soft light yellow and slimy.

These children have DREAD OF DOWNWARD MOTION. When laying the child on a couch, in
cradle or a craib, the baby cries and clings to the nurse (Cham.).

Child awakes suddenly, screaming and grasping sides of the cradle without any apparent cause. Child
has frequent urination and screams before passing urine. Urine burns. So when it passes, the child
cries, but he has desire to urinate.

Urethra pain, as if, sore, after urinating. There is sore mouth in children. They cannot suck the
breast.

General Modalities :

Aggravation: From downward motion, from sudden slight noise, smoking, damp cold weather,
before urination.

Amelioration: Pressure, holding painful sjde with hand.
