TARENTULA CUBENSIS

“Cuban Spider Aranaediceae

INTRODUCTION

Though it is not a polychrest medicine as it
has limited sphere of action, still it has got the
power to make Surgeon’s knife useless and
relieves the most serious cases of blood boils,
carbuncle, felons and gangrene etc. In Allen’s
Keynote Tarentula Hispania & Tarentula
cubensis both are given under one heading
‘Tarantula” though they are different
medicines. Tarentula cubensis is a larger
spider of a dark brown colourless poisonous
variety & covered with more hair than the
Tarentula Hispania.

Tarentula Cubensis is prepared from the whole hairy spider. This spider is very
poisonous.

Clinical
Anthrax. Abscess. Carbuncle. Fever. Felon. Gangrene. Septic Fever. Ulcer ete.

Tarentula cubensis is a medicine for above mentioned dis-eases when there is severe
type inflammations and septic conditions and even malignancy, if the following
symptoms are present.

1) Atrocious burning pains, stinging pains, make the patient restless and compels him
to walk at night. It is the remedy for pains of death.

2) Bluish, purple, discolouration of affected part with swelling.
3) Very early & persistent prostration.
4) Intermittent septic chills.
5) Hyperaesthesia relieved by constant movement & some kind of friction.
6) Modalities :
Aggravation—at night, contact, touch of affected parts
Amelioration—Rubbing affected parts.

Dr. Benthack says Tarentula Cubensis and Arsenicum Alb. are complementary to
tach other in septic inflammatory states,
