Phosphorus 479

Character of blood: Profuse, watery, bright-red blood which is non-coagulable. Bleeding
from various organs and parts of the body. Small wounds bleed much. Pours out freely and then ceases
suddenly for a time, seen as in metrorrhagia, haemoptysis, haematemesis, from nose, stomach, anus, etc.

General Modalities :

Aggravation: Evening and before mid-night (Puls., Rhus tox.), Lying on left or painful side,
during a thunderstorm, any change of weather and extremes of temperature.

Amelioration: Lying on right side, from being rubbed or magnetised, from cold water, cold food (until
it gets warm) and in darkness.

Note : Cold air relieves the head and face symptoms, but aggavates those of chest, throat and neck.

Relations :
Complementary to: Arsenic.
Incompatible to: Causticum i.e. must not be used before or after.
Follows well: After Calcarea and China. Vegetable analogue: Cepa.

Key-note remark : Hahnemann says: “Acts most beneficially when patient suffers from chronic
loose stool or diarrhoea”.
