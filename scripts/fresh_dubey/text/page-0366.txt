INTRODUCTION

Kreosotum occupies the second position the
TRIO OF OFFENSIVE DRUGS—first being
Mercurius and the third, Baptisia. In Kreosote
the characteristic odour is PUTRID. Being a
member of the tar family, ACRIDITY is the
second characteristic of the drug. PUTRIDITY
AND ACRIDITY run through the whole drug.
Kreosote is mainly a left sided medicine.

CLINICAL

Abdominal and stomach troubles, Cough,
Cancer, Carbuncle, Consumption, Constipation, Cholera, Dentition, Diarrhoea, Enuresis,

Haemorhage, Haemorrhoids, Hysteria, Leucorrhoea, Menstrual disorders, Neuralgia,
Pregnancy troubles, Rheumatism, Teeth Caries, Toothache, Ulcers, Urine—incontinence

of, Vomiting, Whooping Cough, etc.

SPHERES OF ACTION

Kreosote acts upon the lymphatic system and especially upon the mucus membrane
of digestive system, female generative organs, the skin and teeth.

PATHOGENESIS

It produces tendency to disorganisation, ulceration and destruction of the parts
involved such as teeth, skin, mucus membrane of digestive system, etc. Scorbutic gum
comes under the pathogenetip action of this drug.

CONSTITUTION

Physical make up: Especially suited to ladies having dark complexion.

Tall, slender, ill developed, overgrown, very tall for her age (Phos.).

Children are old looking, wrinkled, scrofulous with rapid emaciation and difficult
dentition,

Temperament: Irritable temperament.

Relation with heat and cold: Chilly patient.

Miasm: Psora and syphilis are in the background.

Diathesis: Haemorrhagic and scorbutic diathesis.
