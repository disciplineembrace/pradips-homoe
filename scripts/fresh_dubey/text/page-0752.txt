Glossary 749

Numbness : Deprived of the power of feeling, insensible.

Nystagmus : Continuous rolling movement of eyeballs.

Obesity: An abnormal increase of fat in the subcutaneous connective tissues. Fatness,
corpulence.

Oedema : Dropsy, Pathological accumulation of fluid in tissue spaces.

Old toper : A person long used to regular drinking.

Onanism : Masturbation.

Oozing : Flowing or leaking outslowly.

Opthalmia neonatorum : Purulent severe inflammation of eyes of the new born.
Opisthotonos: Tetanic spasm which flexes the head and feet backward.
Orchitis ; Inflammation of the testis.

Otorrhoea : A muco-purulent discharge from the ear.

Ozaena: A foul odour present in certain cases of atrophic syphilitic and other forms of chronic

Panaritium : Felon; whitlow.
Paraplegia : Paralysis of both the lower extremities.

Paralysis: Partial or complete loss or temporary interruption of normal function of some part
of body.

Paresis ; Partial paralysis.
Paronychia ; Felon; whitlow.

paroxysm: A sudden attack or intensification of the symptoms of a disease usually occurring
periodically.

Parturition : Giving birth to a child.

Pearly : Like a pear! as in colour or lustre.

Pemphigus: A disease marked by formation of bullae which after absorption, leave
pigmentary spots.

Pertusis : Whooping cough.

Phlebitis : Inflammation of vein.

Phagadena: An ulcer that rapidly spreads peripherally destroying tissues as it increases in
area.
