SABINA

Savine STAPF Coniferae

INTRODUCTION

This drug was first proved by Hahnemann
and is one of our important haemorrhagic
medicines. Old school uses the roots of the plant
to produce miscarriage. Hahnemann provedit for
the prevention of abortion especially at the third
month. Sabiha may be said to be predominantly
female remedy.

CLINICAL

Abortion, Condylomata, Cystitis, Gonorrhoea,
Gout, Leucorrhoea, Menstural disorders,
Miscarriage, Rheumatism, Skin troubles. Uterine
affection, etc.

SPHERES OF ACTION
It acts especially upon the uterus, serous and fibrous tissues.

PATHOGENESIS

It causes widespread irritation, congestion and inflammation of uterus and serous
and fibrous tissues resulting in uterine and rheumatic symptoms.

CONSTITUTION

It is especially suited to chlorotic women, having tendency to miscarriage,
particularly in the third month.

Relation with heat and cold: Hot patient.
Miasm: Sycosis is in the background.
Diathesis: Haemorrhagic and gouty diathesis.
