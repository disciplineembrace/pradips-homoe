Glossary 745

Gleet: Slight chronic discharge of thin muco-pus from urethra following gonorrhoea.
Globus hystericus: Sensation as if a ball in throat occurring in emotional attacks.
Gold headed cane patient: Fastidious person.

Gloomy: Very sad or dejected, hopeless and melancholic.

Glossary: A partial dictionary giving meaning of technical terms.

Griping: To cause sharp pain in the bowels, very distressing.

Grumous: Scrofulous.

Hallucination: False Imaginations.

Haematemesis: Vomiting of blood.

Haematuria: Presence of blood in urine.

Hemiopia: Loss of vision for one half of the visual field.

Haemoptysis: Spitting of blood.

Haemorrhoids: Piles, Varicose condition of the external haemorrhoidal veins causing
painful condition of anus.

Heart burn: Pyrosis or water brash.

Helminthiasis: Condition of having intestinal vermiform parasites.

Hemicrania: Migraine, Hemicephalia.

Herpes : A spreading eruption of deep seated vesicles on an erethematous base-h. simplex
and h. zoster.

Hernia: Protrusion of a part or an organ or other structure through wall of the cavity
normally containing it.

Hippocratic face: Face of a dying man as described by Hippocratis marked by emaciated
nose, etc.

Hilarity: Excessive mirth.

Headstrong: Determined not to follow order, advice, etc. but to do as he pleases.

Home-sickness: Longing for home and sickness for being away from home and family.

Hour glass contraction: Constriction in the middle Portion of a hollow organ, such as,
stomach or gravid uterus.

Hydrocele: Collection of serous fluid in a sacculated cavity, especially in tunica vaginalis of
testis.

Hydrocephalus: Excessive accumulation of fluid in the cerebral ventricles dilating these
cavities, thinning the brain, and causing separation of cranial bone,

Hydrophobia: Rabies in men. fear of water due to it.
