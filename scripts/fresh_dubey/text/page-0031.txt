ACTAEA SPICATA

Beneberry PETROZ Ranunculaceae

—_

INTRODUCTION

This is a rheumatic & gouty remedy and it
has special affinity for smaller joints. It is
especially suited to old people.

CLINICAL :

Rheumatism.

PARTICULARS

Rheumatism :

This remedy has special affinity for smaller joints,
particularly the wrist. The joints start aching and swelling
after a slight excercise. Affected joints are swollen and in
affected limb great weakness is felt as if paralysed. There is extreme tenderness to touch, weakness &
tingling. The right arm and right wrist are specially affected.

Pains are of a violent, tearing, drawing character & are worse by touch or movement. Pains of this
kind running from decayed tooth to temples.

Modality : Aggravation— at night, motion and change of weather, fatigue.
