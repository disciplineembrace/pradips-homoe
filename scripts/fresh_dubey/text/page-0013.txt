ABIES NIGRA

Black Spruce DR. LEAMAN Coniferae

INTRODUCTION

A powerful and long acting remedy, in
various kinds of diseases, whenever the
characteristic stomach symptoms are present.

CLINICAL
Constipation. Dyspepsia,
SPHERES OF ACTION

The most specific action of this remedy is on
stomach and also on heart and lungs.

GUIDING SYMPTOMS
1) Sensation as if a hard-boiled egg had lodged in the cardiac end of stomach.

2) Pain in stomach always comes after eating.
3) Wakeful and restless at night, with hunger.
4) Dyspeptic troubles of the aged after abuse of tea or tobacco.

PARTICULARS

Gastrointestinal Symptoms:

It is a grand remedy in dyspepsia especially of the aged, habituated to excess of tea and tobacco.
They are generally low spirited and dull and have wakeful nights. The most characteristic symptom of
Abies N. is the sensation as if a hard boiled egg had lodged in the cardiac end of stomach.
The patient may describe this as a feeling as though he had swallowed some indigestible substance which
had stuck in his stomach. Severe pain in stomach is felt after eating. Total loss of appetite in
morning, but great craving for food at noon & night. Dyspepsia with functional heart symptoms.

Chest:

There is painful sensation, as if something were lodged in the chest and had to be coughed up. Chest
cannot be fully expanded. Sharp, cutting pain in heart ; heart's action heavy & slow, tachycardia.
Dyspnoea worse lying down. Water brash succeeds cough, worse coughing.

Relation : Compare, China, Bryo, Puls, in Lump in Stomach; also Thuja, Sabina, Nux Vor, Kali
