Carbo Vegetabilis 185

Haemorrhage :

Carbo veg. is a very important remedy for passive capillary bleeding where there is constant oozing
of dark blood, except in case of nose where blood is brigt red.

Causation: Low vitality, weak or feeble heart wilth relaxation of blood vessels and vaso-motor
paralysis.

Location: Especially on the venous side of the economy,

Modality; Relief BY FANNING.

Concomitants: Cyanosis. Desire to be fanned continuously and form near which gives relief.

Skin :

Skin is cyanosed, cold and ecchymosed due to venous stasis. Moist skin with hot perspiration old and
clammy sweat, itching falling of hair due to general weakneis after exhausting disease.

Senile gangrene which bleed easily and gives off foul smell.

Bedsore which bleeds easily and gives off foul smell, Varicose ulcers due to feeble circulation with

Carbuncle purple in appearance with burning pain which imparts offensive smell: aggravated by heat
and relieved by cold application.

Ulceration of skin and mucous membrane from injury which does not heal readily due to relaxation
of the blood vessels and feebleness of the tissue.

The ulcers give out a bloody, ichorous, acrid and thin discharge with offensive smell.

General Modalities :

Aggravation: In the evening, in open air, from cold, from hot food, butter, coffee, milk, warm damp
weather and alcoholic drinks.

Amelioration: From eructation, from fanning rapidly and from a close distance and by cold
application.
Relations :

Complementary to: China, Kali carb. and Drosera.

Antidotes: Camphor, Arsenic, Lachesis and Coffee.

Antidoted by: Ars., Camphor, Lach., Ferr. m.

N.B. Carbo veg. is the “Homoeopathic Last Aid".
