ALLEN'S KEY NOTE ~ an easy explanation 727

Quack remedies (Nux v.) Same as Nostrum.

Quinsy (Bar. C., Psor.) Peritonsillar abscess; acute suppurative in-flammation of the faucial
tonsil and surrounding tissues.

Rachitis (Calc. P. Sil.) Rickets.

Racking (Bry. Mez.) Shaking the body violently.
Rage (Apis, Crocus.) Violent anger.

Rakes (Cocc.) Dissipated or immoral man of fashion.

Ranula (Ambra, Thuj.) A cystic tumour on the floor of the mouth, due to obstruction on the
duct of the sublingual glands; any cystic tumour of the under surface of the tongue or floor

of the mouth.

Rasping (Spong.) Harsh sound.

Rave (Stram, Agar. M.) Rage; Talk as if mad.

Reflex Symptoms (Actaea, Caulo) Any systemic disorder that exhibits the disorder of
another system.

Repose (Mag. C., Nux M, Rhus.) Rest.

Repress (Lil. T.) Suppress.

Reprimanded (Ign.,Stram.) Rebuked.

Repugnant (Nux V., Rheum.) Distasteful.

Retiring (Mag. C., Zinc.) Going to bed.

Retrocession (Op.) The cessation of the external symptoms of a disease followed by signs of
involvement of some internal organ or part.

Retroversion (Lil. T.) A turning backward, as of the uterus, without flexing or bending the
organ.

Rheumatic diathesis (Ben. A., Led., Phyt., Rhus.) Disposition of being attacked with
rheumatism.

Rheumatic dysmenorrhoea (Actaea) Difficult and painful menstruation resulting from
rheumatism.

Rhuberb (Rheum) Purgative.

Ribbon like stool (Am. M.) Tape like stool.
