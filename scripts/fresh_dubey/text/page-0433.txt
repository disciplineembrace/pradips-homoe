MUREX PURPUREA

Purple fish PETROZ Muricidae

INTRODUCTION

Murex purpurea is prepared from a sea snail
found on the coast of mediterranean sea called
the purple fish. Like Sepia, this purple fish,
secretes a peculiar substance which is of a fine
red hue. Just like Sapia, this remedy is
particularly A WOMAN’S REMEDY. It was first
proved by Petroz, unto whom we owe this
remedy.

CLINICAL

Abortion, Climacteric suffering, Diabetes,
Leucorrhoea, Menstrual disorders, Nymphomania,
Pregnancy troubles, Prolapse of Uterus, etc.
SPHERES OF ACTION

Murex acts especially upon the female genital organs—uterus, ovaries and other
genital organs.
CONSTITUTION

Physical make up: Best adapted to lively affectionate women.

Temperament: Melancholic temperament.
