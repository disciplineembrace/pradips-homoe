CAULOPHYLLUM

Blue Cohosh BURT Berberidaceae

INTRODUCTION

Caulophyllum is known to the common people as
Blue Cohosh or Squaw root. Though the proving was
done for a short period with a limited known
therapeutic sphere, the drug has attained a position of
being one more of what we term as the “LADY'S
REMEDY”

Abortion threatened, Amenorrhoea, Bearing down
pains, Dysmenorrhoea, Disorders of Pregnancy,
Haemorrhage, Gonorrhoea, Labour, Leucorrhoea,
Menstrual disorders, Rheumatism, Uterine affections,
etc.

SPHERE OF ACTION

It acts especially upon the female genital organs-
uterus and cervix. Joints, especially small joints and stomach are also the seats of action
in few cases.
PATHOGENESIS

Caulophllym has mainly pathogenetic changes on uterus and cervix. Its most
prominent value seems to lie in its power of causing INTERMITTENT CONTRACTION
OF GRAVID UTERUS Joints, especially small ones are inflamed. Rheumatic and gouty
inflammation occur causing pain, soreness and Swelling.

CONSTITUTION

Physical make-up: It is generally suited to women during pregnancy, parturition and
lactation, having MOTH SPOTS ON THE FACE AND FOREHEAD AND DROOPING
OF EYE LIDS.

Temperament: Nervous temperament.
Diathesis: Rheumatic and gouty.
GUIDING SYMPTOMS

1. “Weakness, exhaustion, Want of tone” are the keynotes of this remedy.

2. Pains are intemittent, paroxysmal, spasmodic and erratic, changing place every few
minutes.
