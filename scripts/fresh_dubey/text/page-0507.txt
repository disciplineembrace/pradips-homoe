Female Genital Organs :

Podophyllum is especially suited in prolapse of uterus with sub-involution.

Causation: From over-lifting or straining, from constipation and after parturition.

Location: Pain in uterus and right ovary.

Sensation: Dragging down sensation in the region of ovary.

Character of pain: Pain with numbness in right ovary, running down to thigh. The uterus is
extremely sore to touch, even the touch of light clothing is unbearable. In early months of pregnancy, the
patient can lie comfortably only on abdomen.

Fever :

Podophyllum is generally useful in intermittent fever.

Time: Fever paroxysms come at 7 a.m.

With fever, there is pain in the hypochondrium, knees, ankles and wrists.

There is great Joquacity during fever; the patient goes on talking especially during chill and heat
stage.The patient sleeps during the sweat stage. The sweat is profuse and offensive.

The tongue is moist, large and flabby with much salivation and great thirst for large quantity of cold
water.

General Modalites :
Aggravation: In the early morning, in hot weather and during dentition.

Relations :
It antidotes: The bad effects of Mercury, Comphor, Aloe, Chelidonium, Nux vomica and Sulphur.
