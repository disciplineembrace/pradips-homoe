Bismuth 127

Along with the predominant vomiting, there |s purging and diarrhoea. Stool is watery, profuse, painless
and very offensive.

* Concomitants: There is great prostration, with the face looking deathly pale and dark ring
around the eyes, surface covered with sweat, but it is warm sweat.

There is intense thirst for water.
The patient is full of ANGUISH, RESTLESSNESS AND WANTS COMPANY ALWAYS.

Bismuth patient may also suffer from toothache which is relieved by holding cold water in the mouth
(Bryonia, Coffea, Pulsatilla, Natrum sulph.).

General Modalities :
Aggravation: by heat and summer weather.

Amelioration: By cold application, cold bathing and open air. Motion relieves most symptoms except
headache.

Relations :
Antidoted by: Coffea, Nux vom., Calc. carb.
Similar to: Ars., Antim crud., Bell., Bry.
