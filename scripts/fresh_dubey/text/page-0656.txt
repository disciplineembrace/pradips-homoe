XANTHOXYLUM

“Prickly Ash Rutaceae

INTRODUCTION

INTRODUCTION : Just like Sepia, this
remedy is particularly a woman’s remedy,
specially for the nervous women.

CLINICAL

After pains. Headache. Hemiplegia. Indigestion.
Neuralgia-crural. Neuralgic dysmenorrhoea.
Sciatica. Toothache. Uterine fibroma.

SPHERE OF ACTION AND PATHOGENESIS

Its specific action is on nervous system and
mucous membrane, causing excessive mucous
secretion of the mouth by stimulating secretion
from all glands with ducts opening in the mouth. & female genital system.

CONSTITUTION

Best adapted to persons who are thin, emaciated and nervous with nervous
temperament having Psoric and Syphilitic miasm’s in the back ground.

GUIDING SYMPTOMS

1) This remedy is infallible for neuralgic dysmenorrhoea in women of nervous,
delicate nature—who give history of too early, usually thick blackish stringy-blood,
menstrual flow intermitting every other day and generally lasting for 2 weeks. The
most important is the agonizing pain along the course of the genito crural nerve.
The pain shoots down as low as knee and no position really relieves. Generally
associated with backache and headache. During pain patient desires to take deep
breath. Dysmenorrhoea may be attended with left sided ovarian neuralgia. (Lil
Tig, Lachesis, Medo, Thuja)

2) Leucorrhoea in place of menses (Cocculus., lodum., Nux-Mos., Phos.)

3) Headache : Specially when caused by suppression of menses. During headache
there is sensation as if head were surrounded by tight band (Gels-., Nitric Acid.,
Carbo. Acid., Sulph.) Head feels full. Occipital headache. Usu-ally headache is
associated with sleepiness, flatulence, ringing in ears.

Modalities : Aggravation—In the morning.
Amelioration : By cold water, in open air.
