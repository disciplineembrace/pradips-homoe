She cannot bear tobacco in any form. There is great intolerance of tobacco. She is
very greedy and miserly, absent-minded, memory is very weak, unable to recollect
thoughts.

Consolation aggravates her mental condition. “The disposition peculiar to Sepia is
A DEPRESSED, ANXIOUS AND FEARFUL STATE OF MIND WITH A SENSE OF
HELPLESSNESS AND YET SUSCEPTIBILITY TO EXCITEMENT AND STILL
MORE TO TERROR, FREQUENT ATTACKS OF WEEPING AND DESPAIR OF

PARTICULARS

Head :
There is rush of blood to head.
Vertigo with sensation of something rolling around in head.

There is violent pressive headache in terrible shocks at menstrual nisus, with scanty flow:
sensation as if head would burst.

Great falling of hair, after chronic headache at the climacteric period.

Headache of delicate sensitive and hysterical women. Pressing and bursting headache, aggravated by
stooping, mental labour and relieved by external pressure and continued motion,

Herpes behind ears, on nape of neck, pain as if from subcutaneous ulceration, swelling, eruption on
external ear.

Stomach :

Feeling of emptiness, all-gone sensation In the pit of stomach, relieved by eating.

Nausea and vomiting at smell or sight and even thought of food. Nausea worse when lying on side
and nausea from tobacco smoking.

Nausea in the morning before eating. There is burning in pit of stomach. Everything tastes too salty.
