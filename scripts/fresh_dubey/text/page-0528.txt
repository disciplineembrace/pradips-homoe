RATANHIA

Mapato Polygalaceae

——

INTRODUCTION

The Ratanhia tincture is prepared from the
roots of krameria triandra, a plant growing in
dry argil-laceous and sandy soils of Peru. The
colour of the tincture is blood—red. It is a great
astringent and a styptic and is consequently used
jn passive haemorrhages.

CLINICAL

Fissures-anal and nipples. Haemorrhages.
Haemor-rhoids. Pin worms. Toothache.

SPHERE OF ACTION

It has marked action on Gastrointestinal tract specially rectum & anus, also acts on
nipple of breast & teeth.

GUIDING SYMPTOMS
1) Excruciating pains after stool ; burning after stool.

2) Fissures of anus with great sensitiveness of rectum.
3) Fissures of nipples in nursing women (Graph. Sep.)

4) Constipation : Hard stool with much straining; haemorrhoids protrude during stool
& is followed by long lasting aching and burning in anus. (Sulph.) There is pain
after stool as if splinters of glass were sticking in anus & rectum.

5) Burning for hours after each stool is a characteristic symptom and is only relieved
by the application of hot water.

6) Terrible toothache during early months of pregnancy; aggravation by lying,
compelling to rise and walk about.

7) Pin worms with itching of anus.

As regards the fissure of the anus & other rectal symptoms we may compare
Ratanhia to Aesculus, Nitric acid, Paeonia, Thuja & Collinsonia.

In Aesculus the rectum feels full of small sticks like Ratanhia but there is in
Aesculus the seasation of fullness, dryness & heat in the rectum with severe dull pain in
the lumbo-sacral articulation.

In Paeonia, fissures with much oozing due to ulceration of the mucous membrane of
rectum and anus. The parts are usually swollen, offensive and moist all the time.
