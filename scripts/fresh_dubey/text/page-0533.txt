Rhubarb HAHNEMANN Polygonaceac

INTRODUCTION

Rheum is our everyday Rhubarb which
grows plentifully in China. Hahnemann’s
proving of Rheum has confirmed many of its
traditional uses. This medicine is prepared from
the powdered root.

CLINICAL
Colic. Dentition-difficult. Diarrhoea.

1) Suited to children especially during
dentition.

2) Sour smell of the whole body ; child smells sour, even after washing or
bathing (Hep. Mag C.)

3) Profuse & constant sweat on scalp ; whether asleep or awake, quiet or in motion,
the hair is always wet; may or may not be sour. (Calc. Sanic.).

4) Child is restless, irritable, peevish with pale face and sour smell during difficult
dentition. —

5) Desires various kinds of food, but canjjot eat them ; becomes repugnant.

6) Colic : Aggravated at once while uncovering an .arm or leg and standing ; Not better
by stool. Colic with sour stool.

7) Children cry & toss about all night (Psor.)
8) Ailments from : Eating unripe fruits, Dentition.

9) Mind : Mentally he is impatient & vehement, Ugly & impossible to satisfy. He
screams, kicks & makes himself decidedly unpleasant. He cries & tosses about all
night. Desires various kinds of food but cannot eat them ; becomes repugnant. He
desires many things and cries. He dislikes even favourite playthings.

PARTICULARS

Rheum Baby

The whole body of the Rheum baby smells sour. No amount of washing or bathing can rid it.
Sourness pervades the whole system & characterises all the discharges & exhalations of the body. Rheum
is suited especially to baby during dentition,
