INTRODUCTION

Croton tig. is a very valuable remedy prepared
from croton oil seed, having a limited range of action in
therapeutic sphere. Mostly it is a remedy for the relief
of acute troubles. Nevertheless, no other drug in
Hemoeopathic Materia Medica is able to replace it for
its peculiar affinity to mucous membrane of gastro-
intestinal tract and skin and thus the indispensability
of this remedy has been well established.

CLINICAL
Cholera, Colds, Cough, Diarrhoea, Breast affection,
Ear affection, Eczema, Skin affection, etc.
