Metrorrhagia : after miscarriage, confinement or at the climaxis. Profuse menses
after miscarriage; discharge of blood from slightest provocation.

Uterus hypertrophied; prolapsed.
Cervix sensitive, spongy, bleed easily.

Passive haemorrhage from retention of secundines after miscarriage. Blood is
darkish & coagulated. The uterus is enlarged & cervix tumified with constant bearing
down pain. Flow is intermittent. Agalactia. Ch.inflammations.

Skin

Alopceia. Complete loss of hair. Tendency to small boils. Painful, destructive disease
of nails. Skin dry & hot; congested. Pricking, burning, itching a marked erythema of the
skin of the uncovered parts of the body, followed by a parchment like, dark brown skin
with rhagades worse < by warmth copper—coloured spots on skin.
