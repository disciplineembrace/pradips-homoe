BISMUTH

Hydrated Oxide HAHNEMANN Bi,O,0H,
of Bismuth

INTRODUCTION

Just like many other metals, Bismuth too has
been found to be an excellent remedy which
exerts its general influence in the stomach
region. It has been observed by Hahnemann that
the hydrated oxide of Bismuth could be easily
potentised to develop its curative power

CLINICAL

Abdominal, operation and Vomiting, Cholera,
Cough Delirium, Diarrhoea, Gangrene,
Headache, etc.

SPHERES OF ACTION

Bismuth predominantly acts upon the
gastrointestinal tract, mind and nervous system.

PATHOGENESIS

* Bismuth exerts its influence on the stomach
and stomach region. It causes a PURE
gastralgia, but not associated with catarrh, like that of Nux vom. There is ejection of
water immediately after it is taken, but food is retained a little longer.

CONSTITUTION
Bismuth is mostly an acute drug, as such consideration of constitution does not have
much bearing on its selection.

Miasm: Psora and Syphilis are in the background.

GUIDING SYMPTOMS
1. Bismuth patient CANNOT BEAR SOLITUDE THE ALWAYS WANTS AND
DESIRES COMPANY (Stram., Kali carb., Lil., Lyco.).

2. He has MUCH ANGUISH and, therefore, moves about frequently—he sits, walks,
lies, never long in one place.

3. Vomifing of water as soon as it reaches the stomach but solid food is retained for
days together.

4. There is post-operative vomiting, especially of liquid, particulary after laparotomy,
with GREAT ANGUISH OF MIND AND RESTLESSNESS.
