PETROLEUM

Coal oil or Rock oil HAHNEMANN Anthracite

INTRODUCTION

This product from the interior of the earth,
when, used as a remedial agent, “can help a
Homoeopathic physician to fight variety of
diseases”, so says the Master. This crude oil is
purified with the help of sulphuric acid before it
undergoes the process of potentisation. Kent says
that petroleum is one of the drugs abused by his
colleagues of the orthodox school of medicines
and when it is used in rheumatism and bruises
as an external application on the surface, this is
not its homoeopathic action.

CLINICAL

Anaemia, Angina pectoris, Abdominal disorders, Constipation, Diarrhoea, Eczema,
Haemorrhoids, Headache, Peptic ulcer, Psoriasis, Rheumatism, Skin affection, Vomiting,
etc.

SPHERES OF ACTION

It acts chiefly upon the skin and mucous membrane, especially of gastro-intestinal
tract. Underwood claims that it has action on heart, meninges and cerebrum too.

The skin bears the brunt of the action of the drug. It produces a number of skin
changes mainly of dry character as a result of irritation manifesting as CRACKS AND
FISSURES. On brain and heart when it acts, it produces tonic and clonic convulsion with
oppression, giddiness, palpitation of heart and faintness.

CONSTITUTION

Physical make-up: Adapted to persons of light hair and light skin who suffer from
cutaneous eruptions and gastro-intestinal disorders.

Temperament: Irritable, quarrelsome, easily offended by trifles and vexed on every
thing.

Relation with heat and cold: Very chilly patient.

Miasm: Psora is definitely in the background.
