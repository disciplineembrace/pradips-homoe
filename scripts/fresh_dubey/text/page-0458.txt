Nitric Acid

General Modalities :

Aggravation: Evening and at night; after mid-night, contact; change of temperature or weather,
during sweat, on waking and while walking.

Amelioration: All symptoms are better while riding in a carriage.

Relations :
Complimentary to: Arsenic and Calad.
Inimical to: Lachesis.
Resembles: Arsenic in morbid fear of cholera.
Antidoted by: Calc., Hep., Merc., Mez:, Sul.
It antidotes: Calc., Dig., Merc.
