CALCAREA CARBONICA

(Calcarea Ostrearum)

Middle layer of HAHNEMANN Calcium
Oyster Shell Carbonate (Ca Cos)
INTRODUCTION

This great Hahnemannian antipsoric is
a wonderful constitutional remedy par
excellence. Dr. Mrs. Tyler remarks about

Calcarea that—

“With the chalky complexion goes —

Fatness without fitness,

Sweating without heat.

Bones without strength,

Tissues of plus quantity and minus

quality,
Mere flabby, bulky with weakness and
Weariness.”
“Some remedies are difficult to spot; the difficulty is to MISS Calcarea, when typical”

says Tyler. It is one of the greatest monuments of Hahnemann’s genius and is a great
polychrest remedy, and ranks with sulphur and Lycopodium at the head of anti-psorics.

CLINICAL

Abdominal disorders, Acidity, Anaemia, Bone affection, Breast affection, Cough,
Dentition, Diabetes, Diarrhoea, Dropsy, Dyspepsia, Eye troubles, Epilepsy, Fever,
Glandular affection, Headache, Leucorrhoea, Lung affection, Menstrual disorders,
Miscarriage, Pregnancy disorders, Respiratory troubles, Rheumatism, Ricket, Skin
affection, Uterine affection, Vertigo, Worm troubles, etc.

SPHERES OF ACTION

The main seats of action of Calcarea carb. are bones, glands, blood, skin, mind and
female genital organs.

Calcarea carb. produces impairment in nutrition of various organs, particularly
BONES; alteration in composition of blood, with chief action on skin and bones,
manifesting inereased local or general perspiration, swelling of glands, softening and
bending bones, abscesses in deep muscles, polypi and exostoses.
