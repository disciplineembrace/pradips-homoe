RANUNCULUS BULBOSUS

“Buttercup FRANZ Ranunculaceae

INTRODUCTION

Ranunculus bulbosus belongs to the natural
order Ranunculaceae. This was proved and added
to Materia Medica by Franz. The Mother tincture
is prepared from the whole plant “buttercup”.
The juice is highly excoriating and irritating to
skin where it gives rise to many skin eruptions.
Just like Rhus tox., it is poisonous and one is
very often mistaken for the other. It is an
excellent remedy for the bad effects of
alcoholism.

CLINICAL

Alcoholism, Brain affection, Chest pain, Corns, Delirium tremens, Diarrhoea,
Eczema, Intercostal rheumatism, Jaundice, Neuralgia, Skin troubles, Throat and Lung
affection, Writers’ cramp, etc.

SPHERES OF ACTION

It acts on mucous membrane and serous membrane, skin, spinal nerves, pleurae and
lungs as well as on intercostal muscles.

PATHOGENESIS

It causes irritation and smarting pain of eyes, nose and fauces. It also produces
inflammation of the serous membrane, especially pleura and peritoneum where it sets up
an acute stabbing pain accompanied by effusion and pleurisy. It irritates spinal nerves
and causes INTERCOSTAL RHEUMATISM: NEURALGIA AND MYALGIA OF CHEST.

CONSTITUTION

There is no particular constitution for this drug. The patient however, is very chilly,
irritable and quarrelsome, having psoric and gonorrhoea! background and have the
rheumatic diathesis,

GUIDING SYMPTOMS

1. It is one of the most effective remedies for the bad effects of alcoholic beverages,
spasmodic hiccough and delirium tremens.

2. Day blindness (Hemeralopia) as if there is mist before the eyes. Pressure and
smarting pain in eyeballs (Hering).
