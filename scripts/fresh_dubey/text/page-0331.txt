Fever :

In Pneumonia, Broncho-pneumonia or even influenza which tunrs towards typhoid, its pecular
mental symptoms and delirium are very valuable guides (see Mind).

During fever, the sensorium is clouded; with staring eyes; grasping at flocks, and picking up of bed
clothes. Subsultus tendinum and carphology are well marked.

Typhoid state may also prevail.

Tongue is dry and unwieldy; dry or brown; cracked, parched and white; looks like burnt leather.
Tongue paralysed; difficult speech. Tongue protruded wife difficulty, can hardly draw it in.

Teeth are covered with sordes. Breath is offensive, putrid and bad taste in the mouth.
The patient may also pass involuntary stool and urine.

General Modalities :

Aggravation: In the evening and at night, from mental affections, from jealousy, unhappy love, cold
air, from approach of menstruation, when lying down and from touch.

Amelioration : On stooping, from sitting (cough), motion, walking and warmth.
Relations : :

Follows Belladonna well in deafness after apoplexy. After Hyoscyamus, Phosphorous often cures
lasciviousness. Nux or Opium, in haemoptysis of drunkards.

Antidoted by: Vinegar, Belladonna, Camphor, Cinchona, Stram.

Hyoscyamus antidotes Belladonna, Plumbum, Stram.

Remedies following well: Bell., Puts., Stram., Ver. alb.
