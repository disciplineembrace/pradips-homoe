PARTICULARS
Respiratory Symptoms:

Spongia is one of the routine remedies for croupy cough in our practice. There is great dryness of
mucous membranes of air passages such as throat, larynx, trachea, bronchi, etc.

Throat:

There is dryness of mucous membranes of throat and stitching pain, There is also burning, stinging
and tickling which causes cough. Patient clears throat constantly.

Sore throat : Aggravation after eating sweets.

Cough :
Causation— Exposure : Exposure to dry cold wind, cold drinks, tobacco smoking, sweets.

Character of cough : Cough is dry, barking.gasping, ringing, wheezing, whistling. As there is great
dryness of all air passages, cough is dry, sibilant like a saw driven through a pine board and usually with
no mucous rales. Sometimes there may be asthmatic cough with wheezing, suffocation and profuse
expectoration.

Modalities :

Aggravation : Dry cold wind, cold drinks, sweets, smoking, lying with head low, mental
excitement.

Amelioration ; Eating or drinking warm things

Croup :
Croup is anxious, wheezing and worse during inspiration and before midnight.
(Aggravation during expiration—Aeon; Agg. before morning, Hep. Sulph)

Asthma :

Asthma from taking cold, after menses, in consequence of goltre with organic disease of heart.
Patient cannot lie down and fears she will die of suffocation. After every slight motion looses her breath
& becomes faint.

Modalities :
Agg : At full moon, after sleep.
Amelio :; Bending forward.
