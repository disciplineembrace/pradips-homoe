TARAXACUM

Dandelion Compositae

INTRODUCTION

This remedy deserves mention by
reasons of its curative value in affections of
the liver, such as gallstone, bilious attacks,
jaundice & typhoid fever.

IDING SYMPTOMS :
.

1) Mapped tongue (Lach, Merc, Nat.
M.) covered with a white film with
sensation of rawness. This film comes
off in patches, leaving dark red,
tender, very sensitive spots.

2) Jaundice with enlargement & induration of liver.

3) Debility and profuse night sweats, esp. when convalesc-ing from bilious or typhoid
fever.

4) Loss of appetite.

5) Gastric headaches with sensation of great-heat on top of head & mapped tongue.
There may be jaundiced skin.

6) Limbs are very restless & painful to touch, in typhoid fever (Rhus., Zin.)
7) Fever Chilli—Chilliness after eating & aggravated by drinking.

Heat : Heat stage without thirst. Heat in face & in toes. During fever finger tips cold
& bitter taste in mouth.

Sweat—Perspiration on falling asleep.
General Modalities :

Aggravation ; Almost all symptoms appear when sitting ; lying down ; resting.
Relations :

Compare : Bry, chel., Hydr., Nux in gastric and bilious affections.
