Apis Mellifica 71

Relations :
Complementary to Natrum mur.
Inimical to Rhus tox., in eruptive diseases.
Follows well: Arsenic and Pulsatilla.
Antidote: Cantharides, China, lodum, Digitalis.

Apis has low action and must not be changed too soon. Increased flow of urine shows that it is
producing a favourable effect.
