MEZEREUM

“Daphne Mezereum E, STAPF Thymelaceae

INTRODUCTION

It was introduced in the Materia medica by Dr.
Stapf, but extensively proved by Hahnemann. “The
medicine still remains as one of Hahnemann’s precious
medical legacies”, remarks Dr. Tyler in her drug picture,
Hughes tells us that mezereum was one of the vegetable
substances with which it was attempted to replace
mercury in the tretment of syphilis, This fact has been
supported by provings. Hence, the drug is also called
VEGETABLE MERCURY.

CLINICAL

Bone affection, Ear troubles, Exostosis,
Enlargement of glands, Gonorrhoea, Herpes zoster,
Impetigo, Irritation, Leucorrhoea, Neuralgia, Rheumatism, Syphilis, Teeth affection,
Tongue affection, Skin troubles, Ulcers, Vaccination-bad effects of, etc.

SPHERES OF ACTION

It acts on skin, bones, teeth, nerve and mind.

PATHOGENESIS

On the skin it produces variety of destructive changes, right from a simple non-
eruptive itch to malignant ulcers, lesions with or without oozing and black discharges dry
up and become thick leather-like scab with a soft pus-like material intervening between
it and skin surface. On bones, especially the periosteum, it causes inflammations and
bone pains. Nerves, underthe action of this drug produce neuralgia predominantly about
teeth and face.

CONSTITUTION
Physical make-up: It is especially suited to light haired, irresolute persons.

Temperament: Phlegmatic temperament.
Relation with heat and cold: Chilly patient.
Miasm: Psora and syphilis are in the background.

GUIDING SYMPTOMS

1. Bad effects of vaccination (Antim. tart., Silicea, Thuja, Crotalus hor.) especially skin
eruptions.
