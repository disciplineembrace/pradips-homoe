LAPOILUM

Cayenne or HAHNEMANN Solanaceae
Red Pepper
INTRODUCTION

Master Hahnemann says—this Spanish
pepper as it is called, was introduced as a spice to
season the sauces and to work as an appetiser.
But in the rational therapeutic sphere, it has
gained enough deserving name and fame by its
characteristic, though limited sphere of action.
Dr. Tyler remarks in regard to this remedy
“these remedies of limited, but curious and
unique action, are most useful where they fit, one
may need them seldom, But yet nothing on earth
will take their place where symptoms demand
them”.

CLINICAL

Asthma, Abdominal disorders, Brain affection,
Cough, Delirium, Diarrhoea, Dysentery, Ear
affection, Headache, Haemorrhoids, Home
sickness, Fevers, Lung affection, Pregnancy
troubles, Rheumatism, Sciatica, Throat affection, Urinary troubles, Whooping cough
Tonsillitis, etc.

SPHERES OF ACTION

Capsicum affects principally mucous membranes e.g. of mouth tongue, abdomen.
bowels, rectum, etc. Chest, lungs and skin may also be affected in rare instances. It als
affects the bones, particularly bones of the ear.

PATHOGENESIS

It produces inflammation of mucous membrane with mucus discharge from bowel
throat, etc. It also causes well marked chill, fever and neuralgia with threatening dee?
seated abscesses.

CONSTITUTION

Physical make-up: Especially suited to fatty persons with light hair, blue eyes, stov'
and plethoric who easily get exhausted, indolent, dread of any kind of exercise, person
inclined to be jovial, yet angry at trifles, Children dread open air. A Capsicum child #
fatty, dirty, clumsy, and is always chilly.

Temperament: Nervous temperament.
