CAUSTICUM

“Tinctura Acris HAHNEMANN Potassium
Sine Kali Hydrate
INTRODUCTION

This drug is one of Hahnemann’s flashes of
genius; and outcome of the labours of
Hahnemann, the Chemist, Physician and the
true Healer of rational art. It is a unique remedy
and is one of those “without which we cannot do
our practice”. Hering’s Guiding Symptoms say
“Whatever diversity of opinion may exist
theoretically, Causticum remains stamped by our
best practitioners as a polychrest of the highest
order”. Nash too says “If Hahnemann had never
given to the Homoeopathic school any remedy but
Causticum the world would still owe him
everlasting obligation”. Causticum is an indispensable polychrest remedy.

CLINICAL

Bladder affections, Bronchitis, Burns, Chorea, Constipation, Convulsion, Cough, Ear
affection, Epilepsy, Eye affection, Facial Paralysis, Fistula, Haemorrhoids, Headache,
Heart affection, Fevers, Laryngitis, Leucorrhoea, Locomotor ataxia, Menstrual disorders,
Nervous affection, Paralysis, Pregnancy disorders, Neuralgia, Rheumatism, Throat
affection, Ulcers, Urinary disorders, Warts, Whooping cough, Abdominal affections,

Respiratory affections, etc.

SPHERES OF ACTION

The principal organs affected by this great drug, are medulla oblongata and vagus
nerve; larynx, trachea : urinary bladder and mucous membrane; FLEXOR TENDONS

and skin. It is a right sided remedy.

PATHOGENESIS

The general physiological action of this drug is paralysis, slow pulse, low
temperature and general paralytic, weakness. PARALYSIS, especially of vocal cord,
Paralysis of the urinary bladder leading to retention of urine or inconigence of urine.
Contraction of muscles and tendons is a striking characteristic phenomenon of
Causticum. On mucous membranes of different hollow organs, Casusticum produces an
Irritant action with increased secretions.
