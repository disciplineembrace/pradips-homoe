Brain disease from suppressed eruption is also wonderfully cured by this remedy.

The troubles are aggravated from slightest touch, noise, jar and motion and tobacco smoke, and
relieved by rest and in a dark room.

Skin :
Cicuta virosa is a marvellous remedy for skin affections of a peculiar character as follows:-

Eczema with NO ITCHING. There is exudation from the eczema which forms into a hard, lemon-
coloured crust.

The pustules run together and form a thick yellow scab, especially on head and face and other
parts of the body, sycosis menti (Mandibular region).

For Barber's itch or troubles after shaving, Cicuta virosa is an excellent remedy.

Patients who suffer from violent shock through head, stomach, arms, legs, and jerking of the parts,
with head hot may also have to take the help of this remedy for relief.

Furthermore, for injurious chronic effect from concussion of the brain and spine which results in
spasm, trismus and tetanus, from getting splinters into flesh, Cicuta virosa is the remedy generally
indicated (Hyper.).

General Modalities :
Aggravation: From tobacco smoke (Ign.) touch, odour, jar, motion and noise.
Amelioration: By rest and in a dark room.

Relation :
Compare: Hydr. ac., Hyper., Nux vom. and Strych.
It is antidoted by: Arnica and Opium.
