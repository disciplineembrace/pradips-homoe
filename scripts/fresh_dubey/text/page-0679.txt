CLINICAL USE

It is useful in following diseases when the above c aracteristic symptoms are present:

* Any long protractive suppurative diseases or conditions anywhere in the body:
ulcers, boils, carbuncles, felons, pimples and pustules etc.

e In all ailments where pus-formations are liable to develop or have already
developed.

* Neglected wounds, cuts, etc., when discharging pus, and when they do not heal
readily.

* Burns and scalds (after Kali mur. ) , when suppurating.

° Pimples on the faces of young people at the age of puberty

* Inflammation of the eyes, ears

e Skin affections, with yellow scabs {Kali mur.).

¢ Crusta lactea (scald-head), with yellow crusts or secretions.

¢ Diseases of the mouth, if accompanied with purulent secretions.

¢ Ulceration at the roots of the teeth, with swelled gums and cheeks; to abort or
control the suppuration

® Chronic catarrh {Kali sulph., Silicea).

e All ailments of the throat in the third stage of the inflammation: sore throat, quinsy
and tonsilitis

* Last stages of consumption, last stage of croup, pneumonia or bronchitis. Generally
indicated after Kali mur. Pus forming in cavity of lung or pleura

¢ Advanced stages of lung diseases

¢ Leucorrhcea

* Gonorrhoea

¢ Malignant growths after ulceration has set in. Apply locally to the parts on lint.
