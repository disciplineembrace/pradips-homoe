SYMPHYTUM

Camfrey Borraginaceae

INTRODUCTION

Symphytum is a great vulnerary remedy,
same as Arnica and Ruta. It is also called
‘Camfrey’ and it belongs to the natural order of
Borraginaceae. It may be con-sidered the
orthopaedic specific of herbal medicine.
